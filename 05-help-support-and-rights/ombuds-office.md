# Ombuds Office

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/ombuds-office
- **Covered California section:** Help, Support and Rights
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page explains the Covered California Ombuds Office, the independent problem-resolution body that handles Covered California enrollment and application disputes that have already gone through the service center escalation process. It is explicitly not the first step — consumers must call the Covered California service center first — and it does not provide legal advice, which is instead handled free of charge by the Health Consumer Alliance. It also directs Medi-Cal-specific issues to the Department of Health Care Services, because Covered California cannot update the county systems that determine Medi-Cal eligibility.

## Key Facts

- **The Ombuds Office is not the first step.** "You must call the Covered California service center at [before] the Ombuds Office can help you."
- Published conditions that must be met before the Ombuds Office can help:
  - You have a health or dental insurance plan through Covered California.
  - You're experiencing an unresolved issue with your enrollment or application.
  - You contacted the Covered California service center and completed their escalation process.
  - You filed a Covered California complaint **more than 30 days ago** and haven't received an update.
- "The Ombuds Office can help you after you've spoken with a service center representative and your issue remains unresolved."
- **Required before submitting the Ombuds Contact Form:**
  - A 10-digit number associated with your account, starting with a 5 or 1.
  - The case number provided by the service center when they assisted with your case.
- "Response time depends on the complexity of your case."
- **The Ombuds Office does not provide legal advice.**
- The independent contractor is the **Health Consumer Alliance (HCA)**, which "offers free legal advice and representation, including help filing a complaint or grievance and arranging a hearing."
- "The HCA is a network of 10 consumer-assistance programs operated by community-based legal services."
- "HCA provides free legal help in any language."
- HCA hours published: **Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.**
- Covered California service center phone published elsewhere on the site: **(800) 300-1506**

## Details

### When to Contact the Ombuds Office

The page is structured as a gated escalation path, and the gate is strict. All four conditions above must be true. The 30-day complaint rule is the key timing constraint: a consumer must have filed a Covered California complaint more than 30 days ago and still not have received an update. Simple inquiries or quick guidance are routed to chat with a member of the Ombuds Office instead.

### The Contact Form

Consumers complete and submit the Ombuds Contact Form online. Before filling it out, have ready:

1. **Account number** — a 10-digit number associated with your account, starting with a 5 or 1.
2. **Case number** — provided by the service center when they assisted with the case.

Once submitted, the Ombuds Office reviews the case and works toward a resolution or explains why one could not be reached. A representative follows up using the contact method listed on the form.

### Legal Help: The Health Consumer Alliance

The page draws a hard line between the two functions:

- **Ombuds Office** — problem resolution for Covered California enrollment and application disputes. No legal advice.
- **Health Consumer Alliance (HCA)** — free legal advice and representation, including help filing a complaint or grievance and arranging a hearing. HCA is a network of **10** consumer-assistance programs operated by community-based legal services, and provides free legal help in any language, Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.

### Medi-Cal Issues Are Handled by DHCS, Not Covered California

"Covered California can't update the county systems that determine Medi-Cal eligibility." Two resources are offered depending on situation, one being the California Department of Health Care Services (DHCS) Medi-Cal Helpline. DHCS can help with any Medi-Cal concern, including:

- Expedited plan enrollment, plan changes and plan disenrollment.
- Ordering a Benefits Identification Card (BIC), if the local county office is unable to help.
- Updating county codes (when you move to another county).
- Referrals to appropriate agencies for concerns outside of their scope.
