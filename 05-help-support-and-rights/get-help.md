# Get Help

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/get-help
- **Covered California section:** Get Help
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This is the central help hub for Covered California. It states that help is always free and confidential, and routes consumers to six contact channels — Service Center phone, live chat, certified enroller finder, Help On Demand callback, Medi-Cal county offices, and community events. It publishes the Service Center's hours of operation and a complete phone number table covering fourteen languages plus TTY.

## Key Facts

- **Help is always free.**
- Free, confidential help is available **by phone, online, and in person** across California.
- Service Center English line: **(800) 300-1506**.
- **Service Center hours: Monday to Friday, 8 A.M. to 6 P.M.**
- **Service Center hours: Saturday and Sunday — Closed.**
- **TTY: (888) 889-4500.**
- Phone support is available in **14 languages** in addition to English.
- Help On Demand offers a **free callback from a certified enroller within 15 minutes**, for **first-time applicants only**.
- You can also find a certified enroller who **speaks your language**.

## Details

### Help Is Always Free

Free help is available whether you are applying for the first time or managing your coverage. Free, **confidential** help is available **by phone, online, and in person** across California.

### Contact Channels

| Channel | What it is | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Call Us | Speak with a Covered California representative | **(800) 300-1506**, available in multiple languages |
| Live Chat | Chat online with a Covered California representative | Available via the Live Chat page |
| Find Free Local Help | In-person help from a certified enroller or agent near you | Available in multiple languages |
| Help On Demand | Free callback from a certified enroller **within 15 minutes** | **First-time applicants only** |
| Apply for Medi-Cal | Apply for Medi-Cal and other benefits | With help from your **local county office** |
| Events | Enrollment events, workshops and health fairs | Happening in your community |

### Common Questions and Topics

Not sure how something works? The page groups self-service guides by topic:

- **Learn how to create or access your account**, reset your password and manage your personal information.
- **Understand your first payment, monthly bills**, and what to do if you don't receive one.
- **Find phone numbers, websites, and billing contacts** for your health, dental or vision plan.
- **Find all document types** Covered California may accept to confirm eligibility.
- **Report a life change** that may affect your insurance or financial help.
- **Learn how renewal works** and what to do before open enrollment to keep or switch your plan.

### Get Help in Your Language

Phone support and certified enrollers are available in many languages. You can also find a certified enroller who speaks your language, using the Find an Enroller tool. The full language line list is reproduced in the Key Facts table above; the TTY line for deaf and hard-of-hearing callers is **(888) 889-4500**.
