# Insurance Company Contacts

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/insurance-company-contacts
- **Covered California section:** Insurance Company Contacts
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page lists contact details for every health, dental and vision insurance company participating in Covered California: member services lines, prescription lines, billing and payment lines, mailing addresses for payments, and a dedicated "no invoice received" number for each company. It also carries a notice that Molina Healthcare will not offer plans in northeast Los Angeles County or Orange County in 2027, so affected consumers should use Shop and Compare.

## Key Facts

- Covered California publishes **12 health insurance companies**, **5 dental insurance companies** and **2 vision insurance companies**.
- Each company entry includes a website, a member services number, a billing or payment number and a **mailing address for payments**.
- Each company entry includes a separate **"No invoice received?"** number.
- **Molina Healthcare will not offer plans in northeast Los Angeles County or Orange County in 2027.**
- Covered California advises contacting your insurance company directly about your **bill, membership ID card, benefits or prescription drugs**.
- Plan-specific disputes (premiums, billing, claims, provider networks, covered services, benefits, ID cards) are **not** handled by Covered California — see the File an Appeal or a Complaint page for how to route those.
- Several companies offer **TTY: 711** relay access; Balance by CCHP lists **TTY (877) 681-8898**.
- Covered California itself does **not** handle billing or payments; refunds go to the health or dental company.

### First Premium Payment

Most insurers offer a "Pay your first premium online" link on this page. **Valley Health Plan** and **Western Health Advantage** instead direct first-time payers to **sign in to their CoveredCA.com account** and follow the payment instructions.

### Complaint Route — How to Escalate

This page itself directs you to **contact your insurance company directly** with questions about your bill, membership ID card, benefits or prescription drugs, and states that **Covered California does not handle billing or payments**.

The escalation path published elsewhere on the site is:

1. **File a grievance with your insurance company.**
2. If you are not satisfied with the resolution, or you have been in their grievance system for **30 days**, file a complaint with the **Department of Managed Health Care** — DMHC Help Center **(888) 466-2219**.
3. Alternatively, file a complaint with the **California Department of Insurance** — **(800) 927-4357** — for concerns about health insurance plans or companies, agent licensing, Independent Medical Review (IMR), or insurance fraud.
