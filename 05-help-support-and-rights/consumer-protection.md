# Consumer Protection

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/consumer-protection

## Summary

This page explains how to avoid and report fraud against Covered California enrollees. It sets out the rules that identify a scam — never pay for enrollment help, never pay cash, and only deal with certified enrollers — then gives the fraud reporting contacts for Covered California, the FTC, the California Department of Insurance, Medi-Cal (DHCS) and Medicare. It also cites the statutory penalty for improper disclosure of consumer information.

## Key Facts

- **Never pay for help** filling out the Covered California health insurance application. Enrollment help is available but **never required** and **always free**.
- **Pay monthly premiums only to your health insurance company or a licensed insurance agent. Never pay with cash.**
- Only share personal information when completing the Covered California application **online or on paper**.
- Improper disclosure of consumers' confidential information is a violation of federal and state law, subject to **fines of up to $25,000 under the Affordable Care Act**.

## Details

### Before You Enroll

- Before sharing any personal information, make sure the person is **certified by Covered California**.
- Use the certified enroller or agent lookup — **only certified enrollers and agents are listed**.
- If you are unsure, call **(800) 300-1506**.
- If you accept enrollment help from anyone, keep a record of their **name, certification number, phone number, address and organization's website**.
- **Never pay for help** filling out the Covered California health insurance application. Enrollment help is available, but **never required, and always free**.

### Protect Your Personal Information

- You only need to provide personal information, like a Social Security number, when completing the Covered California enrollment application **online or on paper**.
- **Pay monthly premiums only to your health insurance company or to a licensed insurance agent. Never pay with cash.**
- If you do not have access to a bank, contact your **health insurance company** about secure payment options.
- Disclosure of consumers' confidential information is a **violation of federal and state law**. Improper disclosure is subject to **fines of up to $25,000 under the Affordable Care Act**.

### How to Spot Fraud

Red flags published on the page:

- A website asks you to enter personal information to enroll, but it **isn't the official Covered California site**.
- A website asks you to enter confidential information to make a payment but **isn't an official site**.
- A representative **charges a fee** to help you enrol.
- A representative asks to be **paid in cash**.
- Representatives request your personal information **over the phone or door-to-door without a clear reason**.
- A salesperson says you **will go to jail if you don't buy insurance**, or they offer a **one-time-only special discount**.
- A caller requests your **Social Security number and personal information to continue Medicare eligibility**.

### Report Fraud

Covered California offers free enrolment help through its service center, certified enrollment counselors, licensed insurance agents and county eligibility workers. If something feels wrong, report it.

If you think you are a victim of identity theft, notify the **California Office of the Attorney General** (Identity Theft Checklist) and the **Federal Trade Commission** (Identity Theft Resources), or call **(877) ID-THEFT**.
