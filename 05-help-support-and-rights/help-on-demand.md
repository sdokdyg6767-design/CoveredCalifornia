# Help on Demand

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/get-help/help-on-demand
- **Covered California section:** Get Help
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

Help On Demand is Covered California's callback request form for people who cannot use the Service Center phone line. You submit your contact information and a certified enroller calls you back. Critically, the service is limited to **new enrollment only** — anyone who already has a Covered California case is directed to their existing enroller or to the Service Center phone line instead.

## Key Facts

- **Help On Demand is for new enrollment only.**
- If you already have a Covered California case, contact your certified enroller if you have one, or call the Service Center at **(800) 300-1506**.
- A **free callback** is offered; the Get Help page advertises a callback from a certified enroller **within 15 minutes** for **first-time applicants only**.
- The form requires contact information, with **required fields marked with an asterisk (*)**.

## Details

### The Fine Print

The page publishes its restriction explicitly:

> Help On Demand is for new enrollment only.
>
> If you already have a Covered California case, please contact your certified enroller if you have one, or call our service center at (800) 300-1506 for assistance.

### The Request Form

The page presents a contact-information form for requesting a callback.

- **Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*).**
- Submitting the form triggers a free callback from a certified enroller.

### Callback Timing

The Get Help page summarises this service as: "For first-time applicants only. Get a free callback from a certified enroller within 15 minutes."

### Who Should Use This Instead

| Your situation | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| You are a first-time applicant and cannot wait on the phone | Submit the **Help On Demand** callback form on this page |
| You already have a Covered California case | Contact your **certified enroller**, or call **(800) 300-1506** |
| You are already enrolled and need help managing coverage | Call **(800) 300-1506**, Monday to Friday 8 A.M. to 6 P.M. |
| You are deaf or hard of hearing | TTY **(888) 889-4500** |
