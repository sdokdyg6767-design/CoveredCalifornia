# Events

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/get-help/events
- **Covered California section:** Get Help
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page lists in-person Covered California enrollment and renewal events at libraries, community centres and kiosks around the state. Events offer walk-in and scheduled appointments, and many are hosted with representatives from Covered California-contracted health plans on site. The listed events are tied to the **2027 plan year** and to the open enrollment period **11/01/2026 to 01/31/2027**.

## Key Facts

- Events provide **walk-in and scheduled appointments**.
- **Open enrollment period: 11/01/2026 to 01/31/2027.**
- A stated event office-hours pattern is **12 pm to 6 pm**, with **other times by appointment**, including **weekends during the open enrollment period**.
- Events are aimed at the **2027 plan year** — both **enrollment** and **renewal** events are listed.
- Many events include representatives from **Covered California-contracted health plans** who provide information and support.
- Venues include libraries and mall kiosks, and events are labelled **Indoor Event** or **Outdoor Patio**.

## Details

### What These Events Are

Community events let you learn more about Covered California and enrol in coverage near you. Two event types appear in the listing:

| Event type | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Enrollment events | Help potential Covered California consumers explore health coverage options and complete enrollment applications for the **2027 plan year** |
| Renewal events | Help existing Covered California consumers renew their health coverage for the **2027 plan year** |

The renewal events note: "We will offer walk-in and scheduled appointments to assist Covered California consumers with renewing their health coverage for the 2027 plan year. The renewal event will also include representatives from Covered California-contracted health plans who will be available to provide information and support to consumers."

The enrollment events note: "We will offer walk-in and scheduled appointments to assist potential Covered California consumers with exploring their health coverage options and completing enrollment applications for the 2027 plan year. The enrollment event will also include representatives from Covered California-contracted health plans who will be available to provide information, answer questions, and support consumers in making informed health coverage decisions."

### Event Hours

A published hours pattern on the listed events reads:

> Office hours are from 12 pm to 6 pm; other times are by appointment, including weekends during open enrollment period (11/01/2026 -> 01/31/2027).

The corresponding enrollment-period variant reads:

> Office hours are from 12 pm to 6 pm; other times are by appointment, including weekends for open enrollment period.

### Alternatives to Attending an Event

If you cannot attend in person, the same free help is available by phone at **(800) 300-1506** (TTY **(888) 889-4500**), by live chat, or from a certified enroller found through the Find an Enroller tool. The Service Center operates **Monday to Friday 8 A.M. to 6 P.M.**, closed weekends and on announced holidays.
