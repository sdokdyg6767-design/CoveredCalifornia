# Privacy Policy

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/privacy
- **Covered California section:** About / Privacy
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

Covered California publishes its privacy rights and the five core commitments it makes about personal information, plus a downloadable library of official forms for exercising each right. It also describes how to opt out of the HealthCare Evidence Initiative and how to report a deceased enrollee.

## Key Facts

- **Service Center:** **(800) 300-1506** (for inspecting and correcting records)
- **Privacy Office phone:** **(800) 889-3871** — leave a message with your name and phone number
- **Privacy Office email:** **privacyofficer@covered.ca.gov**
- **Privacy Office mail:** Privacy Officer, 1601 Exposition Blvd., Sacramento, CA 95815
- **Amendment turnaround:** Personal information **will be updated within 30 days of receipt**. If the request cannot be granted, you are **notified within 30 days** along with contact information to request review of the decision.
- **Anti-retaliation:** "You will never be retaliated against for filing a privacy-related complaint, and your decision to file a privacy-related complaint will never affect your eligibility for health insurance or Covered California services."
- **HealthCare Evidence Initiative opt-out:** takes effect **in the month after** the request is received; opting out does **not** affect insurance or access to services.
- **Governing statutes referenced elsewhere on the site:** California Government Code section **11015.5** (see [[about-and-resources/privacy-terms]]).

## Details

### How We Treat Your Information

Covered California commits to:

1. Never collect personal information from you without your knowledge or consent.
2. Never knowingly disclose your personal information to a third party, except as provided in the privacy policy.
3. Allow you to inspect and correct your personal information at any time.
4. Take reasonable measures to protect the security of your personal information.
5. Notify you if they suspect that your personal information may have been compromised.

Full documents are downloadable: **Covered California Privacy Policy (PDF)** and **Covered California Terms of Use (PDF)**.

For non-privacy complaints, the site directs users to the separate **Covered California Complaint Form**.

### Reporting a deceased enrollee

Three forms are offered depending on the situation:

- Courtesy Notification of Deceased
- Notification of Deceased by an Enrolled Member
- Notification of Deceased by an Estate Representative

(Related: the application-stage document list includes a `notification-of-a-deceased-member` verification item.)

### Healthcare Evidence Initiative

Enrollees can request that their household information **not** be used in Covered California's **Healthcare Evidence Initiative**, a program that uses member data to improve patient care. The page is explicit that opting out "will not affect your insurance or ability to receive services through Covered California." Requests take effect **in the month after** receipt.
