# Social Media Commenting Disclaimer

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/social-media-commenting-disclaimer
- **Covered California section:** About / Social Media
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

Covered California's moderation policy for comments on its official social media channels. It enumerates the categories of comment that may be removed or limited, and makes an important practical point: social media comments are **not** part of the formal appeal, complaint, or grievance process.

## Key Facts

- **Scope:** Applies to Covered California's **official social media channels**.
- **Enforcement actions available:** hide or remove comments, block or ban users, or take other appropriate action.
- **Critical limitation:** "Comments posted on social media are informal and are not part of Covered California's formal appeal, complaint, or grievance process."
- **Medical disclaimer:** "Nothing on Covered California's social media pages should be considered medical advice."
- **Emergency guidance:** If experiencing a medical emergency, **call 911** or contact your health care provider.
- **Platform violations** should be reported directly to the host platform, in addition to Covered California's policy.

## Details

### About This Policy

Covered California welcomes comments and conversation about its programs and services. The policy explains how comments are moderated and when participation may be removed or limited. The stated goal is to keep the social media community "respectful and safe" for people "of all ages and backgrounds."

### Comments We May Remove or Limit

| # | Category |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Off topic or unrelated to the purpose of the page, post or thread. |
| 2 | Repetitive, excessively long, or spam-like. |
| 3 | Obscene, pornographic, vulgar or profane. |
| 4 | Threatening, including true threats, fighting words, or comments that threaten physical harm. |
| 5 | Defamatory or fraudulent. |
| 6 | Encouraging, promoting, or directly tied to illegal activity. |
| 7 | Promoting or endorsing a business, product, service, agent or commercial transaction. |
| 8 | Promoting political issues, ballot measures, groups, candidates or individuals. |
| 9 | Sharing content that could compromise public safety, security or another person's personally identifiable information. |
| 10 | Threatening the security of government information technology. |
| 11 | Otherwise in violation of law or regulation. |

Note that categories 7 and 8 are unusually broad: they bar promotion of commercial offerings (including agents) and any political content, not merely spam.

### Important Notes

- The policy is **in addition to** any terms, conditions or user policies set by the social media platform that hosts the content.
- If a comment violates the host platform's policy or terms, report it directly to that platform.
- Social media comments are informal and are **not** part of the formal appeal, complaint, or grievance process. For formal help, use the official appeal, complaint, or grievance process.
- Nothing on social media pages should be considered medical advice. In an emergency, call 911 or contact your health care provider.
