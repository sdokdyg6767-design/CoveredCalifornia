## Summary

Covered California is the state's official health insurance marketplace, established under the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act in 2014. It is the only place in California where qualifying residents can get federal financial help while buying private health insurance. Critically, Covered California is not itself an insurance company — it connects residents to brand-name plans and helps them qualify for subsidies.

## Key Facts

- **Established:** 2014, under the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act (ACA).
- **Role:** Marketplace, not insurer. Covered California states plainly: "We are not an insurance company. We don't sell insurance directly or pay claims."
- **Sponsors of CoveredCA.com:** Covered California **and** the Department of Health Care Services (DHCS), which work together to connect Californians with coverage and care.
- **Governance:** Covered California is "an independent part of state government, overseen by a five-member board appointed by the governor and the Legislature."
- **Exclusive function:** The only place in California where qualifying residents can obtain federal financial help while purchasing private health insurance.

## Details

### What is Covered California?

Covered California is California's health insurance marketplace, established under the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act in 2014. It is the only place in California where qualifying residents can get federal **financial help** while buying private health insurance.

> Site tooltip definition of "financial help": "Money from the government that may lower what you pay each month for health insurance based on your income."

Covered California does not sell insurance directly and does not pay claims. Instead it connects Californians with brand-name health insurance plans from well-known companies and helps them obtain financial help to lower costs.

### Governance and sponsorship

CoveredCA.com is sponsored by Covered California and the Department of Health Care Services, which work together to help Californians get the coverage and care that is right for them. Covered California is an independent part of state government, overseen by a five-member board appointed by the governor and the Legislature.

### Our Mission

The stated mission is to "make health insurance accessible and affordable for all Californians — regardless of income, health history or background." The page lists three mechanisms:

- Connecting people with brand-name health insurance plans.
- Helping them understand and access financial help.
- Making sure everyone who applies is connected to the insurance they qualify for, whether that's a Covered California plan or Medi-Cal.

Note the third bullet: the marketplace's role includes screening people toward **Medi-Cal** when they qualify, not just private plans.
