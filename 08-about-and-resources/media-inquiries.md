# Media Inquiries

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/media-inquiries
- **Covered California section:** About / Media & Public Records
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

Journalists seeking information, joining the email list, or requesting public records under the California Public Records Act all route through Covered California's Office of Communications and Public Relations at a single published number.

## Key Facts

- **Media and public records line:** **(916) 206-7777**
- **Use cases listed:** information requests, adding to Covered California's email list, and assistance requesting public records under the **California Public Records Act**.
- **Covered California is committed to transparency** "in its operations and policy implementation" — public records help is offered as part of that commitment.
- Page footer copyright reads "© 2026 Covered California."

## Details

### Office of Communications and Public Relations

The page explains the office's mandate: it is "charged with providing timely and relevant information that educates the public, the media, lawmakers and internal staff members on the policies, events and successes of Covered California."

The office's specific functions named on the page:

- Coordinates press conferences
- Develops and disseminates public information via press releases, advisories, and website and social media postings
- Facilitates media interviews

### California Public Records Act

Members of the media may contact the Office of Communications and Public Relations for assistance requesting public records by calling **(916) 206-7777**. This is offered explicitly in the context of Covered California's stated commitment to transparency in operations and policy implementation.
