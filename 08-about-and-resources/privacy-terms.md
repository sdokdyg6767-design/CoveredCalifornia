### Privacy Policy

The privacy policy describes how Covered California uses and discloses personal information, including sharing with contractors such as **Certified Insurance agents** and **Certified Enrollment Counselors**. By using the site, the user accepts the Privacy Policy. The Notice of Privacy Practices may change without notice; a copy can be requested from the Privacy Office.

### Notice Required Under Government Code Section 11015.5

Pursuant to Government Code section 11015.5, Covered California is required to create a Privacy Notice concerning electronically collected personal information on its website.

The statute defines "electronically collected personal information" as any information maintained by an agency that identifies or describes an individual user — **including but not limited to**:

- Name
- Social security number
- Physical description
- Home address
- Home telephone number
- Education
- Financial matters
- Medical or employment history
- Password
- Electronic mail address
- Information revealing any network location or identity

It **excludes**:

- Information manually submitted to a state agency by a user, whether electronically or in written form
- Information on or relating to individuals who are users **serving in a business capacity**, including business owners, officers, or principals of that business

### Information Volunteered by You

Covered California may ask for identifying information to provide services through its online application. Visitors may also participate in a voluntary survey or send an email. Collected information is not limited to text characters and may include **audio, video, and graphic information**. Types collected and purposes are described in the Notice of Privacy Practices.

The terms recommend providing only the personal information needed to respond to a concern, while acknowledging that insufficient information may prevent staff from helping. Emails may be forwarded to other state employees better able to help, possibly employed by a different agency within the State. Except for authorized law enforcement investigations or as required by law, emails are not shared with other organizations.
