## Summary

Covered California commits to equal access for non-English speakers and people with disabilities. The page lists free language-line phone numbers, TTY access, disability accommodations, WCAG 2.1 Level AA certification, a Section 1557 Civil Rights Coordinator, and the nondiscrimination commitments that apply across the site.

## Details

### Language Support

Covered California "strives to provide equal access to all the services and information within our organization to everyone, including those who are non-English speaking." Phone support and certified enrollers are available in many languages — call the main line at **(800) 300-1506** or use a language-specific number from the list below. Consumers can also use the **find a certified enroller** tool to locate an agent who speaks their language.

### Website Accessibility and Certification

Covered California's chief information officer, or designee, certifies **as of July 2026** that the website is designed, developed and maintained to be accessible. This denotes compliance with:

- California Government Code Sections **7405**, **11135**, and **11546.7**
- **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1** published by the Web Accessibility Initiative of the World Wide Web Consortium
- At a minimum **Level AA** success criteria

### Communication Support for People With Disabilities

Covered California provides "reasonable modifications and free aids and services to people with disabilities," including:

- Qualified sign language interpreters
- Auxiliary aids and services
- Written information in other formats (large print, audio, accessible electronic formats, and other formats)

To access these supports, call **TTY: (888) 889-4500** or use **Live Chat**. Representatives can help obtain written information in other formats, or consumers can request formats through their Covered California online account.

### Tools to Help You Use the Website

- **Make text larger (zoom):** PC/Windows `Ctrl` + `+`; Mac/Apple `Command` + `+`. Zoom out with `Ctrl`/`Command` + `-`.
- **Browser add-ons and extensions:** Firefox accessibility inspector; Chrome Web Store extensions for accessibility; Safari extensions.
- **Change CSS:** Cascading Style Sheets can be turned off or adjusted in the browser's Help or Settings menu to view pages with less design.
- **Keyboard shortcuts:** linked guides for Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, and Apple Safari.

### Nondiscrimination

Covered California complies with applicable state and federal civil rights laws and does not discriminate, exclude, or treat individuals differently on the basis of:

- Race
- Color
- National origin (including limited English proficiency and primary language)
- Age
- Disability
- Sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, sex characteristics including intersex traits, sex stereotypes
- Pregnancy and related conditions

A nondiscrimination policy in other languages is also published on the page.
