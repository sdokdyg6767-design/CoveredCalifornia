# Costs and Financial Help

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/costs
- **Covered California section:** Costs and Financial Help
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This is the top-level landing page for everything about what a consumer pays. It explains that there are two main kinds of health care cost — the monthly premium and out-of-pocket costs (deductibles, copays, coinsurance) — and lists the four factors that determine what an individual pays: which eligibility program they qualify for (Covered California or Medi-Cal), how much financial help they get, which coverage level they choose, and whether a rideshare or delivery company offers a health care stipend. It routes consumers to the Shop and Compare Tool for a personalized estimate.

## Key Facts

- Costs differ for every person and depend on a lot of different factors; many people qualify for financial help that lowers what they pay.
- Four published factors affect your costs:
  1. The eligibility program you qualify for — Covered California or Medi-Cal.
  2. How much financial help you might get — your household size and income, your age and where you live.
  3. The coverage level you choose — Bronze, Silver, Gold or Platinum.
  4. If your rideshare or delivery company offers a health care stipend.
- The coverage levels named are exactly four: Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum.
- The Shop and Compare Tool is described by Covered California as "the fastest way to see what you might pay," giving "a personalized estimate based on your situation."
- Plan-cost terms published on the page:
  - **Premium:** What you pay each month to keep your health plan, even if you do not use it.
  - **Deductible:** The amount you pay for covered care before your health plan starts to pay.
  - **Copay:** A set amount you pay for a covered visit or service.
  - **Coinsurance:** Your share of the cost for covered care after you meet your deductible.
  - **Out-of-pocket maximum:** The most you'll pay in a year for covered services. After that, your plan pays 100% of covered costs.
- The page publishes no dollar amounts, percentage-of-FPL figures, or income thresholds. All of that lives on the Financial Help page.

## Details

### What Affects Your Costs

Covered California lists the four drivers of a consumer's cost, in the order shown on the page:

| Factor | Detail given by Covered California |
| --- | --- |
| Eligibility program | Covered California or Medi-Cal |
| Financial help | Household size and income, age, and where you live |
| Coverage level | Bronze, Silver, Gold or Platinum |
| Employer/gig stipend | Whether your rideshare or delivery company offers a health care stipend |

The page notes that if you receive a health care stipend, that is a factor in what you may pay, and directs consumers on the related financial-help pages to report stipend income.

### Understanding Your Costs

The page draws the central distinction for consumers:

- **Monthly premium** — the amount you pay for having a health plan.
- **Out-of-pocket costs** — what you pay when you use care; can include deductibles, copays and coinsurance.

Note that the same page carries two slightly different wordings of the premium definition in different places ("The amount you pay each month for having a health plan" in the narrative, and "What you pay each month to keep your health plan, even if you do not use it" in the plan-cost terms table). Both are reproduced above.

### Out-of-Pocket Maximum

The most you'll pay in a year for covered services. After that, your plan pays 100% of covered costs. This is the consumer-protection ceiling on the out-of-pocket side of the cost equation.
