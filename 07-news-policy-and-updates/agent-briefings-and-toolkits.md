# Agent Briefings and Toolkits

- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13
- **Type:** News & policy brief

## Summary

This file summarizes the certified-enroller and enrollment-partner resources published at hbex.coveredca.com, the partner-facing site of the California Health Benefit Exchange. The Toolkit hub is organized around the enrollment calendar — separate toolkits for open enrollment, renewals, special enrollment periods and the Medi-Cal transition — plus role-specific and campaign materials. Several toolkits map directly onto the 2026-2027 federal changes, including a dedicated Lawfully Present Immigrant toolkit.

## Key Facts / Details

### The HBEX site

hbex.coveredca.com is the resource hub for partners and for information about Covered California's operations and governance. The home page states that Covered California has "helped more than 2.9 million people buy health insurance" since opening its doors in 2014, and directs consumers to CoveredCA.com. Source: https://hbex.coveredca.com/

Note: the HBEX home page counter module still displays legacy figures ("3.7m gained coverage since 2013," "17.2% 2013 uninsured rate," "7.2% 2017 uninsured rate," "3% remaining eligible uninsured rate"). These appear to be stale template values and should not be relied on.

### Lawfully Present Immigrant (LPI) resources

Given the Jan. 1, 2027 narrowing of premium tax credit eligibility, the LPI toolkit set is the most directly relevant partner resource in this library:

- **LPI Partner Fact Sheets**, available in: English, Spanish, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Russian, Ukrainian, Hindi, Punjabi
- **LPI Consumer Fact Sheets**, including an Affordable Healthcare Resource Guide
- Social Press Kit — Covered California Social Press Kit

### Medi-Cal transition materials

- Medi-Cal Transition — Medi-Cal to Covered California Enrollment Program Toolkit

This maps to the Covered California statements that as of January 1, 2026 adults without qualifying immigration status can no longer enroll in full Medi-Cal, and that DACA recipients became ineligible as of August 2025.
