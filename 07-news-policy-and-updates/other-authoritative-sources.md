# Other Authoritative Sources

- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13
- **Type:** Source guide

## Summary

coveredca.com is the right place to start and often the right place to finish, but it is not the authority on every question a consumer or researcher will ask. Medi-Cal eligibility, federal subsidy law, tax filing obligations, and the legal record on marketplace rulemaking all live elsewhere. This file lists the primary sources worth using alongside coveredca.com, what each one is authoritative for, and where they disagree with or go beyond what Covered California publishes.

## Key Facts / Details

### California Department of Health Care Services (DHCS) — Medi-Cal

- **URL:** https://www.dhcs.ca.gov/medi-cal/
- **Authoritative for:** Medi-Cal eligibility rules, income limits, enrollment, benefits, county office operations, and immigration-status rules for Medi-Cal.
- **Why it matters here:** Covered California itself states the limit of its own authority — "Covered California can't update the county systems that determine Medi-Cal eligibility" — and routes Medi-Cal questions to DHCS, including eligibility determinations, plan changes, Benefits Identification Cards (BIC), and county code updates when a consumer moves county. The Ombuds page directs consumers to the **DHCS Medi-Cal Helpline**.
- **Key Medi-Cal changes referenced by Covered California that are documented at DHCS:** the January 1, 2026 end of full Medi-Cal enrollment for adults without qualifying immigration status, and DACA recipients becoming ineligible as of August 2025.

### Covered California ombuds page — Medi-Cal escalation language

- **URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/ombuds-office
- **Authoritative for:** the boundary between what Covered California can resolve and what requires DHCS, and the free legal help channel (Health Consumer Alliance).

### HealthCare.gov — the federal marketplace

- **URL:** https://www.healthcare.gov/
- **Authoritative for:** the federal ACA marketplace, federal plan options outside Covered California, and the federal framing of premium tax credits. It is also where the federal open enrollment window and eligibility rules are described.
- **Critical distinction:** Covered California is a separate state-based marketplace. Federal open enrollment dates and rules do not apply to Covered California enrollees, and Covered California has consistently run its window from **Nov. 1 through Jan. 31**, rather than the federal platform window (CMS's own deployment materials describe plan year 2026 federal open enrollment as running Nov. 1, 2025 through **Jan. 15, 2026**).

### CMS — Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services

- **URLs:**
  - Marketplace program hub — https://www.cms.gov/marketplace/
  - 2025 Marketplace Integrity and Affordability final rule fact sheet — https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/2025-marketplace-integrity-and-affordability-final-rule
  - "Impacts of City of Columbus v. Kennedy," OE 2026 deployment deck (as of September 19, 2025) — https://www.cms.gov/files/document/columbus-kennedy-impacts.pdf
- **Authoritative for:** the federal rulemaking record — the text, effective dates, and operational implementation of marketplace rules, including the 90 Fed. Reg. 27074 integrity rule and CMS's response to the litigation.
- **Caveat:** the CMS Columbus deck is dated September 19, 2025 and therefore predates the June 12, 2026 vacatur. It describes a stay, not the final judgment.

### Internal Revenue Service

- **URLs:**
  - Individual credits and deductions index — https://www.irs.gov/credits-deductions/individuals
  - IRS updates: frequently asked questions on the premium tax credit — https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/irs-updates-frequently-asked-questions-on-the-premium-tax-credit
- **Authoritative for:** how premium tax credits are claimed and reconciled on a federal tax return, Form 8962, and the repayment consequences of excess subsidies.
- **Why it matters here:** this is the authority behind the "no repayment limit starting with tax year 2026" rule on Covered California's Financial Help page. The IRS, not Covered California, determines what a consumer owes at tax time.
- **Note:** the older IRS URLs for ACA tax credits returned 404 at retrieval; the two URLs above were verified live.

### California Franchise Tax Board

- **URL:** https://www.ftb.ca.gov/
- **Authoritative for:** California state income tax. Relevant because Covered California's state-funded premium subsidy requires an **agreement to file taxes** — the Financial Help page states the California Premium Subsidy is "Available for households that qualify based on income, agreement to file taxes and enrollment in a Covered California health plan."
- **Why it matters here:** a household that qualifies for state premium help must be a California tax filer, so FTB guidance on filing status and residency bears directly on eligibility for the state credit.

### California Health Benefit Exchange (HBEX) — partner and governance site

- **URL:** https://hbex.coveredca.com/
- **Authoritative for:** the operational and governance side of Covered California — enrollment assistance programs, the navigator program, outreach, plan management, SHOP, tribal consultation, state innovation waivers, and statutory reports (SLAA, audit, Ombuds annual reports).
- **Key sub-resources:** Toolkit — https://hbex.coveredca.com/toolkit/ ; Data & research — https://hbex.coveredca.com/data-research/ ; Regulations — https://hbex.coveredca.com/regulations/ ; Policy Insights — https://hbex.coveredca.com/stakeholders/covered-california-policy-insights/
- **Why it matters here:** contains the Lawfully Present Immigrant (LPI) partner and consumer toolkits, available in English, Spanish, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Russian, Ukrainian, Hindi and Punjabi — the most directly useful resource for the January 1, 2027 immigrant eligibility change.

### Covered California Board of Governors

- **URLs:**
  - Board home — https://board.coveredca.com/
  - Meetings, agendas, materials and webcasts — https://board.coveredca.com/meetings/
  - 2026 meeting calendar — https://board.coveredca.com/meetings/2026%20BOD%20Meeting%20Dates%20v2.pdf
- **Authoritative for:** the governing body's official record — meeting agendas (including Executive Director reports and federal policy landscape briefings), minutes, resolutions, action items, and public comment.
- **Why it matters here:** this is where policy positions and regulatory actions are visible *before* they reach coveredca.com's consumer pages. The September 17, 2026 agenda included "Proposed Emergency Eligibility and Enrollment Regulations" and "Overview of HR 1 Medicaid Requirements."

### Covered California agent and contact channels

- **URLs:**
  - Find an agent — https://www.coveredca.com/agents/
  - Contact — https://www.coveredca.com/find-help/contact/
  - Open enrollment — https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment/open-enrollment
  - Eligibility for immigrants — https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment/eligibility/immigrants
- **Authoritative for:** assisted enrollment and consumer support. Certified enrollers provide free, confidential, in-person help in multiple languages.

### Litigation and secondary analysis

- Georgetown Health Law and Policy Clinic litigation tracker — https://litigationtracker.law.georgetown.edu/litigation/city-of-columbus-et-al-v-kennedy-et-al-columbus-i/
  - Docket-style summaries of *City of Columbus v. Kennedy*, useful for tracking the continuing litigation including Columbus II.
- The Baldwin Group compliance analysis — https://baldwin.com/compliance/district-court-vacates-key-portions-of-aca-marketplace-integrity-rule/
  - Independent practitioner summary of the June 12, 2026 vacatur and the July 2026 Columbus II stay.
- Congressional Research Service, *Enhanced Premium Tax Credit Expiration: Frequently Asked Questions* (R48290) — https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/R/PDF/R48290/R48290.4.pdf
  - Nonpartisan congressional analysis of the enhanced premium tax credit sunset.
- American Medical Association, ACA marketplace eligibility and access for immigrants — https://www.ama-assn.org/system/files/aca-immigrant-impact-summary.pdf
  - Physician-association analysis of the H.R. 1 immigrant eligibility changes.

### Notable gap: there is no coveredca.com FAQ page

- **URL attempted:** https://www.coveredca.com/faq/
- **Result:** HTTP 404 at retrieval. Covered California does not publish a page at this address. Consumer questions are handled through the contact page (https://www.coveredca.com/find-help/contact/), the service center, and certified enrollers rather than a consolidated FAQ page.
