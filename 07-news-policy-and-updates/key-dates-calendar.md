# Key Dates Calendar

- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13
- **Type:** Timeline

## Summary

A consolidated, sourced timeline of every hard date relevant to Covered California consumers and researchers: the 2027 open enrollment window, the December 31 deadline for full-year coverage, renewal and special enrollment period rules, the effective dates of federal and state changes, and the 2026 Board of Directors meeting calendar. Each entry carries its source URL. Dates that could not be verified against a primary source are flagged as unverified rather than invented.

### Verification notes

- The Covered California open enrollment window (Nov. 1 – Jan. 31) was verified directly on Covered California pages and in the July 21, 2026 release. It is **not** the federal marketplace window; CMS's deck describes a federal OE for plan year 2026 ending January 15, 2026. California does not follow the federal shortened window.
- The 2026 state subsidy income cap is reported inconsistently across Covered California's own releases — 150% FPL on Oct. 30, 2025 and 165% FPL on Dec. 22, 2025 and Feb. 26, 2026, for the same $190 million appropriation. Both figures are recorded above with their source dates rather than reconciled.
- No Covered California page publishes a numeric 2027 calendar date for the Board meeting schedule; only 2026 dates are published at retrieval.
- The specific DHCS Medi-Cal Helpline and HCA phone numbers are rendered as links on coveredca.com and were not captured as verifiable text at retrieval, so they are omitted rather than guessed.
