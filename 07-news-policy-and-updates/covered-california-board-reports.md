# Covered California Board of Governors Reports

- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13
- **Type:** News & policy brief

## Summary

This file summarizes where the Covered California Board of Governors publishes its materials and what the most recent ones cover. The Board is the state's five-member governing body for the marketplace; its agendas, Executive Director reports, enrollment dashboards and policy items are posted on board.coveredca.com, with a full archive back to 2011. The most recent meeting agenda, September 17, 2026, shows the Board's current work centers: 2027 open enrollment readiness, federal policy monitoring, HR 1 Medicaid requirements, and emergency eligibility and enrollment regulations.

## Key Facts / Details

### The Board itself

- "The Exchange is an independent public entity within state government with a five-member board appointed by the Governor and the Legislature."
- Appointment structure: "Two members are appointed by the Governor; one by Senate Rules Committee; and one by the Speaker of the Assembly. The Secretary of the California Health and Human Services Agency or another designee serves as an ex-officio voting member of the Board. Appointed members serve four year terms."
- Members listed as of retrieval (Oct. 13, 2026):
  - **Kim Johnson, Chair**
  - **Mayra Alvarez, Vice Chair**
  - **Jerry Fleming**
  - **Craig Cornett**
  - **Deborah Kelch** (joined per the May 27, 2026 announcement, "Covered California Welcomes Deborah Reidy Kelch to its Board of Directors")
- Source: https://board.coveredca.com/
