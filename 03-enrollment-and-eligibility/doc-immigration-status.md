# Proof of Immigration Status or Lawful Presence

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment/documents/immigration-status
- **Covered California section:** Eligibility & Enrollment — Documents — Proof of Immigration Status
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

Covered California verifies immigration status and lawful presence electronically at application. If your information cannot be confirmed, you get a notice or account message asking for a document. This page organizes accepted proof into 25 immigration status categories — permanent, refugee and humanitarian statuses; non-immigrant and visa-based statuses; temporary and deferred statuses; and pending applications. You only need to submit one document from your category.

## Key Facts

- Immigration status is verified **electronically** when you apply; documents are requested only if confirmation fails.
- You only need to submit **one document** from the applicable category.
- The page covers **25 immigration status categories**.
- **DACA recipients are no longer eligible** to get health insurance through Covered California due to new federal rules.
- Trafficking certification is verified through HHS; Trafficking Verification Line: **(866)-401-5510**.
- Applicants in category 23 (pending statuses) **must also submit an Employment Authorization Document (I-766)**.
- Asylum/withholding applicants under category 23 must be **under age 14 with an application pending for at least 180 days**, or have employment authorization.

## Details

### Understanding your document request

Covered California verifies immigration status and lawful presence electronically when you apply. If your information cannot be confirmed, you will receive a notice or account message asking you to submit a document. Check your eligibility notice or account message — it tells you who needs to send proof and what type is needed. Find a document in the list below that matches your status and submit a copy. **You only need to submit one document.**

**Note:** People with **Deferred Action for Childhood Arrivals (DACA)** status are no longer eligible to get health insurance through Covered California due to new federal rules.

### Accepted documents by immigration status

#### Permanent, refugee and humanitarian statuses

**1. Lawful permanent residents (LPR; green card holders).** If your LPR application is still pending, see #22.

- Permanent Resident Card or "Green Card" (**I-551**)
- Temporary **I-551** stamp on form **I-94/I-94A** or on a foreign passport
- Machine Readable Immigrant Visa with temporary I-551 language
- Reentry Permit (**I-327**)
- Arrival/Departure Record (**I-94**) showing stamp for admission as LPR
- Arrival/Departure Record (**I-94**) in foreign passport
- Order issued by DHS, Board of Immigration Appeals, or Immigration Judge granting registry, suspension of deportation, cancellation of removal, or adjustment of status
- USCIS receipt showing that Form **I-90** (application for LPR replacement card) has been filed

**2. Asylees.** For pending asylum applicants, see #23.

- Arrival/Departure Record (**I-94**) with stamp showing grant of asylum, "Asylee" or "§208"
- Arrival/Departure Record (**I-94**) in foreign passport with the same stamp
- Order granting asylum by USCIS, DHS, immigration judge, Board of Immigration Appeals, or federal court
- Employment Authorization Document (Card) (**I-766**) annotated "A5"
- Refugee Travel Document (**I-571**)
- Permanent Resident Card, "Green Card" (**I-551**)

**3. Refugees.**

- Arrival/Departure Record (**I-94/I-94A**) stamped "Refugee" or "§207"
- Employment Authorization Document (Card) (**I-766**) annotated "A3"
- Permanent Resident Card, "Green Card" (**I-551**)
- Application for Employment Authorization (**I-765**), or USCIS receipt indicating filing

**4. Conditional entrants granted before 1980.** (Used for refugees prior to the Refugee Act of 1980. Most people now have LPR status, though some still have conditional entrant status.)

- Arrival/Departure Record (**I-94/I-94A**) indicating status as "conditional entrant," "refugee-conditional entrant," "Seventh Preference," "§203(a)(7)" or "P7"
- Permanent Resident Card or "Green Card" (**I-551**)

**5. Individuals granted withholding of deportation or removal.** Includes protection under immigration laws or the Convention Against Torture (CAT). If your withholding application is still pending, see #23.

- Document indicating withholding of removal (order from USCIS, DHS, immigration judge, BIA, or federal court)
- Employment Authorization Document (Card) (**I-766**) annotated "A10"
- Administrative order staying removal issued by DHS
- Arrival/Departure Record (**I-94/I-94A**) stamped "Withholding of Deportation," "§243(h)" or "§241(b)(3)"
- Refugee Travel Document (**I-571**)

**6. Individuals paroled into the U.S.**

- Arrival/Departure Record (**I-94/I-94A**) with stamp indicating "parole" or "PIP" or "212(d)(5)" or other parole notation
- Employment Authorization Document (Card) (**I-766**) annotated "A4" or "C11"
- Employment Authorization and Advance Parole Card (**I-512**) annotated with the reason parole was granted under Title 8 of the Code of Federal Regulations

**7. Cuban or Haitian entrants.**

- Arrival/Departure Record (**I-94/I-94A**) with stamp indicating "Cuban/Haitian Entrant" or "parole under §212(d)(5)", including "CU6," "CU7," or "CH6" — rare since the 1980s
- Employment Authorization Document (Card) (**I-766**) annotated "C8," or "C11"
- Temporary I-551 Stamp (on passport or I-94/I-94A)
- Receipt or notice showing filing or pending status of **I-589**

**8. Qualified domestic violence survivors.** Applies to the parent and/or child of a spouse or child who has been battered or subjected to extreme cruelty in the U.S. by a spouse, parent, or relative of the same household, and who has been approved or has a pending petition under the **Violence Against Women Act (VAWA)**.

- Application to qualify as an abused spouse, child, or parent under VAWA (**I-360**)
- Notice of Action (**I-797**) referencing a pending "I-360"
- Employment Authorization Document (Card) (**I-766**) annotated "A10," "C14," "C9," "C10" or "C31"
- Receipt or other proof of filing **I-485**
- Documents indicating a pending suspension of deportation or cancellation of removal case, including a court receipt for **EOIR-40** or **EOIR-42**
- Receipt or other proof of filing **I-130** visa petition under immediate relative (IR) or second family preference (P-2)

**9. Victims of trafficking and their spouse, child, sibling, or parent.** Includes individuals with a pending application for a victim of trafficking visa.

- Certification from Office of Refugee Resettlement (ORR)
- ORR eligibility letter if **under 18 years of age**
- Employment Authorization Document (Card) (**I-766**) annotated "A16," "C25"
- Notice of Action (**I-797**) — "Approval Notice" for "CP," "T-1" or "extension of T or U non-immigrant status," "T-2 spouse," "T-3 child," "T-4 parent," "T-5 unmarried sibling under 18"
- Employment Authorization and Advance Parole Card (**I-512**)
- T-Visa Application (**I-914**)
- Certification status verified through the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services. Trafficking Verification Line: **(866)-401-5510**

**10. Members of a federally recognized Indian tribe or American Indians born in Canada.**

- Certificate from Department of Interior or Department of Indigenous and Northern Affairs certifying Native American tribal ancestry, or other authoritative document
- Letter from the Canadian Department of Indian Affairs, birth or baptismal record issued on a Canadian Indian Reservation, or tribal or school records establishing that American Indians born in Canada are of at least one half American Indian ancestry

#### Non-immigrant and visa-based statuses

**11. Other non-immigrant statuses (letter visas).** Includes worker visas and student visas.

| Visa category | Accepted documents |
| --- | --- |
| **F-1, F-2, M1, M2** (academic and student non-immigrant) | Form **I-20**; Arrival/Departure Record (I-94/I-94A) showing non-immigrant status; Foreign passport; Employment Authorization Document (I-766) |
| **J-1, J-2** (exchange visitors including professors, scholars and physicians) | Form **DS-2019**, Certificate of Eligibility for Exchange Visitor Status; Arrival/Departure Record (I-94/I-94A); Foreign passport; **I-797**, Notice of Action |
| **H, L, O, P, Q** (temporary worker non-immigrant visas) | Foreign passport; Form **I-797**, Notice of Action |
| **T** (victim of human trafficking) | Foreign passport; I-797, Notice of Action for "T" Status; Arrival/Departure Record (I-94/I-94A); Form **I-485**, Adjustment of Status |
| **U** (victim of criminal activity) | Arrival/Departure Record (I-94/I-94A) indicating "U" non-immigrant visa; Receipt of Form **I-918**; Form **I-539**, Application to Extend Non-immigrant Status |
| **All other valid non-immigrant letter visa categories** | Valid non-immigrant visas begin with the letters **A through V**; including but not limited to **A, K, and R** visas; Arrival/Departure Record (I-94/I-94A) indicating admission to the US with a non-immigrant visa |

**12. Citizens of Micronesia, the Marshall Islands and Palau.** (Compact of Free Association migrants)

- Arrival/Departure Record (**I-94/I-94A**)
- Foreign Passport (annotated "CFA/RMI," "CFA/FSM" or "CFA/PAL")
- Employment Authorization Document (Card) (**I-766**) annotated "A8"

**13. Residents of American Samoa.**

- An official document showing the individual is a resident of, or lives in, American Samoa

#### Temporary and deferred statuses

**14. Lawful temporary residents.**

- Employment Authorization Document (Card) (**I-766**) annotated "A2", or with other evidence indicating eligibility under "§210" or "§245A"
- Application to Adjust from Temporary to Permanent Residence under INA §245A (**I-698**)

**15. Individuals with Temporary Protected Status (TPS).** For pending applicants for TPS, go to #23.

- Employment Authorization Document (Card) (**I-766**) annotated "A12"
- Notice of Action (**I-797**) showing grant of TPS status

**16. Individuals granted Deferred Enforced Departure (DED).**

- Employment Authorization Document (Card) (**I-766**) annotated "A11"
- Immigration documentation with an alien number or I-94 showing status

**17. Individuals granted Deferred Action Status.**

- Notice of Action (**I-797**) showing approval of "deferred action" status
- Employment Authorization Document (Card) (**I-766**) annotated "C14"

**18. Family Unity beneficiaries.**

- Notice of Action (**I-797**) showing approval for "Application for Family Unity [**I-817**]"
- Employment Authorization Document (Card) (**I-766**) annotated "A13"

**19. Individuals with Order of Supervision with employment authorization.**

- Notice or form showing release under "Order of Supervision" and Employment Authorization Document (Card) (**I-766**)

**20. Registry applicants with employment authorization.**

- Receipt or notice showing filing Form **I-485** and Employment Authorization Document (Card) (**I-766**)

#### Pending applications and adjustment of status

**21. Applicants for Special Immigrant Juvenile Status.**

- Notice of Action (**I-797**) — "Special Immigrant Juvenile Approval Notice," "Welcome Notice/Approval of I-485," or "Other Basis of Adjustment **SL6**"
- Permanent Resident Card, "Green Card" (**I-551**) coded "SL6"

**22. Applicants for adjustment to LPR status.**

- Notice of Action (**I-797**) — ASC Appointment notice with Case Type "I-485 Application" and "Receipt of Application for Employment Authorization based on C9"
- Employment Authorization Document (Card) (**I-766**), e.g. annotated "C9" or "C9P"
- Employment Authorization and Advance Parole Card (**I-512**)
- Receipt showing filing or pending status of Form **I-485**

**23. Applicants for the following statuses must also submit an Employment Authorization Document (I-766).**

| Status | Supporting document | EAD annotation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Temporary Protected Status (TPS) with employment authorization | Receipt or notice showing filing or pending status of "Application for Temporary Protected Status" (**I-821**) | "C19" |
| Legalization under IRCA or the LIFE Act with employment authorization | Foreign passport with a stamp or writing by INS/DHS officer indicating pending "§245A Application" | "Application for Temporary Residence under INA §245A" [**I-687**] |
| Asylum or Withholding of Deportation/Removal, including CAT applicants (only if granted employment authorization, or **under age 14 and pending for at least 180 days**) | Receipt or notice showing filing or pending status of "Application for Asylum and Withholding" (**I-589**) | "C8" |
| Cancellation of Removal or Suspension of Deportation with employment authorization | Receipt or notice showing filing Form **EOIR-40**, **EOIR-42**, or "Application for Suspension of Deportation or Special Rule Cancellation of Removal" (**I-881**) | Any I-766 |

**24. Amerasian immigrants.**
**25. Applicants for Creation of Record of Lawful Admission for Permanent Residence, with Employment Authorization.**

### How to submit your document

1. **Check what Covered California asked for** — check your eligibility notice or sign in to your account to see who in your household needs to submit proof and what type is needed.
2. **Find an accepted document** — you only need to submit one document from the list.
3. **Submit a copy** — online, by fax, by mail, or in person.
4. **Watch for updates** — Covered California will review your document, and if more information is needed you will receive a notice or account message.
