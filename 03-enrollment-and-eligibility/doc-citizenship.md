# Proof of Citizenship

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment/documents/citizenship
- **Covered California section:** Eligibility & Enrollment — Documents — Proof of Citizenship or U.S. National Status
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page lists the documents Covered California accepts to confirm U.S. citizenship or U.S. national status. You can submit a single primary document, or — if you have none — copies of two documents, one from List A (citizenship or U.S. place-of-birth evidence) and one from List B (identity evidence). Federally recognized tribal documents can serve as primary proof.

## Key Facts

- **"U.S. national status"** includes U.S. citizens and people born in certain U.S. territories, such as **American Samoa or Swains Island**, or born to non-citizen U.S. national parents.
- You only need to submit **one primary document**.
- If you have no primary document, you must submit copies of **two documents: one from List A and one from List B**. Both are required.
- U.S. medical records must be **signed and on hospital letterhead**, established at the time of your birth, and show your U.S. place of birth.
- U.S. insurance records must be created **at least five years before** the initial Covered California application date, unless the applicant is **under age five**.
- Official religious records must be created **within three months of birth**.
- Identity evidence via clinic/doctor/hospital/school records applies to children **under 19 years old**.
- Department of State Form **FS-545** is no longer issued but is still accepted.

## Details

### Primary documents you can submit

Submit a copy of **one** of the following. You only need to submit one primary document.

| Primary document | Accepted forms |
| --- | --- |
| U.S. Passport | U.S. Passport or U.S. Passport Card, current or expired (issued without limitation) |
| Citizenship or Naturalization Certificates | Certificate of U.S. Citizenship (**Form N-560** or **Form N-561**); Certificate of Naturalization (**Forms N-550** or **N-570**) |
| Federally Recognized Tribal Documents | Documents issued by a federally recognized American Indian or Alaska Native tribe that identify you by name and confirm membership, enrollment or affiliation |

Sample images are available on USCIS.gov.

**Accepted tribal documents include, but are not limited to:**

- Tribal enrollment card
- Certificate of Degree of Indian Blood (**CDIB**)
- Tribal census document
- Documents on tribal letterhead, signed by the appropriate Tribal official

### If you don't have a primary document

If you do not have any of the primary documents listed above, you must submit copies of **two documents**: one from List A and one from List B. **Both documents are required.**

### List A: Citizenship or U.S. Place-of-Birth Evidence

**Birth and citizenship records**

| Document | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| U.S. Public Birth Certificate | — |
| Consular Report of Birth Abroad (**CRBA**) | See travel.state.gov for details |
| Department of State Form **FS-545** | No longer issued but still accepted |
| Department of State Form **FS-240** | — |
| Department of State Form **DS-1350** | — |
| U.S. citizen identification card (**I-197**, or prior version **I-179**) | See USCIS.gov for descriptions |
| Northern Mariana Identification Card (**I-873**) | Issued to a collectively naturalized citizen born in the CNMI before **Nov. 4, 1986** |

**Adoption and child citizenship records**

- A final adoption decree showing the child's name and U.S. place of birth; or, if the adoption is not finalized, a statement from a state-approved adoption agency showing the child's name, U.S. place of birth, and birthdate or age. The agency must state that the source of the place-of-birth information is an original birth certificate.
- Documentation of a foreign-born child (including a foreign-born adopted child) who received automatic U.S. citizenship through **IR3** or **IH3** or the Child Citizenship Act. Documentation must clearly show the child's name and date of birth.

**Service and military records**

- U.S. military records showing a U.S. place of birth
- Evidence of U.S. Civil Service employment before **June 1, 1976**

**Medical, insurance, religious or school records**

| Record type | Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| U.S. medical records (hospital, clinic, midwife or physician records; admission papers from a nursing facility, skilled care facility or other institution) | Must show your U.S. place of birth; be **signed and on hospital letterhead**; be **established at the time of your birth** |
| U.S. insurance records | Must show a U.S. place of birth and be created **at least five years before** the initial Covered California application date, **unless the applicant is under age five** |
| Official religious records recorded in the U.S. | Must be created **within three months of birth**; indicate date of birth or age at the time the record was made; be an official record recorded with the religious organization |
| School records | Including preschool, Head Start and daycare records showing the child's name |

**Other government records**

- Individual Fee Register receipt (**INS Form G-711**)
- Federal or state census record showing U.S. citizenship or U.S. place of birth

### List B: Identity Evidence

| Document | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Driver's license issued by a state or territory | Or an ID card issued by the federal, state or local government |
| School identification card | — |
| U.S. military card or draft record | Or a military dependent's identification card |
| U.S. Coast Guard Merchant Mariner card | — |
| A clinic, doctor, hospital or school record | Including preschool or day care records (for children **under 19 years old**) |
| Two documents containing consistent information that prove your identity | Such as: birth certificate, Social Security card, marriage certificate, divorce decree, employer identification card, high school or college diploma (including high school equivalency diplomas), property deed or title document |

### Not lawfully present?

Not lawfully present in the U.S.? You may still qualify for Medi-Cal. Visit "Information for Immigrants" to learn about your options.

### How to submit

Follow the instructions in your Covered California account or eligibility notice to submit your document online, by fax, or by mail. Submit **copies** only.
