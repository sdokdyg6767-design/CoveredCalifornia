# Documents to Confirm Eligibility

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment/documents
- **Covered California section:** Eligibility & Enrollment — Documents and Verification
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This is the hub page for proof of eligibility. It lists the nine categories of proof Covered California may request — income, identity, citizenship or U.S. national status, immigration status or lawful presence, Social Security number, American Indian/Alaska Native status, lack of minimum essential coverage, non-incarcerated status, and notification of a deceased member — and explains four ways to submit documents. Check your letter or account message first, because Covered California accepts only the document type requested.

## Key Facts

- Proof of Immigration Status covers **25 immigration status types**; you submit **one** accepted document.
- Submit only the document type your notice or account message asked for — **not all documents are accepted for every request**.
- **Send copies only. Do not send original documents** by fax or mail.
- **Fax:** (888) 329-3700 — include the document cover page (PDF).
- **Mail:** Covered California, P.O. Box 989725, West Sacramento, CA 95798-9725.
- **Online:** sign in to your account and upload via Household Summary, Eligibility Results, or Documents and Correspondence sections.
- **In person:** free help from a certified enroller or insurance agent.
- Service center: **(800) 300-1506**.
- Do not wait to submit your application just because you're missing a document.

## Details

### The nine document types

| Document type | What it confirms | Examples listed on the site |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proof of Income | Household income | Pay stubs, tax returns, wage statements or other documents |
| Proof of Identity | Who you are | Driver's license, passport, military ID, school record or other accepted document; options available for children under 16 |
| Proof of Citizenship or U.S. National Status | U.S. citizenship or national status | Birth certificate, passport or naturalization certificate; includes options if you don't have primary documentation |
| Proof of Immigration Status or Lawful Presence | Immigration status category | Find your status category and submit one accepted document; covers **25 immigration status types** |
| Proof of Social Security Number | Your SSN | SSA card, tax document or other accepted record; includes options if you don't have an SSN or your application is pending |
| American Indian or Alaska Native Documentation | Tribal status | Tribal records, federal certifications or other accepted documents |
| Proof You Don't Have Minimum Essential Coverage | That you lack other qualifying health insurance | Coverage letter, employer statement or Medicare documentation |
| Proof of Non-Incarcerated Status | That you aren't currently incarcerated | Release papers, parole documents or a downloadable attestation form |
| Notification of Deceased Status | Death of someone with a Covered California plan | Report the death, or correct an account incorrectly flagged as deceased |

### Before you submit

- Submit **only the document type your notice or account message asked for** — not all documents are accepted for every request.
- **Send copies only. Do not send original documents** by fax or mail.
- Make sure your document is **clear and includes the information requested**.
- Keep your **notice and case number** available in case you need to follow up with Covered California.

### Why documents are requested

When you apply, Covered California checks the information you provide against government data sources, such as the **IRS** and the **Social Security Administration**. If something doesn't match, you may be asked to send proof to confirm the new information. This doesn't necessarily mean you made an error — it's a routine part of the eligibility process.

### If you don't have all the information yet

Don't wait to submit your application just because you're missing a document. You can still apply, and Covered California will contact you about what to do next. If you don't hear from them, call **(800) 300-1506**.
