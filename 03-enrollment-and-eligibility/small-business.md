## Summary

Covered California for Small Business (CCSB) is the marketplace landing page for California employers. It summarizes what an employer can do with CCSB — choose which coverage levels to offer, control contribution costs, and let employees choose their own plans — and publishes two figures of its own: a federal tax credit worth **up to 50 percent** of premium contributions, and the **50 or more full-time-equivalent employees** threshold that triggers the employer mandate. It links to eligibility, enrollment, tax credit and help resources. The page publishes no data tables.

### What you can do with CCSB

**Choose What to Offer.** Select the coverage levels — Bronze, Silver, Gold or Platinum — that you want to make available to your employees. You can offer anywhere from one tier to all four, and add dental insurance as an option.

**Control Your Costs.** You decide how much to contribute toward employee premiums. Receive one monthly bill for all your coverage, making it easier to manage and budget for health benefits.

**Let Employees Choose Their Plans.** Employees pick the plans that work best for their needs and budget from the options you offer. Enrollment is year-round, so adding or removing employees is easy.

### Not the right fit? We can still help.

If you're self-employed with no W-2 employees, individual insurance through Covered California may be a better option — and you could qualify for financial help to lower your monthly premium.

### What to know before you enroll

Most employers start with two questions: Does my business qualify, and can I get help with costs?

### Tax credits may lower your costs

Some small businesses that offer insurance through CCSB qualify for a federal tax credit worth **up to 50 percent** of their premium contributions — available only through Covered California for Small Business. The credit is based on your business size, average employee wages and how much you contribute toward premiums. Not all businesses qualify — eligibility is determined by the IRS.

### Ready to see your options?

Submit a quote request and the CCSB team will follow up **within two business days** with plan options and pricing based on your business needs — at no cost to you.

### If your business has 50 or more employees

Businesses with **50 or more full-time-equivalent employees** may be required by law to offer health insurance that meets federal standards or face a payment to the IRS. CCSB can help applicable employers meet this requirement. If your business has fewer than 50 employees, you are generally not required to offer insurance.
