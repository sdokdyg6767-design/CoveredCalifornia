# Open Enrollment

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment/open-enrollment

- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

Open enrollment is the annual window when every eligible Californian can enroll in, renew, or change a Covered California health or dental plan. It runs from November 1 through January 31. Outside that window, you can only enroll if you qualify for a special enrollment period after a qualifying major life change, which usually lasts 60 days from the date of the event. Because Covered California and Medi-Cal share one application, applying determines which program you qualify for.

## Key Facts

- Open enrollment lasts **from Nov. 1 to Jan. 31**.
- During open enrollment you can: enroll for the first time, switch plans, or renew your plan for the coming year.
- To get a health plan that starts **Jan. 1**, you must enroll by **Dec. 31**.
- If you enroll **in January**, your plan will start **Feb. 1**.
- A special enrollment period "usually lasts **60 days** from the date of the event."

- One application covers both Covered California and Medi-Cal.
- After choosing a plan you pay your first bill; a bill from your insurance company "usually takes about two weeks."
- You can use your plan before your membership ID card arrives.

## Details

### What is open enrollment?

Open enrollment is the period when all eligible Californians can apply for a health or dental insurance plan through Covered California. It lasts from **Nov. 1 to Jan. 31**. During this time you can enroll for the first time, switch plans, or renew your plan for the coming year. When you apply, Covered California checks your information to determine what financial help you could get and what other programs you may be eligible for, including Medi-Cal.

### Other enrollment options

If open enrollment is over, you can enroll in health insurance if you experience a **major life change** — such as losing insurance or getting married. This is called a **special enrollment period**, and it "usually lasts 60 days from the date of the event." Otherwise, you must wait for the next open-enrollment period to enroll.

### Medi-Cal enrollment

Medi-Cal and Covered California share an application. When you apply, Covered California tells you which program you are eligible for. This means people who think they are too late for open enrollment may still be routed into Medi-Cal enrollment.

### How enrollment works

1. **Apply** — One application covers both Covered California and Medi-Cal.
2. **Choose a plan and enroll** — Compare options and pick what fits your budget.
3. **Pay your first bill** — Pay online, or wait for a bill from your insurance company; this usually takes about two weeks.
4. **Get your ID card** — Your insurance company mails the membership ID card once it receives your payment.
