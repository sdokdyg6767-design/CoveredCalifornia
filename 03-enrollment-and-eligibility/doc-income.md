# Proof of Income

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment/documents/income
- **Covered California section:** Eligibility & Enrollment — Documents — Proof of Income
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

Covered California compares your reported income against IRS and Social Security Administration records, and may ask you to confirm income with a document if the information does not match. The page organizes accepted proof by income type — employer wages, foreign income, self-employment including farm income, unearned income, and Social Security or unemployment benefits — and specifies exactly what each document must show. In most cases you submit one document.

## Key Facts

- In most cases you only need to submit **one document**.
- An **employer statement must be dated within 45 days** of submission to Covered California.
- Employer statements must include **two certification statements** verbatim.
- Alimony received is **not counted as taxable income** for divorce or separation documents dated after **Dec. 31, 2018**.
- Scholarships or grants count **for room and board only** — not tuition, course-related fees, books or equipment.
- The **Attestation of Income, No Documentation Available** form is accepted on a **case-by-case basis** and is signed under a penalty of perjury.
- Attestation forms are valid **only for the benefit year** for which coverage is requested and **must be renewed each benefit year**.
- Foreign income uses **dollar conversion based on the date of the document**.

## Details

### Before you submit

Your household income helps determine whether you qualify for Medi-Cal or a Covered California plan and how much financial help you would get. Covered California compares the income you reported with records from government sources, like the **IRS** and **Social Security Administration**. If the information doesn't match, you may be asked to confirm your income by submitting a document.

### Employer wages

Income earned through a job or other work. Submit a copy of one of the following.

| Document | What it must include |
| --- | --- |
| **Pay stub** | Your full name or other identifying information (for example, a Social Security number); income amount; pay period or frequency of pay with the date of payment |
| **Federal Income Tax Form 1040** | Your most recently filed Form 1040, with any appropriate schedules; must include the tax year |
| **Wage or income tax statement** | Includes W-2, 1099-MISC, 1099G, 1099R, 1099SSA, 1099DIV, 1099SS, 1099INT, 1099NEC, or other forms displaying income and taxes. Must include your first and last name, income amount, the year, and employer name if applicable |
| **Employer statement** | Used if no other formal documentation, such as a pay stub or tax return, is available. A sample employer statement is available as a PDF |

#### Employer statement requirements

**Format requirements.** The statement must:

- Be on company letterhead or state the name of the company
- Be signed by the employer
- Be **dated within 45 days** of submission to Covered California

**Required content.** The statement must include:

- Name of employer or company
- Name of person writing the letter
- Employer or company address
- Employer or company telephone number
- Date of the letter
- Start date of employment (and end date, if no longer employed)
- The signature, printed name and job title or position of the person writing the letter

**Required certification statements.** The statement must include **both** of the following:

> "I certify that [first and last name of person employed or receiving income] is/was an employee of [name of company]. [Employee name]'s gross income for this pay period is/was $__________ and frequency of pay is [weekly, every two weeks, twice a month, or monthly]. This letter does not guarantee employment or wages."

> "The information provided above is true and correct to the best of my knowledge."

### Foreign income

You may use a pay stub or other documents. Use **dollar conversion based on the date of the document**.

### Self-employment income (including farm income)

Submit a copy of one of the following:

| Document | What it must contain |
| --- | --- |
| **Profit and loss statement or ledger** | Most recent quarterly or year-to-date profit and loss statement, or a self-employment ledger. Must contain your first and last name and company name; dates covered; net income from profit or loss |
| **Federal Tax Form 1040 with Schedule C** | Your Form 1040 with any appropriate schedules is also acceptable |

### Unearned income

Money earned through investments, disbursements, and other non-work activities. Submit a copy of one of the following:

- Annuity statements
- Statements of pension distribution from any government or private source
- Prizes, settlements and awards, including alimony received and court-ordered award letters
- Proof of taxable gifts and contributions
- Proof of taxable scholarships or grants
- Proof of strike pay and other benefits from unions
- Sales receipts or other proof of money received from the sale, exchange or replacement of things you own
- Interest and dividends income statement
- Royalty or residual income statement or 1099-MISC
- Letter, deposit or other proof of deferred compensation payments

**Notes:**

- For divorce or separation documents dated **after Dec. 31, 2018**, **alimony received is not counted as taxable income**.
- Proof of taxable scholarships or grants counts **for room and board only, not tuition, course-related fees, books or equipment**.

### Social Security and unemployment benefits

| Document | What it must contain |
| --- | --- |
| **Social Security Administration statements** | Includes Social Security Benefits Letters for Retirement, Survivors Disability Insurance (RSDI), Social Security Retirement and Social Security Disability Insurance (SSDI). Must contain your first and last name, benefit amount, and frequency of pay |
| **Unemployment benefits letter** | Must contain your first and last name, source or agency, weekly benefit amount and duration, including start and end date if applicable |

### If you don't have income documents

If you don't have any of the above documents, you may still be able to provide a written statement confirming your income using the **Attestation of Income, No Documentation Available (PDF)** form. Submit it by uploading it to your online account, by fax, or by mail.

- The form is accepted on a **case-by-case basis** and is **signed under a penalty of perjury**.
- Attestation forms are valid **only for the benefit year** for which coverage is requested.
- They **must be renewed each benefit year**.
