# Proof of Social Security Number

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment/documents/social-security-number
- **Covered California section:** Eligibility & Enrollment — Documents — Proof of Social Security Number
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page lists the four documents Covered California accepts to confirm a Social Security number, and confirms that only one document is needed. It includes an option for applicants who refuse to obtain an SSN for established religious objections, commonly called an SSN Waiver.

## Key Facts

- You only need to send **one** accepted document.
- Your eligibility notice or account message tells you **who in your household** needs to submit proof and how to send it.
- An **SSN Waiver** letter is accepted for those who refuse to obtain an SSN for established religious objections.

## Details

### Before you submit

Your eligibility notice or account message will tell you who in your household needs to submit proof of your Social Security number and how to send it.

### Documents you can submit

You only need to send **one** accepted document.

| Document | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Social Security card | Standard proof |
| Tax form | Must show the Social Security number |
| Benefit or income statement from Social Security | Must show the Social Security number |
| Letter stating that the applicant refuses to obtain an SSN for established religious objections | Also called an **SSN Waiver** |

### How to submit

Follow the instructions in your Covered California account or eligibility notice to submit your document online, by fax, or by mail.
