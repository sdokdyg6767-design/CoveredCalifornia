# Proof of Social Security Number

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment/documents/social-security-number
- **Covered California section:** Eligibility & Enrollment — Documents — Proof of Social Security Number
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page tells you which document to submit to confirm a Social Security number (SSN). It confirms that you only need to send **one** accepted document, and organizes the accepted documents into three situations: you have a document with your Social Security number, your Social Security number is pending or unavailable, and you have an established religious objection. It also covers when to submit and how to submit.

## Key Facts

- You only need to send **one** accepted document.
- If your Social Security number is **pending or unavailable**, you can still submit a tax form or a benefit or income statement from Social Security.
- An **SSN Waiver** letter is accepted for those who refuse to obtain an SSN for established religious objections.
- Your eligibility notice or account message tells you **who in your household** needs to submit proof and how to send it.

## Details

### Before you submit

Your eligibility notice or account message will tell you who in your household needs to submit proof of your Social Security number and how to send it.

### Documents you can submit

You only need to send **one** accepted document.

| If this applies to you: | Submit one of these documents: |
| --- | --- |
| You have a document with your SSN. | Social Security card<br>Tax form<br>Benefit or income statement from Social Security that shows the Social Security number |
| Your SSN is pending or unavailable. | Tax form<br>Benefit or income statement from Social Security that shows the Social Security number |
| You have an established religious objection. | Letter stating that the applicant refuses to obtain an SSN for established religious objections (also called an SSN Waiver) |

### Ready to submit your document?

Follow the instructions in your Covered California account or eligibility notice to submit your document online, by fax, or by mail.
