# Proof of Identity

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment/documents/identity
- **Covered California section:** Eligibility & Enrollment — Documents — Proof of Identity
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page lists the documents Covered California accepts to verify identity. Most people submit one document, such as a driver's license, passport, or immigration document. Dedicated options exist for children under 16, and if you have none of the listed documents you may submit two documents that together establish your identity.

## Key Facts

- Submit a copy of **one** of the listed documents in most cases.
- A child **under age 16** has a dedicated document path (clinic, doctor, hospital or school record).
- For children under 16, a parent/guardian birth statement **cannot be used together with a citizenship affidavit** — the two serve the same purpose and cannot substitute for each other.
- If you cannot provide any listed identity document, you must provide copies of **two** documents.
- An **expired passport may be used** as identity proof.

## Details

### Documents you can submit

Submit a copy of **one** of the following documents.

**Government, state or local ID**

- Driver's license issued by a state or territory
- Government-issued photo identification card (federal, state or local)

**Passport or foreign government ID**

- U.S. passport, foreign passport or U.S. passport card. An **expired passport may be used**.
- Identification card issued by a foreign embassy or consulate that contains a photograph

**School, military and voter records**

- School identification card that includes a photograph
- Voter registration card
- U.S. military card, draft record or military dependent's identification card

**Citizenship, immigration or work authorization document**

| Document | Form |
| --- | --- |
| Certificate of Naturalization | Form **N-550** or **N-570** |
| Certificate of U.S. Citizenship | Form **N-560** or **N-561** |
| Permanent Resident Card or Alien Registration Receipt Card | Form **I-551** |
| Employment Authorization Document that contains a photograph | Form **I-766** |

Sample images are available on USCIS.gov for descriptions of naturalization certificates, green cards, and employment authorization documents.

**Other government-issued records**

- Native American tribal document with photograph
- U.S. Coast Guard Merchant Mariner card
- A verified identity record from a federal or state government agency — including public assistance, law enforcement, tax, or corrections agencies. The record must confirm the agency has verified and certified your identity.

### Documents for children under 16

For a child **under age 16**, submit one of the following:

| Option | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Clinic, doctor, hospital or school record | If none of those records is available, use the option below |
| Written statement | Signed under penalty of perjury by the parent or guardian stating the child's date and place of birth |

The written statement **cannot be used together with a citizenship affidavit** — a citizenship affidavit and a birth statement serve the same purpose and cannot substitute for each other.

### If you don't have one of these documents

If you cannot provide any of the identity documents listed above, you must provide copies of **two** documents:

1. Birth certificate
2. Social Security card
3. Marriage certificate
4. Divorce decree
5. Employer identification card
6. High school or college diploma (including high school equivalency diplomas)
7. Property deed or title document
8. Adoption decree
9. Foreign school record that contains a photograph
10. Notice from a public benefits agency
11. Union or worker's center identification card

### How to submit

Follow the instructions in your Covered California account or eligibility notice to submit your document online, by fax, or by mail.
