# Notification of Deceased Status

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment/documents/notification-of-a-deceased-member
- **Covered California section:** Eligibility & Enrollment — Documents — Notification of Deceased Status
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page explains how to report the death of someone covered through Covered California, and how to correct an account that was mistakenly flagged as deceased. It provides three reporting forms depending on your relationship to the deceased, and a long list of accepted proof-of-death documents. It also states the required contents of a declaration or affidavit used to reverse an incorrect deceased flag.

## Key Facts

- Three forms, chosen by your relationship to the deceased:
  - **"I'm not on the plan."** — for a friend, neighbor, hospital or other third party reporting a death.
  - **"I am on the plan."** — for an enrolled Covered California member reporting the death of someone on your plan.
  - **"I represent the estate."** — for the executor, estate representative, or someone acting on behalf of the deceased's estate.
- Before choosing a form, have ready: the deceased's **full name, date of birth, date of death**, and their **Covered California member ID** if available.
- Completed forms may be mailed or faxed using the contact information included in the form.
- Estate representatives must also include documentation of their **legal authority to act** on behalf of the estate.
- "11,000 enrollers are here to help with your documentation at no cost."

## Details

### Report a death

If someone with health insurance through Covered California has died, notify Covered California using the form that matches your situation. You can mail or fax the completed form using the contact information included in the form.

Before you choose a form, have the following ready:

- The deceased's **full name**
- **Date of birth**
- **Date of death**
- Their **Covered California member ID**, if available

### Choose your form

| Form | Who should use it |
| --- | --- |
| **"I'm not on the plan."** | A friend, neighbor, hospital or other third party reporting a death |
| **"I am on the plan."** | An enrolled Covered California member reporting the death of someone on your plan |
| **"I represent the estate."** | The executor, estate representative, or someone acting on behalf of the deceased's estate |

### Accepted proof of death documents

Depending on which form you submit, you may need to include one of the following documents as proof of death:

**Official records**

- Death certificate
- Bureau of Vital Statistics records
- Social Security records
- Hospital or medical records
- Veterans Administration or military service records
- Indian census records
- Insurance company records
- Institutional records

**Statements and notices**

- Signed funeral director's statement
- Newspaper death notice or obituary
- Minister or clergy statement

**Benefits and correspondence**

- Widow or survivor's benefits on the deceased's Social Security number
- Veterans Administration death payment correspondence
- Insurance company death settlement correspondence

**Estate and property documents**

- Proof of executor or estate
- Probate court order or Letter of Testamentary
- Recorded Affidavit of Death of a Joint Tenant
- Notarized Affidavit for Collection of Personal Property

Each form includes specific instructions for what to submit. Estate representatives also need to include documentation of their legal authority to act on behalf of the estate.

### If you were incorrectly marked as deceased

Occasionally, a data error may incorrectly flag someone as deceased. Covered California apologizes for the inconvenience. Submit a **signed declaration or affidavit** to correct the record.

The declaration or affidavit must include:

- A statement that the individual is **not deceased**
- A statement that the information provided is **true and correct to the best of their knowledge**
- The **date and signature** of the individual or their authorized representative
