# Eligibility Guidelines

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment/eligibility
- **Covered California section:** Eligibility & Enrollment — Eligibility Guidelines
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page explains how Covered California and Medi-Cal decide eligibility using household size, income, California residency, and immigration status. One application screens you for both programs and returns eligibility results for each person in your household. Different people in the same household can qualify for different programs, which Covered California calls a mixed-program family.

## Key Facts

- You must be a **California resident** to qualify.
- Your household includes: you, your spouse or domestic partner, and anyone you claim as a tax dependency — even if they are not applying for health insurance.
- Income is measured against the **federal poverty level (FPL)** for your household size.
- Only the people applying need to share Social Security numbers or immigration information; the rest of the household can still be listed.
- Having other health insurance does not disqualify you from applying, but it may affect whether you qualify for financial help.
- Moving to California from out of state is a **major life change** that may allow enrollment outside open enrollment.
- Some eligibility rules depend on age, disability status, and other specific situations.
- If Covered California cannot confirm information from your application, you may be asked to submit documents.

## Details

### How eligibility works

The Covered California application is designed to determine eligibility for health insurance programs, including Medi-Cal, and to show available plans. What you qualify for depends on a few things about you and your household.

### Your household

Your household includes everyone, even people who are not applying for health insurance:

| Included in household | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| You | The applicant |
| Your spouse or domestic partner | Included regardless of whether they apply |
| Anyone you claim as a tax dependency | Included regardless of whether they apply |

Household size and income affect which program you qualify for and how much financial help you may get.

### Your eligibility results

After you apply, you will see eligibility results for each person in your household.

| Program | Who It's For |
| --- | --- |
| **Covered California** | California residents who are lawfully present. Depending on income, you may also qualify for financial help to lower your cost. |
| **Medi-Cal** | People with lower incomes, children, pregnant individuals and others who meet Medi-Cal eligibility criteria. |
| **Mixed-Program Family** | Families where some members qualify for Medi-Cal and others qualify for Covered California. |

Different people in the same household can qualify for different programs. This is normal and is called being a **mixed-program family**.

Shop and Compare can estimate your costs for a health plan through Covered California or check if you qualify for Medi-Cal instead.

### Covered California and Medi-Cal eligibility

Your income and household size determine which program you qualify for. If you qualify for Medi-Cal, you get free or low-cost health insurance. If you qualify for a Covered California plan instead, your income also determines how much financial help you get, if any.

### Factors that determine eligibility

- **Your income** — measured against the federal poverty level for your household size.
- **Where you live** — you must be a California resident. Moving to California from out of state is a major life change that may let you enroll outside open enrollment.
- **Citizenship or immigration status** — only the people applying need to share Social Security numbers or immigration information; the rest of your household can still be listed.
- **Current health insurance** — including insurance through your job, Medicare, or other insurance. Having other health insurance does not disqualify you, but it may affect financial help eligibility.
- **Other factors** — age, disability status, and other specific situations.

### Verification

If Covered California cannot confirm information from your application, you may be asked to submit documents to confirm your eligibility. Covered California will tell you what type of proof is needed and how to submit it so the application can be reviewed.

### Have a specific situation?

A few groups have different eligibility or financial help rules.

- **Immigrants and Mixed-Status Households** — Eligibility and financial help depend on your immigration status. Households with different statuses can still apply together.
- **Young Adults** — If you're under 30, you may qualify for a lower-cost minimum coverage plan in addition to standard options.
- **American Indian and Alaska Native** — Members of federally recognized tribes may qualify for extra benefits and enrollment flexibility.
- **People With Chronic Conditions** — Compare plans for ongoing care, learn what to check before switching and get help if care is disrupted.

### Help at every step

Free and confidential help is available in person, by chat, or by phone. Additional routes include:
- Shop and Compare, to estimate costs for a Covered California plan or check Medi-Cal eligibility.
- Find Free Local Help — a certified enroller who walks you through options and helps you apply.
- Find Your County Medi-Cal Office, for application help and program information.
- Help on Demand — a free callback from a certified enroller within 15 minutes, for first-time applicants only.
