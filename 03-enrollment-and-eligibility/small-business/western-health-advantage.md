## Summary

Western Health Advantage (WHA) is a nonprofit HMO headquartered in Sacramento offering CCSB plans to employers in Northern California. It serves 10 Northern California counties with an HMO network and nearly 30 years of experience delivering local care. All WHA plans include embedded pediatric dental insurance at no extra cost. The page currently publishes 2027 plan documents and a 2027 group service agreement only.

## Key Facts

- Western Health Advantage is a **nonprofit HMO headquartered in Sacramento**, created with a community-based mission by a coalition of doctors and health care providers.
- It serves employers in **10 Northern California counties**: Sacramento, El Dorado, Placer, Yolo, Colusa, Solano, Napa, Sonoma, Marin and Humboldt.
- WHA has **nearly 30 years of experience** delivering local care.
- **All WHA health plans include embedded pediatric dental insurance** — children's dental benefits are built into the health plan at **no extra cost**.
- Plan designs are **HMO only**, including one HDHP variant in the Silver tier.
- WHA offers plans through CCSB for employers located in **Northern California**.
- Only **2027** plan documents and the **2027** group service agreement are published on this page.

### Notes for employers
