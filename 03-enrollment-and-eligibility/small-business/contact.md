# Contact Us — Covered California for Small Business

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/small-business/contact
- **Covered California section:** For Small Business — Contact
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page lists every channel for contacting Covered California for Small Business, separated by new customers, existing customers, billing, and document submission. It also provides four distinct mailing addresses depending on whether you are sending premium payments (standard or overnight) or enrollment documents, and points to external regulators and advocates for issues CCSB cannot resolve.

## Key Facts

- **New customers:** **(844) 332-8384** for quotes and enrollment help; **smallbusiness@covered.ca.gov** for non-urgent questions.
- **Existing customers:** **(855) 777-6782** for renewal support, MyCCSB technical help, and general questions; **ccsbeligibility@covered.ca.gov** for non-urgent questions.
- **Billing and invoices:** **ccsbfinance@covered.ca.gov**.
- Phone hours: **Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.**
- Local licensed insurance agents help with quotes and enrollment **at no cost**.
- **Standard monthly premium payments** go to a different address than enrollment documents.

## Details

### New customers

| Channel | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Call** | **(844) 332-8384** for quotes and enrollment help |
| **Email** | **smallbusiness@covered.ca.gov** for non-urgent questions about quotes, enrollment, or new business |
| **Find an agent** | Local licensed insurance agents help with quotes and enrollment **at no cost to you** |

### Existing customers

| Channel | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Call** | **(855) 777-6782** for renewal support, MyCCSB technical help, and general questions |
| **Email** | **ccsbeligibility@covered.ca.gov** for non-urgent questions about your plan, enrollment, or account |

**Phone hours for both lines:** Monday through Friday, **8 a.m. to 5 p.m.**

### Billing and invoices

For questions about invoices or payments, email **ccsbfinance@covered.ca.gov**.
