# Covered California for Small Business

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/small-business
- **Covered California section:** For Small Business — Covered California for Small Business (CCSB)
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

Covered California for Small Business (CCSB) is the marketplace for California employers with 1 to 100 full-time equivalent employees. Employers choose which of the four metal coverage levels to offer, set their own contribution toward employee premiums, and receive one monthly bill for all coverage. Enrollment is year-round, and qualifying employers may claim a federal tax credit worth up to 50 percent of their premium contributions, available only through CCSB.

## Key Facts

- Open to California employers with **1 to 100 full-time equivalent employees**.
- Your business must have **at least one W-2 employee who is not the owner or the owner's spouse**.
- You must contribute **at least 50 percent toward each employee's premium**.
- Qualifying businesses may receive a **federal tax credit worth up to 50 percent** of their premium contributions, available **only through Covered California for Small Business**. Eligibility is determined by the **IRS**.
- Businesses with **50 or more** full-time-equivalent employees **may be required by law** to offer health insurance meeting federal standards or face a **payment to the IRS**. Businesses with fewer than 50 employees are generally not required to offer insurance.
- You may offer anywhere from **one tier to all four** coverage levels (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum), including HMO and PPO options, and may add dental.
- **Enrollment is year-round.** To start insurance in a given month, submit your completed application **within the first five business days of that month**; otherwise insurance begins the following month.
- A quote request is followed up **within two business days** with plan options and pricing, at no cost.

## Details

### What you can do with CCSB

**Choose what to offer.** Select the coverage levels — Bronze, Silver, Gold or Platinum — that you want to make available to your employees. You can offer anywhere from one tier to all four, and add dental insurance as an option. CCSB makes it easy to choose up to **four adjoining levels of coverage**, giving employees a choice.

**Control your costs.** You decide how much to contribute toward employee premiums. You receive **one monthly bill** for all your coverage, making it easier to manage and budget.

**Let employees choose their plans.** Employees pick the plans that work best for their needs and budget from the options you offer. Adding or removing employees is easy because enrollment is year-round.

### Basic eligibility

Covered California for Small Business is open to California employers with **1 to 100 full-time-equivalent employees**. Your business is eligible for CCSB if:

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Employee count | At least one but no more than 100 eligible employees, meeting certain contribution and participation requirements |
| Location | Your principal business address is in California, or you offer health insurance to each eligible employee at that employee's primary worksite in California |
| Offer | You offer CCSB insurance to **all eligible employees** |
| W-2 employee | At least one employee who receives a W-2 who is **not the business owner or spouse of the owner** |
| Worksite | The **majority of your eligible employees** are employed in California |
| Contribution | You must contribute **at least 50 percent toward each employee's premium** |

### The employer mandate

Businesses with **50 or more** full-time-equivalent employees **may be required by law** to offer health insurance that meets federal standards, or face a **payment to the IRS**. CCSB can help applicable employers meet this requirement. If your business has **fewer than 50 employees**, you are generally **not required** to offer insurance.

### Federal tax credit for small businesses

Some small businesses that offer insurance through CCSB qualify for a **federal tax credit worth up to 50 percent of their premium contributions** — **available only through Covered California for Small Business**. The credit is based on your **business size, average employee wages, and how much you contribute** toward premiums. **Not all businesses qualify** — eligibility is determined by the IRS.

The cost of coverage depends on factors like the **size and location of your business, the ages of your employees, and the plans you select**. By offering a selection of coverage levels, employers can control their contributions while allowing employees to pick plans that fit their budgets. Smaller businesses with lower wages can qualify for bigger credits.

### When insurance starts

Employers can enroll in health insurance for their employees **at any time of the year**. To start insurance in a given month, submit your completed application **within the first five business days of that month**. Otherwise, insurance begins **the following month**.

### Not the right fit?

If you're **self-employed with no W-2 employees**, individual insurance through Covered California may be a better option — and you could qualify for financial help to lower your monthly premium.
