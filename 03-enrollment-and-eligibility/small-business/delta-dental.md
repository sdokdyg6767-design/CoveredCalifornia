## Summary

Delta Dental of California is the dental carrier offered through CCSB, and is part of the Delta Dental Plans Association, one of the largest dental insurance providers in the nation. It offers HMO and PPO dental plans covering either children only or the full family. Employees select their own dental plan through the CCSB application, and employer contribution toward dental premiums is optional.

## Key Facts

- Delta Dental of California is part of the **Delta Dental Plans Association** — one of the largest dental insurance providers in the nation.
- Four plan options are published for **2027**: Children's Dental HMO, Family Dental HMO, PPO Children's Dental, PPO Family Dental.
- **2026** and **2025** documents are also published as separate PDFs for each of the four designs.
- Plan formats: **HMO and PPO**; coverage scope: **children only or the full family**.
- CCSB's 2027 Dental Benefits, Limitations, and Exclusions document applies to all Delta Dental plans.

### Plan documents

Plan documents include the **Summary of Benefits and Coverage**, disclosure form, and **Evidence of Coverage**. The CCSB **Dental Benefits, Limitations, and Exclusions** document for 2027, 2026, and 2025 shows what dental services are covered and what is restricted or excluded.

### Notes for employers

- Dental is a **separate decision** from health coverage in CCSB and can be offered whether or not you change your health plan selection.
- **Employer contribution toward dental premiums is optional.** If you contribute, you must select a **dental reference plan**, which determines how your contribution is calculated.
- **HMO** dental plans typically have lower premiums with a network of assigned dentists; **PPO** plans offer more provider flexibility.
- **Children's Dental** covers dependents **up to age 19**; **Family Dental** covers eligible dependents **up to age 26**, including children who have aged out of Children's Dental coverage.
- Eligible employees and dependents may add a dental plan **during the plan year** after a major life change — such as losing other dental coverage or a dependent child aging out of Children's Dental coverage — or during their annual open enrollment period.
