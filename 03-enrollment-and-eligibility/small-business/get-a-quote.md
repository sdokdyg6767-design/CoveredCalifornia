### What related CCSB pages state about quotes

From the **Covered California for Small Business** landing page (`/small-business`):

- A quote request is free. "Submit a quote request and our team will follow up **within two business days** with plan options and pricing based on your business needs — **at no cost to you**."
- If you need help, small business owners are encouraged to contact Covered California for Small Business or a licensed insurance agent to get a **customized quote** tailored to their specific situation.
- The easiest way to enroll is to contact the sales team for a quote and get free help choosing the best health and dental plan for your business needs.

From **Financial Help for Employers** (`/small-business/costs-and-financial-help`):

- A quote is the route to a **personalized cost estimate**.
- Employers can control costs by setting a contribution level **tied to a reference plan**.

From **How to Apply** (`/small-business/apply`):

- After confirming your business meets the eligibility criteria, you **get a quote** to see your insurance options and costs.
- Share basic information about your business and a **CCSB representative will reach out to gather more details** and build a personalized quote.
- You can still review and change your selection: "Once you review your quote and choose your health insurance, our support team will work closely with you to finalize the enrollment process."

### Information likely requested during quoting

Based on the eligibility and application pages, a quote conversation typically draws on:

- Employer name as reported to the California Employment Development Department
- Federal Employer Identification Number (FEIN)
- Local business license
- DE-9C
- Total number of full-time employees
- Total number of part-time employees working 20 to 29 hours per week
- Employee roster with addresses, hire dates, dates of birth, and Social Security or tax identification numbers
- Dependent information, including dates of birth
