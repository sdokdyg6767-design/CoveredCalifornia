# Eligibility Guidelines — Covered California for Small Business

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/small-business/eligibility
- **Covered California section:** For Small Business — Eligibility
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page sets out the four basic requirements for participating in Covered California for Small Business, explains how a full-time-equivalent employee count is calculated, and identifies which employees can be offered coverage based on weekly hours. It closes with a checklist of documents to gather before applying.

## Key Facts

- You must have **100 or fewer full-time-equivalent employees**.
- You must have **at least one employee who receives a W-2** — this cannot be the owner or the owner's spouse.
- The **majority of your eligible employees** must live or work in California.
- You must offer health insurance to **all eligible employees** and meet contribution and participation requirements.
- Businesses with **more than 100** full-time-equivalent employees are **not eligible**; contact an insurance agent or broker for large-group options.
- Employee eligibility by hours: **30 or more hours/week** — eligible for health insurance; **20-29 hours/week** — may be offered health insurance at the employer's discretion; **fewer than 20 hours/week** — **not eligible** through CCSB.
- Calculating your FTE count is **your responsibility** as an employer.
- Tax-credit eligibility is **separate** from general program eligibility.

## Details

### Is your business eligible?

CCSB connects employers with health and dental insurance for their employees. To participate, your business must meet a few basic requirements:

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Size | **100 or fewer full-time-equivalent employees** |
| W-2 employee | At least one employee who receives a W-2 — this cannot be the owner or the owner's spouse |
| Location | The **majority of your eligible employees** live or work in California |
| Offer | You offer health insurance to **all eligible employees** and meet contribution and participation requirements |

Tax credits are available to some qualifying small businesses, but **tax-credit eligibility is separate from general program eligibility**.

### What is a full-time-equivalent employee?

A full-time-equivalent (FTE) employee is not a single person — it is a **calculation**. To determine your business size for eligibility purposes, you must count **all full-time and part-time employees** who worked during the **previous calendar year** (or who are reasonably expected to work in the current year if your business is new).

Calculating your FTE count is **your responsibility** as an employer. An FTE calculation tool on HealthCare.gov is available if you need help estimating your count.

### Which employees can be offered health insurance?

| Weekly hours | Eligibility |
| --- | --- |
| **30 or more hours per week** | Eligible for health insurance |
| **20-29 hours per week** | May be offered health insurance at the employer's **discretion** |
| **Fewer than 20 hours per week** | **Not eligible** through CCSB |

### Common questions

**What if I'm a sole proprietor?** If you're a sole proprietor with no W-2 receiving employees, your business may not qualify for Covered California for Small Business. You may be eligible to buy insurance through Covered California's individual marketplace instead. Financial help may be available if you qualify.

**What if my business has more than 100 full-time-equivalent employees?** Businesses with more than 100 full-time-equivalent employees are not eligible for Covered California for Small Business. Contact your insurance agent or broker to explore large-group insurance options.
