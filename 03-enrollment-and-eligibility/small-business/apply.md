# How to Apply — Covered California for Small Business

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/small-business/apply
- **Covered California section:** For Small Business — How to Apply
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page walks through applying for small business health and dental insurance with CCSB. It lists what information to gather, the mailing and email addresses for paper applications, and states the timing rules: apply year-round, submit within the first five business days of a month to start coverage that month, and expect a response within two weeks. Free help is available from the CCSB support team and from licensed insurance agents.

## Key Facts

- Businesses can apply **at any time of the year**.
- To start insurance in a given month, the completed application must be submitted **within the first five business days of that month**; otherwise coverage begins **the following month**.
- After you submit, CCSB will review your materials and contact you **within two weeks** about eligibility and next steps.
- **Mail to:** Covered California for Small Business, P.O. Box 7010, Newport Beach, CA 92658.
- **Email to:** ccsbeligibility@covered.ca.gov
- An Enrollment Application (PDF) is available to download, complete, and submit by mail or email.
- Help from a licensed insurance agent is **free**.

## Details

### Steps to apply

1. **Check your eligibility.** Your business generally needs 100 or fewer full-time-equivalent employees, at least one W-2 employee, and a majority of eligible employees living in California.
2. **Get a quote.** Share basic information about your business and a CCSB representative will reach out to gather more details and build a personalized quote.
3. **Complete your enrollment.** Once you review your quote and choose your health insurance, the CCSB support team will work with you to finalize enrollment.

### What you need to apply

Gathering this information before you start can help speed up your application. You'll typically need:

| Item | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Employer name | As reported to the **California Employment Development Department** |
| Federal Employer Identification Number (FEIN) | — |
| Local business license | A copy is fine |
| DE-9C | Reconciled by the employer |
| Total number of full-time employees | — |
| Total number of part-time employees working 20 to 29 hours per week | If you plan to offer them insurance |
| Employee roster | Including addresses, hire dates, dates of birth, and Social Security numbers or tax identification numbers |
| Dependent information | Including dates of birth, if offering dependent insurance |

### Other ways to apply

Download the **Enrollment Application (PDF)**, complete it, and submit by mail or email — or get free help from a local agent.

**By mail.** Mail your completed employer application, employee applications, and required documents to:

```
Covered California for Small Business
P.O. Box 7010
Newport Beach, CA 92658
```

**By email.** Send your completed employer application, employee applications, and required documents to **ccsbeligibility@covered.ca.gov**.

**With help from an agent.** Find a local agent to get free help with your application and next steps.

### Timing

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| When you can apply | **Any time of the year** |
| Deadline to start coverage in a given month | Completed application must be submitted **within the first five business days of that month** |
| If you miss that deadline | Coverage begins **the following month** |
| Response time | CCSB contacts you **within two weeks** about eligibility and next steps |

### Support

The Covered California for Small Business support team can help you collect information, answer your questions, and guide you through every step of the enrollment process — including creating a personalized quote before you commit to a plan. Licensed insurance agents can also help you understand your options and complete the enrollment process **at no cost to you**.
