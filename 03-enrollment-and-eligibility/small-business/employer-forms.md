# Applications and Forms for Employers — Covered California for Small Business

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/small-business/employer-forms
- **Covered California section:** For Small Business — For Employers
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page collects the guides, applications, deadline documents, and change forms employers use to enroll and manage a CCSB group. It states the late-submission rule for new business — CCSB accepts submissions through the first five business days of a month for that same month's insurance only if the New Business Late Submission Acknowledgement is signed and returned. Support is available Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.

## Key Facts

- CCSB accepts new business submissions **through the first five business days of the month** for that same month's insurance — **provided** the New Business Late Submission Acknowledgement (PDF) is signed and submitted with the enrollment application.
- If the group does not agree to the submission acknowledgement, the effective date is **the first of the month following the requested effective date**.
- Submission deadline documents are published for **2026** and **2025**.
- Employer applications and change forms are available in **English and Spanish**.
- The Employer Change Form covers changes to the group's **reference plan, coverage levels and contribution levels**, or the **address or ownership** of the business.
- Support hours: **Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.**
- **Email:** ccsbeligibility@covered.ca.gov
- **Mail:** Covered California for Small Business, P.O. Box 7010, Newport Beach, CA 92658
