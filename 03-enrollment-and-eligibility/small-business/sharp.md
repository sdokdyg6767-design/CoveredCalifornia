## Summary

Sharp Health Plan is a not-for-profit insurer available through CCSB, but only to **employers located in San Diego County**. It has served San Diego and southern Riverside counties since 1992 and is a subsidiary of Sharp HealthCare. Plans are offered under two networks — Performance and Premier — across Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum tiers, all with embedded children's dental insurance.

## Key Facts

- Sharp is a **not-for-profit** health insurance company that has served **San Diego and southern Riverside counties since 1992**.
- It is a subsidiary of **Sharp HealthCare**, the largest provider of comprehensive health care services in San Diego.
- **Sharp is available to employers located in San Diego County** only.
- **All Sharp health plans include embedded children's dental insurance** — children's dental benefits are built into the health plan at **no separate cost**.
- Plan networks: **Performance** and **Premier**.
- CMS quality ratings shown for **2026**: HMO plans rated **4 out of 5 stars** and **5 out of 5 stars**.

Ratings are on a scale of 1 to 5 stars, where **5 stars** indicates the plan scored among the top health insurance companies nationwide. Ratings are reported after a plan's **first two years** with Covered California. Ratings are calculated by CMS using data provided by health plans in **2025** and are displayed for health plans for the **2027 plan year**.
