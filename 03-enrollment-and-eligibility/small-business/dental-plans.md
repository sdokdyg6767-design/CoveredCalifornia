## Details

### How dental coverage works through CCSB

Dental insurance through CCSB is a **separate decision** from health insurance — and a valuable one. When you offer dental, your employees can choose a dental insurance plan for **themselves and their dependents directly through the CCSB application**. Dental plans are available alongside health plan options but are **evaluated and selected independently**. You can offer dental insurance whether or not you change your health plan selection.

If you choose to offer dental, you'll need to select a **reference plan** — the plan used to calculate your contribution toward employee premiums. Employer contribution toward dental premiums is **optional and not required** for coverage.

### Plan types: HMO and PPO

CCSB offers Delta Dental of California dental plans, with HMO and PPO options available for employees and their dependents. Delta Dental provides access to a broad network of dentists focused on preventive care and oral health.

| Plan format | Characteristics |
| --- | --- |
| **HMO** | Typically **lower premiums**, with a **network of assigned dentists** |
| **PPO** | **More provider flexibility** |

Dental plans are available in HMO and PPO formats, with options for **children only** or the **full family**. Review plan documents to understand what dental services are covered, what restrictions apply, and what services are not included.

### Employee and dependent choice

| Plan | Who it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Children's Dental** | Dependent children **up to age 19** |
| **Family Dental** | Eligible dependents **up to age 26**, including children who have aged out of Children's Dental coverage |

Employees can select a dental plan for themselves and their dependents directly through the CCSB application.

### Employer contribution

Employer contribution toward dental premiums is **optional** — you are not required to contribute to your employees' dental costs. If you choose to contribute, you must select a **dental reference plan**, which determines how your contribution amount is calculated.

### When dependents can be added

Eligible employees and dependents may add a dental plan during the plan year if they experience a **major life change** — such as losing other dental coverage, or a dependent child **aging out of Children's Dental coverage** — or during their **annual open enrollment period**.
