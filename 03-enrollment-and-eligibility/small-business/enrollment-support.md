## Key Facts

- Page title confirmed as **"Enrollment support | Covered California for Small Business"**.
- CCSB support is available **Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.**
- Licensed insurance agents provide enrollment help **at no cost** to the employer.
- After you submit an application, CCSB will contact you **within two weeks** about eligibility and next steps.
- Applications submitted within the **first five business days of a month** start coverage that month, provided the New Business Late Submission Acknowledgement is signed.
