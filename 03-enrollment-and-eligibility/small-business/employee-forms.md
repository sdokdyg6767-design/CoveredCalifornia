# Applications and Forms for Employees — Covered California for Small Business

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/small-business/employee-forms
- **Covered California section:** For Small Business — For Employees
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page collects the forms employees use to enroll in, change, or manage CCSB coverage. A combined enrollment and change request form is available, with separate enrollment and change forms also accepted, in English and Spanish for the 2027 and 2026 plan years. It also covers COBRA election and cancellation forms and explains how to get a member ID card.

## Key Facts

- A **combined enrollment and change request form** allows a new enrollment request and changes to existing insurance on **one form**.
- **Separate enrollment and change forms are also accepted.**
- Forms are published for **2027** (English) and **2026** (Spanish), with **2025** forms also referenced.
- Four COBRA documents are published: Election Form, Cancellation Form, Rights Document, and a Sample COBRA Termination Notice.
- Employees who lose group coverage due to losing a job, reduced hours, or leaving the company may be able to continue coverage temporarily through **COBRA**.

The combined form lets you submit a new enrollment request or make changes to your existing insurance on one form. Separate enrollment and change forms are also accepted.

### COBRA forms

If you lose group health insurance coverage — for example, due to losing your job, reduced hours, or leaving the company — COBRA may allow you to continue that coverage temporarily.

| Document | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **COBRA Election Form (PDF)** | Elect COBRA continuation coverage |
| **COBRA Cancellation Form (PDF)** | Cancel COBRA coverage |
| **COBRA Rights Document (PDF)** | Understand your COBRA rights |
| **Sample COBRA Termination Notice (PDF)** | Reference template |

For more information, visit the Department of Labor's COBRA Continuation Coverage page.

### Member ID card

People who have a health or dental plan through CCSB can find insurance company-specific steps on "how to access or order a member ID card (PDF)". This is most helpful when someone needs proof of insurance for an appointment, pharmacy visit, or a mailed replacement card.
