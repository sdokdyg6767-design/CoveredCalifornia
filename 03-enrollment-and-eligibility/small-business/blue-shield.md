## Summary

Blue Shield of California is a California-based not-for-profit health insurer offering CCSB plans with **statewide coverage** across HMO, PPO, and HDHP formats and all four metal tiers. All Blue Shield plans include **embedded children's dental insurance**, so children's dental benefits are built into the health plan at no separate cost. The page publishes Summary of Benefits and Coverage and Evidence of Coverage documents for 2027, 2026, and 2025, plus group service agreements.

## Key Facts

- Blue Shield of California is a **California-based not-for-profit** health insurance company.
- Plans offer **statewide coverage** across multiple plan types and coverage levels.
- **All Blue Shield health plans include embedded children's dental insurance** — children's dental benefits are built into the health plan at **no separate cost**.
- Available plans **vary by region**.
- Plan families offered: **Access+**, **Trio**, and standard **Bronze / Silver / Gold / Platinum** HMO, PPO, and HDHP lines.
- CMS quality ratings shown for **2026**: one plan rated **3 out of 5 stars**, one plan rated **4 out of 5 stars**.
- Ratings are on a scale of 1 to 5 stars; 5 stars indicates the plan scored among the top health insurance companies nationwide. Ratings are calculated by CMS using 2025 data provided by health plans and are displayed for the **2027 plan year**.
