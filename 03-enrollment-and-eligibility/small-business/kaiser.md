## Summary

Kaiser Permanente offers CCSB HMO plans built around an integrated care model, with hospitals, care, and pharmacy in one place. Plans are offered across Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Platinum tiers, with HDHP variants in Bronze, Silver, and Gold. All plans include embedded children's dental coverage. The page publishes Summary of Benefits and Coverage and Evidence of Coverage for 2027, 2026, and 2025.

## Key Facts

- Kaiser Permanente is a **nonprofit, group-practice** health insurance company headquartered in **Oakland, California**.
- It is composed of **Kaiser Foundation Health Plans** (nonprofit, public-benefit corporations), **Kaiser Foundation Hospitals** (a nonprofit, public-benefit corporation), and the **Permanente Medical Groups** (for-profit professional organizations).
- Plan designs are **HMO**, including HDHP variants.
- All listed plans include **+ Child Dental** (embedded children's dental).
- CMS quality ratings shown for **2026**: HMO plans rated **5 out of 5 stars** and **4 out of 5 stars**.
- Ratings calculated by CMS using **2025** data, displayed for the **2027 plan year**.
