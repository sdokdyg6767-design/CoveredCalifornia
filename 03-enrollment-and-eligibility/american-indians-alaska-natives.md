# Coverage for American Indians and Alaska Natives

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment/eligibility/american-indians-alaska-natives
- **Covered California section:** Eligibility & Enrollment — Specific Populations — American Indians and Alaska Natives
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

American Indians and Alaska Natives (AI/AN) can qualify for two special cost-sharing structures in Covered California plans — a zero cost-sharing plan and a limited cost-sharing plan — based on household income and federal poverty level. Members of federally recognized tribes can enroll or change plans at any time rather than waiting for open enrollment, and are exempt from the state penalty for not having insurance. Some of these benefits require tribal membership, while others extend to anyone eligible for services through the Indian Health Service, a tribal program, or an urban Indian health program.

## Key Facts

- **Zero cost-sharing plan:** available when household income is between **100 and 300 percent of the FPL**. No copays, coinsurance, or deductibles for care covered by the plan.
- **Limited cost-sharing plan:** available at **any household income**. No copays, coinsurance, or deductibles when getting care through an Indian health care provider, or for care received through a referral from one.
- **Medi-Cal:** if your income qualifies, you may be enrolled with no premiums or out-of-pocket costs.
- As an AI/AN you can enroll in a Covered California plan **at any time** — you do not have to wait for open enrollment.
- Choose **"Federally recognized American Indian/Alaska Native"** as your major life change.
- You can change your health plan **once each month**.
- Your insurance starts on the **first day of the month after** you choose or change your plan.
- Members of federally recognized tribes **do not have to pay the penalty** for not having health insurance, which is required by California law.

## Details

### Who these benefits are for

| Group | Eligibility basis |
| --- | --- |
| Members of federally recognized tribes | Tribal membership |
| Alaska Natives | Alaska Native status |
| Anyone eligible for services through the Indian Health Service (IHS), a tribal program, or an urban Indian health program | Service eligibility, even if you are not a tribal member |

Some benefits are **only** for members of federally recognized tribes. Others apply to anyone eligible for services through the IHS, a tribal program, or an urban Indian health program, even if you are not a tribal member.

### What you may qualify for

Covered California uses your household size and income to see what help you may qualify for. These benefits are based on the **federal poverty level (FPL)**.

| Benefit | Income rule | What you pay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zero cost-sharing plan | Household income between 100 and 300 percent of FPL | No copays, coinsurance, or deductibles for health care covered by the plan |
| Limited cost-sharing plan | Any household income | No copays, coinsurance, or deductibles when getting care through an Indian health care provider, or for care received through a referral from one |
| Medi-Cal | If your income qualifies you for Medi-Cal | No premiums or out-of-pocket costs |

### Enroll or change plans anytime

As an AI/AN you do not have to wait for open enrollment; you can enroll in a Covered California plan at any time. Choose **"Federally recognized American Indian/Alaska Native"** as your major life change. You can change your health plan **once each month**, and your insurance starts on the **first day of the month after** you choose or change your plan.

### Penalty exemption

You may qualify for a penalty exemption. Members of federally recognized tribes do not have to pay the penalty for not having health insurance, which is required by California law. See "Penalty Details and Exemptions" for more information.

### What you may need to apply

| What | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Proof of tribal membership or Alaska Native status | Such as a tribal enrollment card, or a letter from your tribe or the Bureau of Indian Affairs |
| Estimated yearly household income | Needed for the FPL-based benefit test |
| Social Security number | If you have one |
| Household information | Information about each person in your household who needs insurance |

### Common questions

**Can I still get zero out-of-pocket costs if I am not a tribal member?**
Yes. You may still qualify for no out-of-pocket costs for services you get through the Indian Health Service (IHS), tribal programs, or urban Indian health programs if you are eligible for services from those providers. You may also qualify for Medi-Cal based on your income and other eligibility rules.

**Can my whole household be in an AI/AN plan?**
Your household may need to be split into different plan groups. If AI/AN and non-AI/AN members stay in the same mixed enrollment group, that group cannot choose an AI/AN zero cost-sharing plan or an AI/AN limited cost-sharing plan. The household can instead choose a standard plan together, with financial help if eligible.

**Does enrolling with Covered California stop me from using the IHS?**
No. Enrolling in a Covered California plan does not prevent you from continuing to get services through the IHS, tribal health programs, or urban Indian health programs. In a limited cost-sharing plan, you do not pay out of pocket for outside care if you have a referral from an Indian health care provider. Without that referral, your out-of-pocket costs are based on the coverage level you chose.
