# Proof of Non-Incarcerated Status

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment/documents/not-incarcerated
- **Covered California section:** Eligibility & Enrollment — Documents — Proof of Non-Incarcerated Status
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page lists the proof accepted to confirm you are not incarcerated: official release papers, parole documents, a downloadable Non-Incarceration Attestation Form, or a self-written attestation letter with prescribed language. Importantly, the page states that Covered California is not currently asking members for this proof, so anyone who receives such a notice should call first.

## Key Facts

- **Covered California is not currently asking members to provide proof of non-incarceration status.**
- If you received a notice requesting this document, contact **(800) 300-1506** before submitting anything.
- The Non-Incarceration Attestation Form has four required sections: case number, your printed name, your signature, and today's date.
- A written attestation letter must include two prescribed statements and be declared **under the penalty of perjury, under the laws of the State of California**.

## Details

### Current status

> **Note:** Covered California is **not currently asking members to provide proof of non-incarceration status**. If you received a notice requesting this document, contact us at **(800) 300-1506** before submitting anything.

### Documents you can submit

You can send a copy of one of the following documents as proof that you are not incarcerated.

| Document | Requirements |
| --- | --- |
| **Official release papers** | Documentation from the institution or Department of Corrections confirming your release |
| **Parole documents** | Parole papers confirming your current status |
| **Non-Incarceration Attestation Form** | Download the Non-Incarceration Attestation Form (PDF) and fill out four sections: case number, your printed name, your signature, and today's date |
| **Written attestation letter** | You may write your own letter instead of using the form; see requirements below |

### Written attestation letter requirements

The letter must include:

- Your **full name and address** — the same address on your enrollment application
- Your **case number**, if available
- The following statements, verbatim:

> "I, [your full name] am not incarcerated (in jail, prison, correctional facility, or similar penal institution) for the entire period during which I am seeking coverage through Covered California."

> "I declare under the penalty of perjury, under the laws of the State of California, that what I stated in this letter is true and correct."

- Your **signature and today's date**

### How to submit

Once complete, submit your form or letter by uploading it to your online account, or by fax or mail using the contact information on the form.
