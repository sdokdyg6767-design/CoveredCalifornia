# American Indian or Alaska Native Documentation

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment/documents/american-indian-or-alaskan-native
- **Covered California section:** Eligibility & Enrollment — Documents — American Indian or Alaska Native Documentation
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page lists the documents Covered California accepts to confirm American Indian or Alaska Native status. You submit a copy of one accepted document, which can be a tribal enrollment or membership record, an Alaska Native shareholder document, or one of several other accepted records including a CDIB, an I-872 American Indian Card, or an HHS tribal exemption letter.

## Key Facts

- Submit a copy of **one** accepted document.
- Tribal enrollment or membership documents must be **on tribal letterhead**, or an enrollment or membership card containing the **tribal seal and/or an official signature**.
- A **CDIB** is accepted only if issued by the Bureau of Indian Affairs or a tribe **and** it includes tribal enrollment information.
- The **I-872 American Indian Card** applies to Texas and Oklahoma Kickapoo American and Mexican members.
- An HHS letter granting a **tribal exemption** based on tribal membership or Alaska Native shareholder status is accepted.

## Details

### Documents you can submit

Submit a copy of **one** of the following.

| Category | Accepted documents |
| --- | --- |
| **Tribal enrollment or membership documents** | Tribal enrollment or membership card; enrollment or membership document from a **federally recognized tribe** or the **Bureau of Indian Affairs**. Must be on tribal letterhead, or an enrollment or membership card that contains the **tribal seal and/or an official signature** |
| **Alaska Native shareholder documents** | Document acknowledging shareholder status, issued by an **Alaska Native village or tribe**, or an **Alaska Native Corporation Settlement Act (ANCSA)** regional or village corporation |
| **Other accepted documents** | **I-872 American Indian Card** (Texas and Oklahoma Kickapoo American and Mexican members); **Certificate of Degree of Indian Blood (CDIB)** issued by the Bureau of Indian Affairs or a tribe, if the CDIB includes tribal enrollment information; **Letter from the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS)** granting a tribal exemption based on tribal membership or Alaska Native shareholder status |

### How to submit

Follow the instructions in your Covered California account or eligibility notice to submit your document online, by fax, or by mail. Submit copies only.
