# Marketing and Branding

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment-partners-individuals/marketing-branding-policies
- **Covered California section:** For Enrollment Partners — Individuals & Families — Marketing and Branding
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page sets the rules for how licensed insurance agents and community enrollment partners may use Covered California trademarks and logos. The partner logo signals certification and may only be used with a signed agreement. It specifies website domain-name prohibitions, a mandatory disclaimer for sites using Covered California logos, and how to join the free Storefront Program and submit events.

## Key Facts

- The partner logo signals that you have met Covered California certification requirements. It **may only be used if you have a signed agreement** — an **Agent Agreement** for licensed insurance agents, or a **Community Enrollment Partners Agreement** for community enrollment partners.
- **You may not use "Covered CA," "Covered California," or "California Health Benefit Exchange" as any part of your domain name.**
- If you use Covered California logos on your website, you **must** include the required disclaimer on your **homepage** and on any **"Who We Are"** or **"About Us"** pages.
- The **Storefront Program participation is free**.
- Storefront questions: **storefront@covered.ca.gov**.
- Agent branding questions: **agents@covered.ca.gov** or **(877) 453-9198**. Community partner branding questions: **branding@covered.ca.gov**.

## Details

### Co-branding your marketing materials

Covered California licensed insurance agents and community enrollment partners are **encouraged to promote Covered California** by co-branding consumer-facing marketing materials with the Covered California, licensed insurance agent, or Certified Enrollment Counselor logos.

You can also reproduce and distribute print-ready collateral from the **Printable Materials** page.

**Before developing any materials**, download the logos and follow the brand guidelines on the **Logos and Brand Style Guide** page. Your logo signals to consumers that you have met Covered California certification requirements.

| Requirement | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Signed agreement required** | **Agent Agreement** for licensed insurance agents; **Community Enrollment Partners Agreement** for community enrollment partners |
| **Trademark use** | Covered California's trademark and logos **must be used appropriately** while advertising in the marketplace |

### Adding logos to your website

You can add a Covered California logo or certified partner badge to your website. Download the files from the logo resources page and follow the **Brand Style Guide** when placing them.

**Domain name restrictions.** You may **not** use "Covered CA," "Covered California," or "California Health Benefit Exchange" as any part of your domain name.

**Required website disclaimer.** If you use Covered California logos on your website, include the following disclaimer on your **homepage** and on any **"Who We Are"** or **"About Us"** pages:

> "This website is owned and maintained by **[Your Business or Entity Name]**, which is solely responsible for its content. This site is not maintained by or affiliated with Covered California, and Covered California bears no responsibility for its content. The email addresses and telephone number that appear throughout this site belong to **[Your Business or Entity Name]** and cannot be used to contact Covered California."

### Storefronts

The Covered California **Storefront Program** helps licensed insurance agents and community enrollment partners increase their visibility by opening a **physical location** in their community where people can get **free, confidential, in-person enrollment help**. **Participation is free.**

| Item | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Consumer search** | Consumers can search for storefronts by **ZIP code, hours of operation, and languages spoken** using the **Find an Enroller** tool |
| **Eligibility and how to apply** | Download the **Storefront Toolkit (PDF)** |
| **Questions** | Email **storefront@covered.ca.gov** |

### Events

Events give people the opportunity to get **free enrollment help** from licensed insurance agents and community enrollment partners in their area.

| Task | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **View upcoming events** | Browse the **Covered California events calendar** to see approved events near you |
| **Submit a new event** | Use the **event submission form** to add your event |
