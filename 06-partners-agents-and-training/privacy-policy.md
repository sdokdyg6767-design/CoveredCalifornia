### Verified privacy obligations for partners

The partner-facing privacy requirements that are retrievable appear on the **Training and Education** page at
https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment-partners-individuals/training-and-education (see `individual-training-and-education.md`):

| Obligation | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **PII security** | Personally Identifiable Information (PII) **must remain secure and confidential** in accordance with Covered California's security and privacy policies, including all applicable laws and regulations |
| **Partner responsibility** | It is the **responsibility of certified partners** to ensure that they handle this information appropriately |
| **Email security** | **Emails with consumer PII must be encrypted or secured** |
| **Encryption guidance** | A resource titled **"Using 7-Zip for Encryption (PDF)"** is published |
| **Policy document** | A resource titled **"Privacy and Security (PDF)"** is published |
| **Delegation** | Consumers may **delegate their application** to an authorized representative, covering filling out and submitting the application, making changes to cases, terminating cases, renewing plans, and selecting a plan |
| **LMS course** | **Privacy, Security and Compliance** is one of seven named LMS certification course topics |

### Where to go for more

- **Training and Education** for the Privacy and Security policy PDF and encryption guidance.
- **Marketing and Branding** for rules on displaying contact information on your own website. Note the required disclaimer states that the email addresses and telephone number appearing on a partner site **cannot be used to contact Covered California**.
- The Agent Code of Conduct for application data-integrity rules: agents may not enter their own email address, telephone number, or mailing address on a consumer application unless it is the consumer's actual information.
