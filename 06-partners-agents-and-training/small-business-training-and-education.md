# Training and Education for CCSB Agents

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment-partners-small-business/training-and-education
- **Covered California section:** For Enrollment Partners — Small Business — Training and Education
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page is the resource desk for CCSB-certified agents, covering orientation videos, plan and dental plan information, a named list of general agent partners, marketing and branding collateral, quoting tools, and a library of orientation and sales videos in English and Spanish. It also routes agents to the Small Business sales team.

## Key Facts

- Small Business sales team: **(844) 332-8384**.
- **Nine general agent partners** are listed: Beere & Purves; CRC Benefits; Claremont; Dickerson Insurance Services; AMWINS Benefits; Emerson Rogers; Warner Pacific.
- Marketing assets include a **Tri-Fold Brochure (PDF)**, **Sales Overview Flyer (PDF)**, and **CCSB Program Presentation (PDF)**.
- Quoting tools include a **CCSB Quoting Template (.xlsx)** and a **Quoting for Small Business online form**.
- **Nine orientation videos** are listed; three titles are available in both **English and Spanish**.
- Marketing materials can be ordered from the **Covered California Print Store** and **Covered California Online Store**.
