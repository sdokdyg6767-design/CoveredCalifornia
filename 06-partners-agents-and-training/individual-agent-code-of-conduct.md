# Agent Code of Conduct

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment-partners-individuals/become-an-agent/code-of-conduct
- **Covered California section:** For Enrollment Partners — Individuals & Families — Agent Code of Conduct
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

The Agent Code of Conduct sets twelve binding rules for all Covered California licensed insurance agencies and agents, covering ethical sales, prohibition of consumer fees including translation fees, plan steering, Health Care Sharing Ministry (HCSM) sales, supplemental product sales, treatment of consumers, and data integrity. Failure to comply is a breach of the Agency or Non-Monetary Agent Agreement and may result in termination of the Agreement and revocation of certification. Document revision is **Rev. 3/2025**.

## Key Facts

- Document revision: **Rev. 3/2025**.
- Failure to comply constitutes a **breach of the Agency or Non-Monetary Agent Agreement** and may result in **termination of the Agreement and revocation of certification** pursuant to **Section E of Exhibit C**.
- Agencies and agents **may not charge any fee** to consumers for performing any service under either Agreement.
- Agencies and agents **may not charge consumers translation fees** — these services must be provided **free of charge**.
- HCSM consumers must sign a **Health Care Sharing Ministry Consumer Acknowledgement Form**; executed forms must be retained for a **minimum of three (3) years** and produced to Covered California upon request.
- Authorized Representative designations require **express written consumer consent** per **10 CCR § 6508(b)**.
- Agents may **not enter their own email address, telephone number, or mailing address** on an application unless it is the consumer's actual address or number.
- Questions: **AgentContracts@covered.ca.gov**.
- Covered California **reserves the right to modify or amend this Code of Conduct at any time and without prior notice**.

## Details

### Scope and enforcement

Covered California contracts with licensed insurance agencies and agents to connect Californians to the health insurance plan that meets their needs. To maintain certification with Covered California, all agencies and agents must comply with the Agent Code of Conduct, which includes rules on the **ethical sales of insurance plans** through Covered California.

> **Note:** Failure to comply with these rules constitutes a **breach of the Agency or Non-Monetary Agent Agreement** and may result in **termination of the Agreement and revocation of certification** pursuant to **Section E of Exhibit C**.

### The twelve rules

| # | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **1** | Promote Covered California health plans **fairly and accurately** to consumers. |
| **2** | **Will not charge any fee** to consumers for performing any service under the Agency Agreement or Non-Monetary Agent Agreement. |
| **3** | **Will not charge consumers translation fees** for services offered in connection with a Covered California enrollment. These services must be provided **free of charge**. |
| **4** | Assist consumers with finding the health plan that best meets their needs. Recommendations must be based on the **consumer's best interests**, and must not be influenced by **financial compensation and incentives from health issuers**. |
| **5** | Only sell **Health Care Sharing Ministry (HCSM)** products per the Agency Agreement and this Code of Conduct. See HCSM procedures below. |
| **6** | Must not mislead consumers into purchasing **supplemental products as a requirement** for a Covered California plan. Consumers must be told the purchase of supplemental products is **optional and not required**. |
| **7** | Must not **unduly influence** consumers to enroll in lower-premium plans when it is not in the consumer's best interest. Agents **cannot steer** consumers into lower-premium plans to facilitate supplemental product sales. |
| **8** | Must not **condition enrollment** in Covered California plans on enrollment in unrelated products. Agents must assist with Covered California enrollment **even when the consumer does not want to purchase a supplemental product**. |
| **9** | Must not engage in **abusive, intimidating, threatening, or any other detrimental behavior** toward Covered California consumers, employees, qualified health plan issuers, contractors, or other representatives acting on Covered California's behalf. |
| **10** | Covered California-Approved Admin Staff may be designated as **Authorized Representatives only for consumers who have expressly consented in writing**, as required by **10 CCR § 6508(b)**. Admin Staff may only perform duties as authorized in the Agency Agreement and **may not enroll consumers** into Covered California plans while acting on behalf of the agency or agent. |
| **11** | **American Rescue Plan exception** — see below. |
| **12** | Must collect and provide **accurate applicant information** on each application. See data integrity rules below. |

### Rule 5 in detail: Health Care Sharing Ministry (HCSM) procedures

For each consumer who signs up for an HCSM product, agency and agent must:

1. Perform an **eligibility determination** as outlined in the Agency Agreement for any consumer interested in purchasing an HCSM product. **Before enrolling** a consumer in an HCSM product, share the **full results of the eligibility determination** with the consumer and explain the **benefits of enrolling in a Covered California plan**. Disclose **all financial assistance** the consumer may be eligible to receive and **all insurance affordability programs** they may be eligible to enroll in, such as **Medi-Cal**.
2. Require consumers to complete a **Health Care Sharing Ministry Consumer Acknowledgement Form** before enrolling them into an HCSM product. The agency must maintain hard or electronic copies of executed forms for a **minimum of three (3) years** and make them available to Covered California **upon request**.
3. Ensure its agents complete an **attestation** to confirm compliance with this section while completing any **annual training requirements**.
4. If requested by Covered California, disclose the **total number of HCSM products sold in the previous plan year**.

### Rule 11 in detail: American Rescue Plan migration exception

When migrating an **off-exchange** consumer to a Covered California qualified health plan (QHP) in order to maximize the premium subsidy under the American Rescue Plan, agency and agent are **not required to present all available enrollment options** to the consumer.

- This limited exception applies **only** to enrollments from off-exchange to Covered California when a consumer can take advantage of **enhanced subsidies** under the American Rescue Plan.
- Agency and agent **must present all enrollment options** if specifically requested by the consumer during the off-exchange transition, **and for all other enrollments**.
- Agency and agent **must track** all off-exchange to Covered California enrollments. Covered California **may request information** related to those enrollments **at any time**.

### Rule 12 in detail: data integrity requirements

| Field | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Household income projection** | The **consumer or their authorized representative must confirm the accuracy** of the household income projection before submitting the application. The projection must be authorized and confirmed by the consumer or authorized representative. |
| **Email address** | Must belong to the **consumer or their authorized representative**. The email account must be **secure**. The agent may only enter it **after receiving consent**. It may not use domains that remove email after a set period; must be accessible by the consumer and **not accessible by the agency or agent**; and may not use domains belonging to the agency or agent. |
| **Telephone number** | Must belong **only** to the consumer or their authorized representative. The agency or agent **may not enter their own telephone number** or their business or agency's number, unless it is the actual number of the consumer or their authorized representative. |
| **Mailing address** | Must belong to, or be **primarily accessible by**, the consumer or their authorized representative, and must be an **actual residence or a secure location** where the consumer may receive correspondence. The agent **cannot input their own mailing address** unless it is the consumer's actual address. |

### Amendment process

Pursuant to **Section C(24) of Exhibit A**, this Code of Conduct is **incorporated by reference into the Agency Agreement as well as the Non-Monetary Agent Agreement**. Agencies and agents are mandated to comply with the rules as set forth in this document.

Covered California **reserves the right to modify or amend this Agent Code of Conduct at any time and without prior notice** to agency or agent. Any changes shall be posted to the **Become an Agent** page. In advance of amending the Code of Conduct, Covered California will communicate changes to agencies and agents via the appropriate communication channels.

**Questions about the Code of Conduct:** contact **AgentContracts@covered.ca.gov**.
