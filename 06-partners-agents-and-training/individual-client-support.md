## Summary

This page provides the Enrollment Assistance Authorization Form used to document a consumer's permission for enrollment help. The form is available in five languages, and partners must select the version matching the client's preferred language. Questions about which form to use or how to complete it go to Service and Support for Agents.

## Key Facts

- The Enrollment Assistance Authorization Form documents a **consumer's permission for a Certified Enrollment Counselor to provide enrollment help**.
- **Five language versions** are published: English, Spanish, Vietnamese, Chinese, and Korean.
- Select the form that matches **the client's preferred language**.
