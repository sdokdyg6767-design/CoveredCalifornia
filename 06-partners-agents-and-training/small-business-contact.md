# Contact and Support for CCSB Agents

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment-partners-small-business/contact
- **Covered California section:** For Enrollment Partners — Small Business — Contact CCSB
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page routes CCSB-certified agents to the right support channel. The CCSB Service Center handles five categories of question; everything account-related is redirected through the CCSB Contact Sheet. It also lists the consumer-facing resources an agent should point clients to, and an escalation directory covering Public Records Act requests, legal notices, appeals, and event speakers.

## Key Facts

- **CCSB Service Center: (855) 777-6782** or **ccsbeligibility@covered.ca.gov**.
- The service center handles: **general questions about Covered California for Small Business, product support, agent training and certification, agent sales support, and commission inquiries**.
- For **benefits, billing, eligibility, invoice payments and other account-related issues**, agents are referred to the **CCSB Contact Sheet (PDF)**.
- Feedback: **OutreachandSales@covered.ca.gov**.

## Details

### Contact the CCSB Service Center

Contact the CCSB Service Center at **(855) 777-6782** or **ccsbeligibility@covered.ca.gov** for:

1. **General questions** about Covered California for Small Business
2. **Product support**
3. **Agent training and certification**
4. **Agent sales support**
5. **Commission inquiries**
