# Enrollment Partners for Individuals & Families

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment-partners-individuals
- **Covered California section:** For Enrollment Partners — Serving Individuals & Families
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This is the hub page for licensed insurance agents and community enrollment partners who help individuals and families enroll in Covered California. It splits the audience into two certification paths — licensed insurance agents and Certified Enrollers — and points to training, marketing and branding resources, and contact routes. The certification path depends on the partner's role.

## Key Facts

- Two distinct certification paths exist: **licensed insurance agent** and **enrollment counselor / Certified Enroller**.
- Resources include **LMS certification courses, enrollment tool kits, and role-specific resources** for agents and enrollment counselors.
- Dedicated partner support lines exist that are **separate from Covered California's consumer-facing contact options**.

## Details

### Two certification paths

| Role | Resource page | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Licensed insurance agents** | Become a Certified Agent | Review the **certification steps, agent agreements, and compensation** for selling individual and family plans |
| **Community organizations, health plan enrollers, and application counselors** | Become a Certified Enroller | Certification guides and **Enroller Portal help** for your role |

### Key resources for certified partners

| Resource | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Training and Education** | Plan information, **LMS certification courses**, enrollment tool kits, and role-specific resources for agents and enrollment counselors |
| **Marketing and Branding** | **Logo and co-branding rules**, website requirements, and storefront resources for outreach |

### Getting started

"New to Covered California? Start your certification." Get certified as a licensed insurance agent or enrollment counselor to start helping individuals and families enroll. Your certification path depends on your role.

### Support

Support is available for agents and enrollment counselors — find the right contact for your situation through the **Contact Us** page for partners, which is distinct from the consumer line.
