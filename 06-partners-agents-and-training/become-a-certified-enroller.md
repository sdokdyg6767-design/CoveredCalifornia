# Become a Certified Enroller

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment-partners-individuals/become-a-certified-enroller
- **Covered California section:** For Enrollment Partners — Individuals & Families — Become a Certified Enroller
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page provides the how-to guides and Enroller Portal user guides for becoming a Certified Enroller. The path depends on your role: entity guides cover entity setup, while counselor and enroller guides cover completing your own certification for your program type. Certification guidance spans three program tracks — Certified Application Counselor, Medi-Cal Managed Care Plan, and Plan-Based Enroller.

## Key Facts

- Three certification program tracks are documented: **Certified Application Counselor**, **Medi-Cal Managed Care Plan**, and **Plan-Based Enroller**.
- Each track has **two documents**: a How-to Become guide and an Enroller Portal User Guide.
- **Six how-to guides** and **six Enroller Portal User Guides** are published in total.
- Use the entity guides for entity setup; use the counselor or enroller guides for completing your own certification.
