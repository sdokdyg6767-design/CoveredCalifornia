# CCSB Certification

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment-partners-small-business/ccsb-certification
- **Covered California section:** For Enrollment Partners — Small Business — CCSB Certification
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page explains the CCSB-specific certification pathway for licensed insurance agents selling Covered California for Small Business plans. The path forks into Agency Certification and Downline Agent Certification depending on whether you are setting up a new agency or certifying under an existing certified agency. Every CCSB agent must sign either the CCSB Agency Agreement or the CCSB Non-Monetary Agreement and follow the CCSB Agent Code of Conduct.

## Key Facts

- There are **two certification paths**: **Agency Certification** and **Downline Agent Certification**.
- All CCSB licensed insurance agents must sign **either the CCSB Agency Agreement or the CCSB Non-Monetary Agreement**.
- **CCSB Agency Agreement** is completed by the **agency manager**. If the agency manager lacks authority to sign for the agency, an **authorized signer must also complete** the agreement.
- **CCSB Non-Monetary Agreement** is completed by **downline agents**. **No information is collected for commission-payment purposes** because all commissions are reported under the **agency's FEIN**.
- **A new agency** must appoint an **Authorized Signer or Agency Manager** to initiate the contracting process. The agency manager is responsible for all business submitted to CCSB and management of all agents using that agency's **Tax ID**.
- **Downline agents** certify under an existing certified agency. Before a downline can enroll, the agency must either **be in the process of becoming certified or already certified**.
- Agents already selling individual-market plans **may already have dual certification** to enroll small businesses. If dual certification was terminated, you must **complete CCSB certification** again.
- Contracting questions: **agentcontracts@covered.ca.gov** or **(855) 777-6782**.
- LMS training technical questions: **cculearning@covered.ca.gov**.
- Failure to comply with the Code of Conduct **may result in termination of the agreement and revocation of certification**.

## Details

### Get certified to sell CCSB plans

CCSB certifies licensed insurance agents to enroll California small businesses into group health insurance that meets the diverse needs of employers and employees. This page covers the **CCSB-specific certification pathway**. To certify for the individual and family market instead, use the certification guide for enrollment partners for individuals and families.

### Choose your certification path

The right path depends on whether you are setting up a new agency or certifying under an existing certified agency.

| Path | Who it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Agency Certification** | A **new agency** appointing an Authorized Signer or Agency Manager to initiate the contracting process |
| **Downline Agent Certification** | Agents who are **employed by an agency**, certifying under an existing certified agency |

### Agency structure

A new agency must appoint an **Authorized Signer or Agency Manager** to initiate the contracting process. The agency manager is responsible for:

- All business submitted to CCSB
- The management of all agents using that agency's **Tax ID**

**Downline agents**, or agents who are employed by an agency, certify under an existing certified agency to manage small-business enrollments. Before a downline can enroll, the agency must either **be in the process of becoming certified or already certified**.

> Source note: the site publishes these two items as cards labelled **"Agency Certification"** and **"Downline Agent Certification"** under the heading "Choose Your Certification Path."

### Agreements and compliance

To align with the CCSB agency contracting structure, all CCSB licensed insurance agents must sign **either the CCSB Agency Agreement or the CCSB Non-Monetary Agreement**.

| Agreement | Who completes it | Notes | Document |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **CCSB Agency Agreement** | **Agency manager** | If the agency manager does not have authority to sign on behalf of the agency, an **authorized signer must also complete** the agreement | CCSB Agency Agreement (PDF) |
| **CCSB Non-Monetary Agreement** | **Downline agents** | Associates agents with their agency and ensures contractual obligations are met. **No information is collected for commission-payment purposes** because all commissions will be reported under the **agency's FEIN** | CCSB Non-Monetary Agreement (PDF) |

**Additional resources:** **Agency Manager Toolkit (PDF)**.

### Code of conduct

Agents must also follow the **CCSB Agent Code of Conduct** to maintain certification with Covered California. **Failure to comply may result in termination of the agreement and revocation of certification.**

### Dual certification from the individual market

If you are currently a Covered California licensed insurance agent selling for the individual market, you **may already have dual certification** to enroll small businesses. If your dual certification was **terminated**, you will need to **complete CCSB certification** to sell CCSB plans. **Confirm your certification status** before starting the process.
