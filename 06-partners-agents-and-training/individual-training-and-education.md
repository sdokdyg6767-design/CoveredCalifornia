# Training and Education for Enrollment Partners Serving Individuals and Families

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment-partners-individuals/training-and-education
- **Covered California section:** For Enrollment Partners — Individuals & Families — Training and Education
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page is the training and reference desk for certified enrollment partners serving individuals and families. It covers the Learning Management System (LMS) certification courses, the Enrollment Partner Tool Kit, privacy and security requirements for handling consumer PII, delegation policy, and the delegation and delegation change request process. It also separates role-specific resources for Certified Enrollment Counselors and Plan-Based Enrollers versus licensed insurance agents.

## Key Facts

- The LMS offers seven named courses: Introduction to the Affordable Care Act; Introduction to Health Insurance; Covered California for Small Business; Eligibility for Individuals and Families; Plan Option; Privacy, Security and Compliance; CalHEERS.
- **Personally Identifiable Information (PII) must remain secure and confidential**; **emails containing consumer PII must be encrypted or secured**.
- Consumers may **delegate their entire application** to an authorized representative, including filling out and submitting the application, making changes to cases, terminating cases, renewing plans, and selecting a plan.
- The Enrollment Partner Tool Kit contains **seven tool kits**: Special Enrollment; Covered California Health and Dental Plans; Webinars and Briefings; Subsidy-Eligible Maps; Storefront; Small Business; Online Application (CalHEERS) Login.
- Role-specific LMS refresher courses: **Understanding Your Community Enrollment Partners Role** (enrollment partners) and **Understanding Your Agent Role** (agents).
- Questions about premiums, payment due dates, and costs of doctor visits should be directed to the health, dental, or vision insurance company — **not** to Covered California.
- Book of business transfers use the **Individual Marketplace Book of Business Transfer Form (PDF)**.

## Details

### Plan information

**Health Benefit Tables (PDF)** provide the health benefit tables for Covered California individual and family plans. Plan booklets, formularies, and provider directories are available on each plan's own website, listed on **Insurance Company Contacts**.

### Where to direct plan-specific questions

Questions about **premium bills, payment due dates, and costs associated with doctor visits or other services** should be directed to the health, dental, or vision insurance company. Company representatives can also answer questions about doctor and hospital networks and can help people choose a provider. Phone numbers and websites of insurance companies working with Covered California are listed in the **Plan Contact Information** resource.

### Policies and responsibilities

#### Delegation and Delegation Change Request Policy

Consumers can **delegate their application** to assist them with the entire application process, including:

- Filling out the application
- Submitting the application
- Making changes to cases
- Terminating cases
- Renewing plans
- Selecting a plan

Resource: **Delegation (PDF)**.

#### Privacy and Security

**Personally Identifiable Information (PII) must remain secure and confidential** in accordance with Covered California's security and privacy policies, including all applicable laws and regulations. It is the **responsibility of certified partners** to ensure that they handle this information appropriately.

> **Reminder:** emails with consumer PII **must be encrypted or secured**.

| Resource | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Using 7-Zip for Encryption (PDF)** | Guidance on encrypting files |
| **Privacy and Security (PDF)** | Full privacy and security policy |
