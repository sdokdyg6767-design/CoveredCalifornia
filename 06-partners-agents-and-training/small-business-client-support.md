# Client Support Resources for CCSB Agents

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment-partners-small-business/client-support
- **Covered California section:** For Enrollment Partners — Small Business — Client Support Resources
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page is the one-stop resource for CCSB-certified agents serving small-business clients. It splits employer-facing materials (applications, submission deadlines, change requests, administrative forms) from employee-facing materials (enrollment and change forms, COBRA materials, member ID card guidance), and adds the MyCCSB portal, renewal, and book-of-business transfer guides. Anything the forms do not answer is directed to the CCSB Service Center.

## Key Facts

- **Six downloadable agent resources** are published, including the Enrollment and Eligibility Guide, Renewal User Guide, and Book of Business Transfer Form.
- The **CCSB Service Center** is available for questions that the forms and guides do not answer.
- The **Book of Business Transfer Form (PDF)** is used to transfer a CCSB book of business to **another licensed agent or agency**.
- A dedicated **Complaint Form (PDF)** is available **for agents and agencies**.
- The **MyCCSB Portal Support (PDF)** covers managing group enrollment and updating employee information online.
- Related consumer-side resources referenced: Eligibility Guidelines, How to Apply, Health Plans, and Dental Plans.

## Details

### Support for every client need

Use the resources below to find the right form, guide, or support path for your client's situation. If you have a question that the forms and guides do not answer, the **CCSB Service Center** is available to help.

### Employer forms and applications

Employer applications, submission deadlines, employer change requests, and administrative forms for plan sponsors managing their CCSB group insurance. Specific documents published:

| Document | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Enrollment and Eligibility Guide (PDF)** | Step-by-step guides for navigating the **MyCCSB portal**, understanding **employer eligibility**, and completing **new business enrollment** |
| **Renewal User Guide (PDF)** | Renewal guide and process support for agents helping employer groups through their **annual CCSB renewal** |
| **Book of Business Transfer Form (PDF)** | Transfer your CCSB book of business to **another licensed agent or agency** |
| **Complaint Form (PDF)** | Complaint form **for agents and agencies** |
| **MyCCSB Portal Support (PDF)** | Agent user guide for the **MyCCSB Enrollment Online Portal**, including how to manage group enrollment and update employee information online |

### Employee forms and insurance support

Employee enrollment and change request forms, **COBRA materials**, and **member ID card guidance** for employees covered under a CCSB group plan. Live documents:

- **Employer Forms and Applications** — applications, submission deadlines, change requests, administrative forms.
- **Employee Forms** — enrollment and change request forms, COBRA documents, member ID card guidance.

### More ways to support your clients

Point clients to the right next step, from reviewing plan options to completing enrollment.

| Client need | Resource |
| --- | --- |
| Review employer and employee eligibility rules before supporting an application or renewal | **Eligibility Guidelines** |
| Walk an employer through the application path and the information they need to apply | **How to Apply** |

### Get support

The **CCSB Service Center** is available to support agents with **general questions, sales support, and commission inquiries**. See the **Contact CCSB** page for the phone number, email, and routing guidance for billing, escalation, and other issues.
