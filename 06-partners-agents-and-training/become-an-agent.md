## Summary

This page explains how to become a Covered California licensed insurance agent. There are two agent categories with different requirements, and to align with Covered California's agency-contracting structure and agency portal, every licensed agent must sign one of two agreements — the Agency Agreement or the Non-Monetary Agent Agreement. Agents can become licensed once they complete all requirements of the certification process.

## Key Facts

- There are **two categories of agents**, with **different requirements for certification**.
- Every Covered California licensed insurance agent must sign **one of two agreements**: the **Agency Agreement** or the **Non-Monetary Agent Agreement**.
- **Agencies of one or more:** the agency's **authorized signer or agency manager** must sign the Agency Agreement. Every agency must **designate an agency manager**, who is responsible for all business submitted to Covered California and for managing all agents using that agency's federal tax identification number.
- **Current independent agents are required to sign the Agency Agreement** because they are identified as an **agency of one** and will be designated as the agency manager.
- **Agents working for an agency** must sign the **Non-Monetary Agreement** to associate with that agency. **No information is collected for commission-payment purposes.**
- Agency managers must complete the certification process **and submit additional forms** to become certified.
- Commission for individual enrollments and CCSB enrollments is set out in the **Agent Commission Structure (PDF)**.

## Details

### Agent categories and agreements

There are two categories of agents, which have different requirements for certification. To align with Covered California's agency-contracting structure and agency portal, all Covered California licensed insurance agents must sign **one of two agreements**: the agency agreement or the non-monetary agreement.

### Agencies of one or more

The agency's **authorized signer or the agency manager** must sign the **Agency Agreement (PDF)**.

Every agency must **designate an agency manager**. The agency manager is responsible for:

- All business submitted to Covered California
- The management of all agents using that agency's federal tax identification number

**Current independent agents are required to sign the Agency Agreement** because they are identified as an **agency of one** and will be designated as the agency manager.

Agency managers must complete the certification process and submit additional forms to become certified. Resources:

| Resource | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Agency Manager Toolkit (PDF)** | Guidance for agency managers |
| **Agency Certification Onboarding Task Guide (PDF)** | Step-by-step onboarding tasks |

### Agents working for an agency

Agents who are employed by agencies must sign the **Non-Monetary Agreement (PDF)** in order to associate them with that agency and to ensure the agent meets his or her contractual obligations with Covered California.

**No information will be collected for commission-payment purposes.**

Agents must also complete the certification process and submit additional forms to become licensed. Resource: **Downline Agent Onboarding Guide (PDF)**.

### Agent compensation

The **Agent Commission Structure (PDF)** outlines the commission-payment source for:

- Individual enrollments
- Covered California for Small Business enrollments

### Agent code of conduct

Review the **Agent Code of Conduct** to understand the standards all Covered California licensed insurance agents must follow.

### Who this is for

Being a Covered California licensed insurance agent means you are at the forefront of the effort to make affordable health insurance a reality for Californians. Insurance agents can become licensed with Covered California once they complete **all requirements of the certification process**.
