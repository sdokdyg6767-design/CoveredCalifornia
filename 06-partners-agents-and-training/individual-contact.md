# Contact Us — Enrollment Partners Serving Individuals & Families

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment-partners-individuals/contact
- **Covered California section:** For Enrollment Partners — Individuals & Families — Contact Us
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page lists the dedicated partner support channels for certified individual-market agents and community enrollment partners, including Certified Enrollment Counselors (CECs) and Plan-Based Enrollers (PBEs). These lines are separate from consumer-facing support. It also routes plan-specific questions to insurance companies and gives the email for the Outreach and Sales Team.

## Key Facts

- **CEC/PBE Helpline: (855) 324-3147** — general questions and support on Covered California products; partner training and certification.
- **Agent Service Center: (877) 453-9198** or **Agents@covered.ca.gov** — agent training and certification; agent sales support.
- **Consumer line: (800) 300-1506** — consumers needing help with their own application or account should be directed here.
- **Outreach and Sales Team:** OutreachandSales@covered.ca.gov.
- Questions specific to a health plan — premiums, provider networks, or plan-specific insurance details — should go to the **insurance company directly**.
- Agents with questions about **individual marketplace commission** inquiries or **insurance company appointments** should also contact the relevant company.

## Details

### Find the right support channel

As a certified enrollment partner, you have access to **dedicated support lines that are separate from Covered California's consumer-facing contact options**. Use the contacts below for questions about Covered California products, partner training and certification, sales support, and client-enrollment issues.

### Support contacts by role

| Channel | Contact | Handles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CEC/PBE Helpline** | **(855) 324-3147** | General questions and support on Covered California products; **partner training and certification** |
| **Agent Service Center** | **(877) 453-9198** or **Agents@covered.ca.gov** | **Agent training and certification**; agent sales support |

### When to contact an insurance company

For questions specific to a health plan — such as **premiums, provider networks, or plan-specific insurance details** — contact the **insurance company directly**.

Agents with questions about **individual marketplace commission** inquiries or **insurance company appointments** should also contact the relevant company.

### Outreach and Sales Team

Have feedback? The Covered California **Outreach and Sales Team** wants to hear it. Send your questions and comments to **OutreachandSales@covered.ca.gov**.

### Directing consumers

If a consumer needs help directly — such as with their own application or account — direct them to Covered California's **consumer support line at (800) 300-1506**, or visit the **Contact Us** page for a full list of consumer support options.
