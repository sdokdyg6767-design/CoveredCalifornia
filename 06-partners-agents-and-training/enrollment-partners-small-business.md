# Helping Employers and Their Employees (CCSB) — Support and Tools for Certified CCSB Agents

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment-partners-small-business
- **Covered California section:** For Enrollment Partners — Serving Small Business
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This is the hub page for licensed insurance agents certified to sell Covered California for Small Business. It routes agents to four areas — CCSB certification, training and education, client support resources, and contact — and highlights the CCSB service center hours and contact details. Covered California certifies agents who help California small businesses enroll employees in group health and dental insurance.

## Key Facts

- CCSB service center: **(855) 777-6782** and **ccsbeligibility@covered.ca.gov**.
- Hours: **Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.**
- The service center handles **general questions, product support, training and certification, and commission inquiries**.
- Four featured resource areas: Become a CCSB Agent; Training and Education; Client Support Resources; Contact CCSB.

## Details

### Four resource areas

| Resource | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Become a CCSB Agent** | **Certification pathway, required training and agreements** to complete before your first enrollment |
| **Training and Education** | **Plan information, training resources and marketing tools** to support your CCSB clients and grow your small business book |
| **Client Support Resources** | **Employer and employee forms, enrollment and renewal guides** and support paths for your small business clients |
| **Contact CCSB** | Get help with CCSB agent questions, contracting, and other support |
