# CCSB Agent Code of Conduct

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/enrollment-partners-small-business/ccsb-certification/code-of-conduct
- **Covered California section:** For Enrollment Partners — Small Business — CCSB Agent Code of Conduct
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

The CCSB Agent Code of Conduct sets the conduct rules all agencies and agents certified with Covered California for Small Business must follow as a condition of certification. It is substantively identical in scope to the individual-market Agent Code of Conduct, covering ethical sales, prohibition of consumer and translation fees, HCSM procedures, supplemental product and anti-steering rules, professional conduct, Authorized Representative designations, American Rescue Plan off-exchange transitions, and application data integrity. Document revision is **Rev. 3/2025**.

## Key Facts

- Document revision: **Rev. 3/2025**.
- Failure to comply constitutes a **breach of the Agency or Non-Monetary Agent Agreement** and may result in **termination of the Agreement and revocation of certification** pursuant to **Section E of Exhibit C**.
- Agencies and agents **may not charge any fee** to consumers for performing any service under either Agreement.
- Agencies and agents **may not charge consumers translation fees** — these services must be provided **free of charge**.
- HCSM consumers must sign a **Health Care Sharing Ministry Consumer Acknowledgement Form**; executed forms must be retained for a **minimum of three years** and produced to Covered California upon request.
- Authorized Representative designations require **express written consumer consent** per **10 CCR § 6508(b)**.
- Agents may **not enter their own email address, telephone number, or mailing address** on an application unless it is the consumer's actual information.
- Questions: contact the **Agent Contracts team** (Agent Contracts email).
- Covered California **reserves the right to modify or amend this Code of Conduct at any time and without prior notice**.

## Details

### Why this code exists

Covered California's mission is to **increase the number of insured Californians, improve health care quality, lower costs and reduce health disparities** through an innovative and competitive marketplace that empowers consumers to choose the health plan that best meets their needs.

Covered California **contracts with licensed insurance agencies and agents** to implement this mission and ensure consumers get quality service and enrollment help. The Agent Code of Conduct was developed to **promote ethical sales practices and preserve the integrity of its mission and the trust of its consumers**. Agencies and their agents must comply with the rules to maintain certification.

> **Note:** Failure to comply constitutes a **breach of the Agency or Non-Monetary Agent Agreement** and may result in **termination of the Agreement and revocation of certification** pursuant to **Section E of Exhibit C**.

### Ethical sales and consumer-first assistance

1. Promote Covered California health plans **fairly and accurately** to consumers.
2. **Will not charge any fee** to consumers for performing any service under the Agency Agreement or Non-Monetary Agent Agreement.
3. **Will not charge consumers translation fees** for services offered in connection with a Covered California enrollment. These services must be provided **free of charge**.
4. Agree to assist consumers with finding the health plan that best meets their needs. Enrollment recommendations must be based on the **consumer's best interests** and must not be influenced by **financial compensation and incentives from health issuers**.

### Health Care Sharing Ministry products

Agents will only sell **Health Care Sharing Ministry (HCSM)** products in accordance with the Agency Agreement and this Code of Conduct. For each consumer who signs up for an HCSM product:

1. Perform an **eligibility determination** as outlined in the Agency Agreement for any consumer interested in purchasing an HCSM product. **Before enrolling**, share the **full results of the eligibility determination** with the consumer and explain the **benefits of enrolling in a Covered California plan**. Disclose **all financial assistance** the consumer may be eligible to receive and **all insurance affordability programs** they may be eligible to enroll in, such as **Medi-Cal**.
2. Require consumers to complete a **Health Care Sharing Ministry Consumer Acknowledgement Form** before enrolling them. The agency must maintain hard or electronic copies of executed forms for a **minimum of three years** and make them available to Covered California **upon request**.
3. Ensure agents complete an **attestation** to confirm compliance with this section when completing any **annual training requirements**.
4. If requested by Covered California, disclose the **total number of HCSM products sold in the previous plan year**.

### Supplemental products and steering

5. Must not mislead consumers into purchasing **supplemental products as a requirement** for a Covered California plan. Consumers must be told the purchase of supplemental products is **optional and not required** to obtain a Covered California plan.
6. Must not **unduly influence** consumers to enroll in lower-premium health plans when it is not in the consumer's best interest. Agents **cannot steer** consumers into plans with lower monthly premiums in order to facilitate **supplemental product sales**.
7. Must not **condition enrollment** in Covered California plans on enrollment in other products unrelated to Covered California plans. Agents must assist with Covered California enrollment **even when the consumer does not want to purchase a supplemental product**.

### Professional conduct

8. When performing any service under the Agency Agreement that involves interacting with Covered California consumers, employees, **Qualified Health Plan (QHP) issuers**, contractors, or other representatives acting on Covered California's behalf, agency and agent **shall not engage in abusive, intimidating, threatening, or any other detrimental behavior**.

### Approved Admin Staff and Authorized Representatives

9. Agencies and agents will ensure their **Covered California-Approved Admin Staff** are only designated as **Authorized Representatives for those consumers who have expressly consented in writing** to the designation, as required by **10 CCR § 6508(b)**. Admin Staff designated as Authorized Representatives may only perform those duties as authorized in the Agency Agreement and **may not enroll consumers** into Covered California plans while acting on behalf of the Agency or Agent.

### Off-exchange transitions under the American Rescue Plan

10. When migrating an **off-exchange** consumer to a Covered California qualified health plan (QHP) to maximize the premium subsidy under the American Rescue Plan, agency and agent are **not required to present all available enrollment options**. This limited exception applies **only** to off-exchange to Covered California enrollments where the consumer can take advantage of **enhanced subsidies**. Agency and agent **must present all enrollment options** if specifically requested by the consumer during the transition, **and for all other enrollments**. Agency and agent must **track** all such enrollments; Covered California **may request information at any time**.

### Accurate application information

| Field | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Household income** | The consumer or authorized representative **must confirm the accuracy** of the household income projection **before submitting** the application |
| **Email address** | Must belong to the **consumer or their authorized representative** and the account must be **secure**. The agent may only enter it **after receiving consent**. The address may not use domains that remove email after a set period; must be accessible by the consumer and **not accessible by the agency or agent**; and may not use domains belonging to the agency or agent. |
| **Phone number** | Must belong **only** to the consumer or their authorized representative. The agent **may not enter their own telephone number** or their business or agency's number, unless it is the consumer's actual number. |
| **Mailing address** | Must belong to, or be **primarily accessible by**, the consumer or their authorized representative, and must be an **actual residence or a secure location** where the consumer may receive correspondence. The agent **cannot input their own mailing address** unless it is the consumer's actual address. |

### Agreement incorporation and updates

Pursuant to **Section C(24) of Exhibit A**, this Code of Conduct is **incorporated by reference into the Agency Agreement as well as the Non-Monetary Agent Agreement**. Agencies and Agents are mandated to comply with the rules as set forth in this document.

Covered California **reserves the right to modify or amend this Agent Code of Conduct at any time and without prior notice**. In advance of amending, Covered California will communicate changes via the appropriate communication channels.
