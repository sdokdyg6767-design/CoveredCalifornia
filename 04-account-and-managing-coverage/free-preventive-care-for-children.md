# Free Preventive Care for Children

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/account/using-your-plan/free-preventive-care-for-children
- **Covered California section:** Managing Your Account
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

Every Covered California health plan covers a defined set of child and adolescent preventive services, vaccines, screenings and supplements at no added cost when the child sees an in-network provider, even before the annual deductible is met. The page organises benefits into well-child and adolescent visits, the childhood vaccine schedule, newborn screening, supplements, and screening services for children at elevated risk.

## Key Facts

- Preventive services are free **when your child sees an in-network provider**, **even if you have not met your deductible for the year**.
- Tell the office you are coming in for a **preventive care visit** when scheduling.
- Mentioning a **new symptom or health concern** may result in a **separate charge** not covered as preventive care.
- Autism screening is done **at 18 and 24 months**; newborn hearing screening **at birth**.
- Developmental screening runs **through age 3**; most age-banded services run **through age 17**.
- **Hepatitis B** vaccine is given in a series **starting at birth**.
- **Influenza (flu)** vaccine is given **every year**.
- **HPV** and **meningococcal disease** vaccines are **recommended for preteens and teens**.
- **Iron supplements**: babies **6 to 12 months** at risk for anemia.
- **Hepatitis B screening**: adolescents **ages 11 to 17** at high risk.
- **Fluoride varnish** is applied **when teeth come in**, typically provided by a dentist.
- **HIV pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP)** is covered **as needed**.

## Details

### What to Know Before Your Child's Visit

Preventive care includes checkups, vaccines, screenings and counseling that help catch health concerns early.

- These services are **free when your child sees an in-network provider**, even if you have not met your deductible for the year.
- When you schedule the appointment, **tell the office you are coming in for a preventive care visit**. This helps make sure you are not charged.
- Mentioning a new symptom or health concern during the visit may result in a separate charge, and that part of the visit may not be covered as preventive care.

### Well-Child and Adolescent Visits

| Service | What it is | When it's covered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wellness exams | Regular checkups to track your child's growth, development and overall health | Throughout childhood |
| Medical history | A review of your child's health history at each visit to catch concerns early | Through age 17 |
| Height, weight and BMI measurements | Tracking height, weight and BMI to watch growth and prevent obesity | Through age 17 |
| Behavioral assessments | Check-ins to spot emotional or behavioral concerns early | Through age 17 |
| Developmental screening | Screenings to make sure babies and toddlers are developing on track | Through age 3 |
| Autism screening | A check to look for early signs of autism | At 18 and 24 months |
| Vision screening | Routine vision tests to catch problems early | Throughout childhood |
| Newborn hearing screening | A hearing test to detect hearing loss early | At birth |
| Depression screening | Screenings to check for signs of depression, with referrals to support as needed | Children and teens |
| Tobacco use prevention | Counseling to help prevent tobacco use | Through age 17 |
| Alcohol, tobacco and drug use assessments | Discussions to help prevent misuse of alcohol, tobacco and drugs | Through age 17 |

### Vaccines

| Vaccine | When it's given |
| --- | --- |
| Diphtheria, tetanus and pertussis (DTaP/Tdap) | Given in a series during childhood and adolescence |
| Haemophilus influenzae type b (Hib) — protects against meningitis and pneumonia | Given in a series during infancy and early childhood |
| Hepatitis A | Given in a series during childhood |
| Hepatitis B | Given in a series starting at birth |
| Human papillomavirus (HPV) — protects against certain cancers later in life | Recommended for preteens and teens |
| Inactivated poliovirus (IPV) | Given in a series during childhood |
| Influenza (flu) | Every year |
| Measles, mumps, rubella (MMR) | Given in a series during childhood |
| Meningococcal disease | Recommended for preteens and teens |
| Pneumococcal disease — protects against pneumonia | Given in a series during infancy and early childhood |
| Rotavirus | Given in a series during infancy |
| Varicella (chickenpox) | Given in a series if your child has not had chickenpox yet |

### Newborn Screening

| Service | When it's done |
| --- | --- |
| Blood screening | A general blood test to check for anemia or other conditions, as needed at birth |
| Bilirubin concentration screening | A test to check bilirubin levels and screen for jaundice, at birth |
| Hemoglobinopathies or sickle cell screening | A blood test for sickle cell disease or other inherited conditions, at birth |
| Hypothyroidism screening | A test for hypothyroidism to prevent growth and developmental delays, at birth |
| Hearing screening | A hearing test to check for hearing loss, at birth |
| Gonorrhea preventive medication | Eye medication to prevent gonorrhea infection, at birth |
| Phenylketonuria (PKU) screening | A test for PKU, a condition that can cause serious health issues if not caught early, at birth |

### Supplements and Dental Prevention

| Service | When it's covered |
| --- | --- |
| Iron supplements | Babies 6 to 12 months at risk for anemia |
| Fluoride chemoprevention supplements | Children without access to fluoridated water — typically provided by a dentist |
| Fluoride varnish | When teeth come in — typically provided by a dentist |

### Screening Services

| Service | When it's covered |
| --- | --- |
| Obesity screening and counseling | Through age 17 |
| Lead screening | Children at risk |
| Hematocrit or hemoglobin screening | As needed |
| Tuberculin testing | Children at risk, through age 17 |
| Hepatitis B screening | Adolescents ages 11 to 17 at high risk |
| HIV screening | Teens at high risk |
| STI prevention counseling and screening | Adolescents at higher risk |
| Cervical dysplasia screening | Sexually active young women |

### HIV Pre-Exposure Prophylaxis (PrEP)

Covered **as needed**. PrEP includes the **medication and the required services**, including HIV testing, hepatitis B and C testing, kidney function tests, pregnancy testing, STI screening and adherence counseling.

### Summary of the Coverage Promise

All Covered California health plans include preventive care for children at **no added cost**.
