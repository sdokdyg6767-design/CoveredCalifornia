# Cancel Your Plan

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/account/cancel
- **Covered California section:** Managing Your Account
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page explains how to cancel a Covered California health or dental plan. Cancellation requires at least 14 days' advance notice and coverage ends on the last day of the month, so the practical deadline is the 14th of the month you want coverage to end. It also warns about the downstream tax effects of cancelling mid-year and notes that health and dental plans must be cancelled separately, and vision plans must be cancelled directly with the vision insurer.

## Key Facts

- Submit your cancellation request **at least 14 days in advance**.
- To end insurance on **June 30**, you would need to submit by **June 16**.
- Coverage ends on the **last day of the month**.
- If you cancel mid-month, your insurer **does not have to refund** you for days you already paid for, and your next plan's start date is likely the first of the following month — leaving a **gap in coverage**.
- Short-notice requests are handled **case by case**.
- **Health and dental plans are separate** — you must cancel each one individually to end both.
- **Vision plans must be cancelled by calling the vision insurance company directly.**
- There are **no fees for cancelling**.
- Going without health insurance may mean you **owe a penalty on your California state taxes**.
- If you received financial help to lower your premium, **cancelling mid-year may also affect your taxes** when you file.
- You generally **cannot cancel insurance for months that have already passed**.

## Details

### Before You Cancel

- Canceling ends your Covered California insurance, and you may have to **wait until open enrollment** to re-enroll unless you experience a major life change.
- You generally **cannot cancel insurance for months that have already passed**.
- **If cost is why you are cancelling**, the page recommends reporting a change in income or household size first — sign in to your account to see whether your costs change before making a final decision.
- If you are unsure, contact Covered California before deciding.

### How to Cancel

- **Submit your cancellation request at least 14 days in advance.** To end insurance on June 30, for example, you would need to submit by June 16.
- Coverage ends on the **last day of the month**.

### If You Cancel Mid-Month

Ending your plan on the last day of the month is encouraged. If you cancel mid-month:

- Your health insurance company **does not have to refund** you for the days you already paid for.
- Your next plan's start date is likely the **first of the following month**, leaving you with a **gap in coverage**.

If you need to cancel sooner, short-notice requests are handled on a **case-by-case basis** — contact Covered California or your insurance company.

### Cancelling a Vision Plan

To cancel your vision plan, **call your vision insurance company directly**. Covered California does not process vision plan cancellations.

### Health and Dental Are Separate

Health and dental plans are separate; you need to **cancel each one individually** if you want to end both. Act by the deadline on your notice to avoid a gap in coverage. If you are losing Medi-Cal coverage, see the Losing Medi-Cal Coverage page for next steps.

### Refunds and Fees

- There are **no fees for cancelling**.
- Contact your health or dental insurance company about refunds — **Covered California does not handle billing or payments**.
- If you go without health insurance, you may owe a **penalty on your California state taxes**.
- If you received financial help to lower your monthly premium, **cancelling mid-year may also affect your taxes** when you file.

Contact Covered California before cancelling if you have questions about how it may affect you.
