# Guide to Your Bill and Payments

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/account/billing
- **Covered California section:** Managing Your Account
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page explains that billing comes from the health insurance company, not from Covered California, and walks through the first payment, recurring monthly bills, and what happens if a payment is missed. Its central warning is that a plan will not start until the first bill is paid, and that any care received before payment is fully at the enrollee's cost. It also notes that children's dental and vision are bundled into the health plan while adult dental and vision are separate plans with separate bills.

## Key Facts

- Your bill comes from your **health insurance company**, not from Covered California.
- The **first payment** can be made online through the "Pay Now" option in your Covered California account, but that option is **only available for your first payment**.
- If you do not pay online, your insurer mails you a bill; you pay the insurer, **not Covered California**, by the due date on the bill.
- **Your plan will not start if you do not pay your first bill.**
- Any care you receive before your plan starts is not covered and you are responsible for the **full cost**.
- After the first payment, your insurer mails a bill **each month** stating the due date, what you owe, and how to pay (online, by phone or by mail).
- Missing a payment can result in cancellation; to get insurance again you may have to wait until **open enrollment** or until you have a **qualifying life event**.
- Children's dental and vision are included in the health plan and are **not a separate bill**.
- Adult dental and vision are **separate plans, each with its own bill**.
- Your monthly payment can change when your financial help changes, for example after a renewal or after reporting an income change.

## Details

### Your First Payment

- Once you enroll, look for **Pay Now** in your Covered California account. This online option is available only for the first payment.
- If you do not pay online, your health insurance company will mail you a bill. Pay that company — not Covered California — by the due date shown on the bill. You can also phone the insurer to pay.
- **Coverage does not begin without the first payment.** Care received before the plan starts is not covered and you bear the entire cost.

### Your Future Payments

- After the first payment, your insurer sends a bill every month.
- Each bill states the due date, the amount owed, and the ways to pay: online, by phone or by mail.

### Missed Payments

- If you miss a payment, your plan could be canceled.
- To regain insurance you may have to wait for the next open-enrollment period, or until you experience a qualifying life event.
- Paying by the due date each month is what keeps coverage active.

### Get Help With Your Bill

- Contact your insurance company for billing questions; they can confirm your bill and due date and tell you about faster online or phone payment options.
- Children's dental and vision are part of the health plan and do not generate a separate bill.
- Adult dental and vision are separate plans with separate bills.

### Why Your Payment May Change

Your monthly payment can change if your financial help changes — for example after a renewal, or after you report a change in income. See the Guide to Your Account for how to update your information (Covered California enrollees must report changes within 30 days).
