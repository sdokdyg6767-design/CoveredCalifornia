# Mental Health and Therapy Coverage

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/account/using-your-plan/mental-health
- **Covered California section:** Managing Your Account
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page distinguishes free preventive mental health screenings from non-preventive mental health treatment, which is covered but subject to a deductible and copay depending on the plan's coverage level. It also records the statutory parity requirements California imposes on health plans, and the special mental health benefit available to the Minimum Coverage Plan for people under 30.

## Key Facts

- Free mental health **screenings** are covered as part of **preventive care**, and are usually provided during a **routine check-up**, not during a visit for specific symptoms or therapy.
- **Preventive mental health screenings are covered at no cost**; therapy and ongoing treatment have their own costs and rules.
- **People under 30 may get extra mental health coverage.**
- Some insurers provide additional mental health benefits free of charge, which depending on the plan may include **free meditation apps**, a **24/7 crisis-support hotline**, and **peer support for substance abuse**.
- Non-preventive services are covered but subject to a **deductible and copay**; what you pay depends on your **coverage level**.
- California state law requires all health plans to cover diagnosis and treatment for specific serious mental health conditions including **major depressive disorder, bipolar disorder and emotional disturbances in children**.
- Mental health treatment must be **equal to other forms of health care**, meaning out-of-pocket costs must be **comparable** to other services.
- Health plans must offer members a **return behavioral health appointment within 10 business days** after referral or initial visit.
- The **Minimum Coverage Plan** (catastrophic plan) includes **up to three outpatient or urgent-care mental health visits per year that are completely free** (no copays or deductibles), and is **only available to people under 30**.

## Details

### What's Covered

Mental health is one part of your overall health, and your plan covers more than free preventive screenings. Depending on your plan, you may also have access to **added mental health benefits**, therapy and other ongoing mental health care. **People under 30 may get extra mental health coverage.**

- **Free mental health screenings** are covered as part of your preventive care and are usually provided during a **routine check-up**, not during a visit for specific symptoms or therapy. See the Free Preventive Care for Adults page for the full list.

### Other Mental Health Benefits

Some health insurers provide **additional mental health benefits free of charge**. Depending on the plan you choose, you may get access to:

- **Free meditation apps**
- A **24/7 crisis-support hotline**
- **Peer support for substance abuse**

### Non-Preventive Services

Some plans include additional services that are covered by your health plan but are subject to a **deductible** and **copay**. What you pay depends on which **coverage level** you choose. These services include:

- Counseling or therapy for a diagnosed condition (such as anxiety or depression)
- Psychiatric evaluations
- Medication management
- Substance use disorder treatment (such as rehab or therapy)
- Mental health hospitalization or partial hospitalization

### California Law Requirements

- California state law requires that **all health plans cover diagnosis and treatment for specific serious mental health conditions**, such as major depressive disorder, bipolar disorder and emotional disturbances in children.
- **Mental health treatment must be equal to other forms of health care.** That means out-of-pocket costs must be comparable to other services.
- Health plans must also offer members a **return behavioral health appointment within 10 business days** after a referral or initial visit.

### Minimum Coverage Plan

The Minimum Coverage Plan — also known as a **catastrophic plan** — includes **up to three outpatient or urgent-care mental health visits per year that are completely free** (no copays or deductibles).

- This plan is **only available to people under 30**.
- It usually has a **higher cost when you use medical services** but **lower monthly premium payments**.
