# How to Renew Your Plan

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/account/renew
- **Covered California section:** Managing Your Account
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

Renewal opens each October, ahead of open enrollment, and Covered California notifies enrollees by mail or with a message on sign-in. If you take no action you are re-enrolled into your current plan within 30 days of the renewal deadline using the most recent household and income information on file, which is why the page stresses updating income before renewing. The page also contrasts Covered California renewal with Medi-Cal renewal and flags Medicare transition as something to plan for during renewal.

## Key Facts

- Renewal opens **each October**.
- If you do nothing, you are **re-enrolled into your current plan within 30 days of the renewal deadline**, using the most recent household and income information on file.
- Your account or renewal notice carries **your specific deadline** and next steps.
- Your renewal notice tells you if your current plan is **no longer available** and you must choose a new one.
- All Covered California plans cover the **same essential health benefits**; the main difference is what you pay monthly and what you pay when you use care.
- During renewal you can apply for financial help **for the first time** by signing in and following the prompts.
- Covered California renewal happens **each October**; Medi-Cal renewal happens **throughout the year** based on your Medi-Cal anniversary date.
- For Covered California, **some renewals are automatic** — follow the instructions in your renewal notice.
- Update your estimated yearly income before renewing to see whether your **financial help** or **program eligibility** changed.
- Pay any required premium to keep coverage active.
- "What to Review" is published as three cards: **Household and Contact Information**, **Income and Financial Help**, and **Plan Options**.

## Details

### When You Can Renew

Renewal opens in October. When it is time, Covered California sends you a notice with what you need to know and next steps, or you will see a message when you sign in to your account.

### What to Review

**Household and Contact Information** (card)

- Confirm that your address, phone number and email are up to date.
- Add anyone who joined your household.
- Remove anyone who moved out or is no longer a dependent.

Changes to your household may affect eligibility and financial help.

**Income and Financial Help** (card)

- Financial help changed
- Program eligibility changed

Update your estimated yearly income before renewing to see whether your financial help or program eligibility changed. Keeping your current health or dental plan, or choosing a different plan that better fits your needs and budget, are both permitted during renewal.

- **If you do nothing, you will be re-enrolled into your current plan within 30 days of the renewal deadline, using the most recent household and income information on file.**
- If that information has changed, update it in your account — using outdated information can affect your financial help and your taxes.
- Your account or renewal notice has your deadline and next steps, and will tell you if your current plan is no longer available.

**Plan Options** (card)

When comparing plans, check whether:

- Your doctors and hospitals are in network
- Your prescriptions are covered
- The costs fit your budget

### Compare Plans

Plans and prices can change each year. Use the Shop and Compare Tool to review options before renewing.

All Covered California plans cover the same essential health benefits. The main difference is how much you pay each month and how much you pay when you use care.

### Covered California and Medi-Cal Renewals Are Different

| Renewal Topic | Covered California | Medi-Cal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| When it happens | Each October during the renewal period | Happens throughout the year based on your Medi-Cal anniversary date |
| Who contacts you | Covered California sends renewal information or account notices | Your county may contact you if information is needed |
| What to do | Review your application, compare options, keep or change your plan | Follow the instructions in your renewal notice; some renewals are automatic |

### Financial Help at Renewal

Yes, your financial help can change if your income or household size change. Updating your information before you renew helps make sure you get the right amount. During renewal you can apply for financial help **for the first time** — sign in to your account and follow the prompts to see if you qualify.

### Steps to Renew or Change Your Plan

1. **Sign in.** Sign in to your account to review your renewal information and deadline.
2. **Review and update your information.** Check your household, income, tax and contact information, and update anything that has changed.
3. **Compare your options.** Use Shop and Compare to look at plan costs and benefits, and whether your doctors, hospitals and prescriptions are covered.
4. **Choose a plan.** Keep your plan if it still meets your needs, or choose a new plan before your deadline. Pay any required premium to keep your coverage active.

### Renewing and Medicare

If you are approaching Medicare eligibility, renewal is the right time to plan your transition. The page directs you to learn when to report your eligibility, how to cancel your Covered California plan, and what happens if you do not act in time. ### Major Life Changes During the Period

Some life changes — such as getting married, having a baby, or losing other insurance — let you change plans outside of open enrollment. See the Major Life Changes page.

### Free Help

Free help is available to renew. Contact an enroller in your area or call Covered California at **(800) 300-1506**.
