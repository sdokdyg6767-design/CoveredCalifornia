# Free Preventive Care for Adults

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/account/using-your-plan/free-preventive-care-for-adults
- **Covered California section:** Managing Your Account
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

Every Covered California health plan covers a defined set of adult preventive services and vaccines at no added cost when you use an in-network provider and schedule the visit as preventive care, even if you have not met your annual deductible. The page lists the covered screenings, the covered vaccines, and behavioral health and infectious disease services with their exact recommended frequencies.

## Key Facts

- Preventive services are free **when you see an in-network provider** and schedule the visit as **preventive care**, **even if you have not met your deductible for the year**.
- Tell the office you are coming in for a **preventive care visit** when you make the appointment.
- Mentioning a **new symptom or health concern** during the visit may result in a **separate charge** that is not covered as preventive care.
- Preventive **mental health screenings** are covered at no cost; therapy and ongoing treatment have their own costs and rules.
- Colorectal cancer screening is for **adults 45+**, covered **every 1–10 years depending on the test**.
- Abdominal aortic aneurysm screening is for **men aged 65–75 who have smoked**, covered **once per lifetime**.
- Fall-prevention counseling is for **adults 65+**, covered **every year**.
- Pneumococcal disease vaccine is covered for **adults 65+, or at risk**.
- Tdap is covered **once every ten years**.
- HPV is recommended **up to age 26, or older if at risk**.
- Herpes zoster (shingles): **typically two doses for adults 50 and older**.
- HIV screening: adults **15–65**, **once, or more depending on risk**.

## Details

### What to Know Before Your Visit

Preventive care includes checkups, vaccines, screenings and counseling that help catch health concerns early, before they become bigger problems. These services are part of **every** Covered California health plan.

- They are **free** when you see an **in-network** care provider and schedule the visit as preventive care, **even if you have not met your deductible for the year**.
- When you make the appointment, **tell the office you are coming in for a preventive care visit**. This helps make sure you are not charged.
- Mentioning a new symptom or health concern during the visit may result in a separate charge, and that part of the visit may not be covered as preventive care.

### Screenings and Counseling

| Service | What it is | When it's covered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annual wellness exam | A yearly checkup to review your overall health, catch issues early and stay up to date on vaccines | Every year |
| Blood pressure screening | A check of your blood pressure to catch high blood pressure early | Every year |
| Cholesterol screening | A blood test to check cholesterol levels and help prevent heart disease based on age and risk | Usually every 4-6 years |
| Abdominal aortic aneurysm screening | An ultrasound to check for an aortic aneurysm in men aged 65-75 who have smoked | Once per lifetime |
| Obesity screening and counseling | Weight checks and support to reach or maintain a healthy weight | Ongoing |
| Type 2 diabetes screening | A blood sugar test to catch diabetes early in adults with high blood pressure | Every year |
| Diet counseling | Nutrition advice for adults at higher risk for chronic disease | As needed |
| Skin cancer counseling | Guidance on protecting your skin and lowering skin cancer risk | Adults at high risk |
| Fall-prevention counseling | Advice on preventing falls, including exercise and home safety tips for adults 65+ | Every year |
| Colorectal cancer screening | A test for colon cancer for adults 45+ | Every 1–10 years depending on the test |
| Lung cancer screening | For people at high risk, like long-time smokers | Every year |

### Vaccines

| Vaccine | When it's given |
| --- | --- |
| COVID-19 | Every year or as advised |
| Influenza (flu) | Every year |
| Hepatitis A | Usually a two-dose series if you're at risk |
| Hepatitis B | Usually a two- or three-dose series if you're at risk |
| Herpes zoster (shingles) | Typically two doses for adults 50 and older |
| Human papillomavirus (HPV) | Recommended up to age 26, or older if at risk |
| Measles, mumps and rubella (MMR) | If you don't have evidence of immunity |
| Meningococcal disease | If you're at increased risk |
| Pneumococcal disease | Covered for adults 65+, or at risk |
| Tetanus, diphtheria and pertussis (Tdap) | Once every ten years |
| Varicella (chickenpox) | If you never had chickenpox or been vaccinated |
| Haemophilus influenzae type b | If you're at increased risk |

### Mental Health and Substance Use

| Service | When it's covered |
| --- | --- |
| Alcohol misuse screening and counseling | As needed |
| Depression screening | Every year, or as needed |
| Tobacco screening and cessation support | Every year, or as needed |

Preventive mental health screenings are covered at no cost. **Therapy and ongoing treatment have their own costs and rules** — see the mental health and therapy coverage page.

### Infectious Disease Services

| Service | When it's covered |
| --- | --- |
| HIV screening | Once, or more depending on risk |
| HIV pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP) | As needed |
| STI prevention counseling | As needed |
| Syphilis screening | As needed |
| Hepatitis C screening | Once, or more depending on risk |

**HIV pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP)** includes the medication **and** the required services before starting:

- HIV testing
- Hepatitis B and C testing
- Creatinine testing and kidney function tests
- Pregnancy testing
- STI screening and counseling
- Adherence counseling to support proper use

### Summary of the Coverage Promise

All Covered California health plans include preventive care at **no added cost**.
