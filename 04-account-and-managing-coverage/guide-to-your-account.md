# Guide to Your Account

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/account/guide-to-your-account
- **Covered California section:** Managing Your Account
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page explains how to create, access and update a Covered California account: linking an existing application with an access code, resetting a forgotten password or username, reporting household and income changes, managing consent to verify eligibility, and authorizing or removing an enroller. Its most decision-relevant content is the reporting deadline, which differs by program: 30 days for Covered California enrollees and 10 days for Medi-Cal enrollees.

## Key Facts

- Covered California enrollees must update changed information **within 30 days** of the change.
- Medi-Cal enrollees must notify their county office **within 10 days** of the change.
- Consent to verify eligibility can be given for **up to five years**.
- If you choose **zero years** of consent, you keep your health plan but receive no help paying your monthly premium until you give consent again.
- Service Center phone: **(800) 300-1506**.
- TTY: **(888) 889-4500**.
- If you have no access code, call (800) 300-1506 and a representative can generate one.
- Password reset requires username and date of birth; a one-time passcode is sent.
- To recover a forgotten username you enter your **email address and date of birth**, and the username is sent to your email.
- **To unlock a locked account, call (800) 300-1506.**
- Enrollers are added either by a representative authorization form or by calling (800) 300-1506.
- You can cancel or limit an enroller's access in writing at any time.

## Details

### Create or Link an Account

- If you have never applied before, select **Create New Application** from the account creation page.
- If you applied through an agent or a Covered California representative, you need an **access code** to connect that existing application to a new online account. The access code was included in a notice mailed to you after your application was submitted.
- Go to the account creation page, select **Link Existing Application**, answer **Yes** when asked whether you have an existing application to link, and enter your access code.
- If you don't have an access code, call **(800) 300-1506** so a representative can generate a new one.

### Troubleshooting Sign-In

**Forgot your password**

1. Select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.
2. Enter your username and date of birth; a one-time passcode is sent.
3. Enter the passcode, then create a new password.

**Forgot your username**

1. On the sign-in page, select **Forgot username?**
2. Enter your email address and date of birth.
3. Your username is sent to your email.

**Account is locked**

"Call us at (800) 300-1506 to unlock your account. We'll just need to confirm it's you first."

If your account is locked, call **(800) 300-1506**. Covered California only needs to confirm your identity first.

### Update Your Information

When your situation changes during the year, you must report it. Updating your information may trigger a new eligibility check, and a change can affect:

- **The plans available to you** — if you move, your new area may offer different plans.
- **How much financial help you get** — you may get more or less help. If you receive more than you qualify for, you may have to pay some or all of it back when you file your federal taxes.
- **Which program you qualify for** — your income determines whether you qualify for Medi-Cal or Covered California.

### What Changes to Report

| Category | Changes |
| --- | --- |
| Household | Marriage, divorce, birth, adoption, gaining or losing a dependent |
| Income | Any change in income, including starting or losing a job |
| Address | Moving to a new address |
| Other | Getting health insurance through a job, through Medicare or through Medi-Cal |
| Status | Disability status, tax-filing status, citizenship or immigration status, incarceration or release, American Indian or Alaska Native tribal status |
| Identity | Corrections to your name, date of birth or Social Security number |

### When to Update Your Information

The deadline depends on the program you get health insurance through:

| Program | Deadline |
| --- | --- |
| Covered California | Sign in to your account and update within **30 days** of the change |
| Medi-Cal | Notify your local county office within **10 days** of the change |

### Consent to Verify Your Information

Covered California checks your eligibility whenever you apply, update your information, or renew your plan. This determines which program you qualify for (Covered California or Medi-Cal) and how much financial help you can get. To run the check, Covered California needs your consent to confirm your application information using past records and government data.

- You may be asked to submit documents to confirm eligibility; see the Documents to Confirm Eligibility page for the accepted list.
- **Consent can be given for up to five years.** Each year you renew, Covered California uses this consent to check whether you still qualify for financial help without asking again.
- **Choosing zero years** means Covered California cannot check your eligibility for financial help at renewal. You keep your current health plan, but without help paying your monthly premium, until you give consent again. If you have Medi-Cal, Medi-Cal eligibility is still checked.

**How to update consent**

- By text: reply to the Covered California text message about your consent.
- Online: sign in to your account and go to Account Information.
- By phone: call **(800) 300-1506**.

### Get Help From an Agent or Enroller

You do not have to apply on your own. A licensed insurance agent or a certified enrollment counselor (an enroller) can help you apply for and enroll in a health plan for free. Adding an enroller gives them permission to see the information needed to check whether you qualify and to enroll you in a plan.

- Add an enroller by completing a representative authorization form, or by calling **(800) 300-1506**.
- To remove an enroller, tell them you no longer need their help, or call Covered California. You can cancel or limit their access in writing at any time.

### Keep Your Personal Information Safe

- When enrolling or managing your account online, confirm you are on the official Covered California website, coveredca.com.
- Only share personal information with people you know and trust.
- If someone offers to help you enroll, make sure they are certified by Covered California.
