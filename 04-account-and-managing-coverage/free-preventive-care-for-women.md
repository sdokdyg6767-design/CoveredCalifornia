# Free Preventive Care for Women

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/account/using-your-plan/free-preventive-care-for-women
- **Covered California section:** Managing Your Account
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page lists the preventive services Covered California plans cover for women, from routine well-woman visits and cancer screening through contraception, pregnancy care and breastfeeding support. Most of these services are covered at no added cost when you use an in-network provider and the visit is scheduled as preventive care. The page organises the benefits into general screening, breast and reproductive health, and pregnancy and maternity care.

## Key Facts

- Most preventive services are covered at **no added cost** when you see an **in-network provider** and the visit is **scheduled as preventive care**.
- Cervical cancer screening (Pap test): **every 3 years for most women starting at age 21**.
- HPV DNA test: **every 3 years for women 30+ with normal results**.
- Breast cancer mammography: **every 1–2 years for women over 40**.
- Osteoporosis screening: **as needed, depending on risk**, for women over 60.
- Gestational diabetes screening: **usually at 24–28 weeks** of pregnancy.
- Hepatitis B screening: **once per pregnancy**.
- Folic acid supplements: **ongoing** (daily supplements to help prevent birth defects).
- Contraception: **as needed** — access to FDA-approved birth control methods, counseling and education.
- Breastfeeding support and counseling: **ongoing**, plus access to pumps and supplies.
- Domestic and interpersonal violence screening and counseling: **as needed**.

## Details

### What to Know Before Your Visit

Plans offered through Covered California include important health care services for women, from screenings, birth and reproductive health services to pregnancy and maternal health, sexual and reproductive health care and more.

- **Most preventive services are covered at no added cost** when you see an in-network provider and the visit is scheduled as preventive care.

### General Screening and Wellness

| Service | What it is | When it's covered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Well-woman visit | An annual checkup to review your overall reproductive and general health, screenings and preventive care needs | Every year |
| Cervical cancer screening | A Pap test to find early signs of cervical cancer | Every 3 years for most women starting at age 21 |
| HPV DNA test | A test to find high-risk HPV types that cause cervical cancer | Every 3 years for women 30+ with normal results |
| Osteoporosis screening | A bone density test to check for osteoporosis and help prevent fractures for women over 60 | As needed, depending on risk |
| Urinary incontinence screening | A yearly check and help managing incontinence, if needed | Every year |
| Tobacco screening and cessation support | Screening for tobacco use, with help to quit if needed, plus extra counseling during pregnancy | As needed |
| Domestic and interpersonal violence screening and counseling | Private counseling and support if you may be experiencing domestic or interpersonal violence | As needed |

### Breast and Reproductive Health

| Service | When it's covered |
| --- | --- |
| Breast cancer genetic counseling (BRCA) | As need for women at higher risk — genetic counseling for women with a family history or other breast cancer risk factors |
| Breast cancer mammography screening | Every 1–2 years for women over 40 |
| Breast cancer chemoprevention counseling | When recommended — advice on medications that can help lower breast cancer risk |
| Contraception | As needed — access to FDA-approved birth control methods, counseling and education |

### Pregnancy and Maternity

| Service | What it is | When it's covered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anemia screening | A blood test to check for anemia during pregnancy | During pregnancy |
| Folic acid supplements | Daily supplements to help prevent birth defects | Ongoing |
| Gestational diabetes screening | A blood sugar test to find gestational diabetes early | Usually at 24–28 weeks |
| Hepatitis B screening | A test for hepatitis B | Once per pregnancy |
| Preeclampsia prevention and screening | Monitoring and support to help prevent preeclampsia | During pregnancy |
| Rh incompatibility screening | A blood test to check for issues that could affect you or your baby | During pregnancy |
| Urinary tract or other infection screening | Tests to protect you and your baby | During pregnancy |
| Syphilis screening | A blood test for syphilis | During pregnancy |
| Breastfeeding support and counseling | Personal support and counseling, plus access to pumps and supplies | Ongoing |

### Summary of the Coverage Promise

All Covered California health plans include preventive care for women at **no added cost**.
