## Summary

This page covers IRS Form 1095-A: when Covered California sends it, who does not get one, how the advance premium tax credit on it is reconciled against actual income at tax time, and how to dispute an error. The headline date is January 31 — Covered California will send Form 1095-A by that date each year.

## Key Facts

- Covered California will send IRS **Form 1095-A by January 31**.
- Form 1095-A shows the **months you had insurance** and the **federal financial help** you received during the year.
- The financial help shown is the **advance premium tax credit (APTC)**; at tax time the IRS compares it against your actual income.
- If your income was **lower** than you estimated, you may get a **credit or refund**; if **higher**, you may have to **repay some** of the help.
- You will receive your **1095-A and 3895** forms by mail (to the address on file) and online (online mailbox, with an email or text when ready).
- You may get **more than one** form.
- You will **not** receive a 1095-A if you had **Medi-Cal**, a **minimum coverage plan (catastrophic plan)**, or **employer health insurance through Covered California for Small Business (CCSB)**.
- Service Center phone for a missing confirmation: **(800) 300-1506**.
- Covered California **does not provide tax advice**.

## Details

### Form 1095-A

Covered California will send you IRS Form 1095-A **by Jan. 31**. You need it when filing your federal and state taxes. It shows:

- The **months you had insurance**.
- The **federal financial help** you received during the year.

California requires most people to have health insurance. If you had a gap last year, you may owe a **penalty on your state taxes** unless you qualify for an exemption.

### How Financial Help Affects Your Taxes

Your 1095-A shows the financial help you received during the year, called the **advance premium tax credit (APTC)**. At tax time the IRS checks that amount against your actual income:

- **Income lower than estimated** — you may get a credit or refund.
- **Income higher than estimated** — you may have to repay some of the help.

This is separate from your overall refund. For how financial help and repayment work, see What is Financial Help.

Covered California does **not** provide tax advice or help, but it can connect you with the experts — the **IRS** and the **Franchise Tax Board (FTB)**.

### Find Your Form

You receive your **1095-A and 3895** forms the same way you get other documents and letters from Covered California:

| Delivery | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| By mail | Forms are mailed to the address on file |
| Online | Forms go to your online mailbox, and Covered California emails or texts you when they are ready |

- You may get **more than one** form.
- You can change how you receive communications from Covered California in your account.

### Didn't Receive Your Form?

You will **not** receive an IRS Form 1095-A if you had:

- **Medi-Cal**
- **A minimum coverage plan**, also known as a catastrophic plan
- **Employer health insurance through Covered California for Small Business (CCSB)**

If none of those apply to you, first check your Covered California online mailbox. Download your tax form by signing in to your account.

### What to Do If Your Form Looks Wrong

A form that looks wrong is sometimes correct. Check these common reasons first:

- **$0 help for some months** — this can happen if you missed a premium payment and your insurance ended for those months. Any help from earlier months still shows on your form.
- **No help all year** — this can happen if you paid full price for your plan. In that situation your form is correct.

### How to Report an Error

If you still believe there is a mistake:

1. **Submit the online dispute form.** You will need your case number, health plan name and primary member information.
2. **Watch for confirmation.** Covered California will let you know it received your request.
3. Covered California reviews the request and, if it finds a mistake, sends you **corrected tax forms** to use when you file your taxes.

If you do not receive a confirmation, call **(800) 300-1506**.
