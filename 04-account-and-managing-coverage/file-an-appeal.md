# File an Appeal or a Complaint

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/account/file-an-appeal
- **Covered California section:** Managing Your Account
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This page routes an enrollee to the right remedy: an appeal for a Covered California eligibility decision, a complaint for a service or process problem, the insurance company for plan-specific issues, and the Ombuds Office as a last resort. The key deadlines published are a **90-day** window to file an appeal from the date the eligibility decision was mailed, up to **90 days** for the hearing process and decision, and **30 business days** for a complaint response.

## Key Facts

- You generally must file an appeal **within 90 days** of the date Covered California mailed your eligibility decision.
- For most appeals, the hearing process and decision take **up to 90 days** from the date Covered California receives a valid appeal request.
- Covered California will review a complaint and respond **within 30 business days**.
- If you get no response or update within that timeframe, you can contact the **Ombuds Office**.
- To file an appeal, fill out and **mail** the appeal form; instructions are on the form.
- To file a complaint, use the **online** complaint form.
- The Ombuds Office should be contacted **only after** the Service Center and only if the issue remains unresolved.
- Hearings are **usually held by phone** unless you ask for an in-person hearing.
- You may represent yourself, or have an attorney or another representative help you.
- You can appeal for yourself **or on behalf of any household member listed on your application** — list each name on the form.
- The appeal form is available in **multiple languages**, and in other formats such as **Braille or large print**.
- Covered California phone: **(800) 300-1506**, TTY **(888) 889-4500**.
- Medi-Cal Helpline: **(800) 541-5555**. DHCS Ombuds Office: **(888) 452-8609**.
- After a county receives your completed application it may take **up to 45 days** to get your Benefits Identification Card (BIC) from Medi-Cal.
- Health Consumer Alliance (free legal help and representation): **(888) 804-3536**.

## Details

### Choosing the Right Path

Not every problem follows the same process. Pick the option matching your issue:

| Route | Use this when | Where to go |
| --- | --- | --- |
| File an Appeal | You disagree with a Covered California eligibility determination, or a Covered California action or failure to act affected your health insurance or financial help | Fill out and mail the appeal form |
| File a Complaint | Problems with Covered California's service, website, enrollment process, a service center representative, or a certified enroller or agent. Not for disputing an eligibility determination | Online complaint form |
| Contact Your Insurance Company | Monthly premiums, billing, claims, provider networks, covered services, benefits, membership ID cards or other plan-specific issues | Insurance Company Contacts page |
| Contact the Ombuds Office | Only after you have contacted the Service Center and the issue remains unresolved | Ombuds Office page — learn how to open a case |

Your eligibility notice explains what you qualify for and which programs you do not qualify for. If you think Covered California made a mistake about your eligibility, filing an appeal is the right next step.

### Reasons for Filing an Appeal

**Coverage or enrollment decisions**

- You were denied enrollment in a health plan through Covered California.
- Your insurance ended because of a mistake made by Covered California, an agent, a certified enroller or the insurance company.

**Financial help or out-of-pocket cost decisions**

- The amount of financial help you were approved for is not correct.
- The level of cost-sharing reductions you were approved for is not correct.

**Application processing or notice timing**

- Covered California did not process your application, or the information you submitted, on time.
- Covered California said your application was incomplete and you do not agree.

**Identity, residency, income and other eligibility decisions**

You can file an appeal if you believe Covered California made a mistake, including where it said you are not eligible because:

- You are not a U.S. citizen, U.S. national or lawfully present person living in the United States.
- You are not a California resident.
- Your income is too low to qualify for insurance through Covered California.
- You do not qualify for financial help because you are eligible for or enrolled in other insurance, such as Medi-Cal, Medicare or job-based insurance.
- You do not qualify for an exemption from California's individual mandate.
- You do not qualify for special enrollment because Covered California said you did not have, or did not prove, a major life change.
- You did not pay your monthly premium by the due date.

### Appeal Steps, Deadlines and Who Decides

| Appeal step | Deadline | Who decides |
| --- | --- | --- |
| File the appeal form | Generally must be filed **within 90 days** of the date Covered California mailed your eligibility decision | Covered California receives and screens the request |
| Pre-hearing contact (optional resolution) | After you file; Covered California may contact you first to see if the issue can be resolved before a hearing is scheduled | Covered California |
| Hearing scheduling | If the issue is not resolved, a hearing is scheduled | California Department of Social Services |
| Hearing itself | The hearing process and decision take **up to 90 days** from the date Covered California gets a valid appeal request | Administrative law judge |
| Final decision | After the hearing, the judge reviews the information and **mails you a final decision** | Administrative law judge |
| Complaint review | Covered California reviews the complaint and responds **within 30 business days** | Covered California |
| Escalation to Ombuds | Only after contacting the Service Center and if the issue remains unresolved (a complaint filed more than 30 days ago with no update) | Ombuds Office |

Hearings are usually held by phone unless you request an in-person hearing. You can represent yourself or have an attorney or another representative help you.

### Filing for Household Members

You can file an appeal for yourself or on behalf of any household member listed on your Covered California application. **List each name on the appeal form** so Covered California knows whose eligibility determination is being appealed.

### Accessibility and Language Access

The appeal form is available in multiple languages. If you need it in another language or format, such as **Braille or large print**, or if you need help filling it out, call **(800) 300-1506** (TTY: **(888) 889-4500**).

### If You Have an Active Appeal

Do not take additional action on your case without guidance. Contact Covered California at **(800) 300-1506** (TTY: **(888) 889-4500**) for help.

### Medi-Cal Issues

Covered California cannot update the county systems that decide Medi-Cal eligibility. After your county gets your completed application, it may take **up to 45 days** for you to get your **Benefits Identification Card (BIC)** from Medi-Cal.

- Medi-Cal Helpline: **(800) 541-5555**
- DHCS Ombuds Office: **(888) 452-8609**
- Or contact your local county office.

### Legal Help

Covered California cannot give legal advice. The **Health Consumer Alliance** offers free legal advice and representation by phone or in person — call **(888) 804-3536** or visit the Health Consumer Alliance website.
