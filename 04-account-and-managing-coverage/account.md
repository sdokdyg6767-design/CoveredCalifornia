# Managing Your Account

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/account
- **Covered California section:** Managing Your Account
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

This is the hub page for everything a current enrollee does after enrolling: paying bills, using insurance, renewing, cancelling, getting tax forms and appealing a decision. It carries the site's interactive "What's your situation?" major life change tool, which lists the qualifying life changes a consumer may claim and the exact number of days they have to act. It also sets out three recurring annual tasks: renew the plan, report income or household changes, and pick up tax forms each January.

## Key Facts

- Three annual tasks for an enrolled consumer: renew your plan, report any income or household changes, get your tax form(s).
- Tax forms are available in your account **each January**.
- The Service Center phone number is **(800) 300-1506**.
- Report a change of income within **30 days**; if the update opens a special-enrollment period you then have **60 days** to choose a plan.
- Report a job change within **30 days** of the job or income change; if the update opens a special-enrollment period you have **60 days** to choose a plan.
- Most major life changes give **60 days** from the triggering date to apply; many also allow applying up to **60 days before** the event.
- Loss of Medi-Cal coverage gives **90 days** from the date Medi-Cal insurance ends to choose a Covered California plan; you may apply up to **60 days before**.
- The California Shared Responsibility Penalty appeal window is **60 days** from the date you filed your California state tax return showing the penalty.
- American Indians and Alaska Natives have **no deadline** and may apply or change plans **once per month, any time of year**.
- The Medi-Cal share-of-cost qualifying event can be used **only once per calendar year**, with **60 days** to apply.
- The HRA qualifying event allows **60 days before and 60 days after** the HRA date insurance begins.
- You may file an appeal for an enrollment or eligibility mistake; you usually have **60 days** from the date you learned about the mistake or got notice of the determination.
- Insurance generally begins the **first day of the month after you choose a plan**, or later of that and the date your prior coverage ended.
- Insurance begins the first day of the month after you choose a plan **and** your health insurance company processes your first payment.

## Details

### The Three Annual Tasks

Once enrolled, the page lists three things that come up each year:

- **Renew your plan** — review and confirm your insurance before each new plan year.
- **Report any income or household changes** — updating your information keeps your financial help accurate.
- **Get your tax form(s)** — tax forms are available in your account each January.

### Major Life Changes and Their Deadlines

The page's "What's your situation?" tool pairs each qualifying life change with its application window and the date coverage begins. The published windows are:

| Qualifying life change | Application window | When insurance begins |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Loss of employer job-based coverage | 60 days after last day of coverage; may apply up to 60 days before | First day of the month after coverage ends or after you choose a plan, whichever is later |
| Loss of COBRA coverage | 60 days before and after; coverage may begin earlier | First day of the month after coverage ends or after you choose a plan, whichever is later |
| Loss of coverage under a parent's plan (turning 26) | 60 days from loss; may apply up to 60 days before | First day of the month after coverage ends or after you choose a plan, whichever is later |
| Loss of a child-only plan | 60 days from the end date; may apply up to 60 days before | First day of the month after coverage ends or after you choose a plan, whichever is later |
| Loss of Medi-Cal | 90 days from the date Medi-Cal ends; may apply up to 60 days before | First day of the month after coverage ends or after you choose a plan, whichever is later |
| Loss of student health insurance | 60 days from the end date; may apply up to 60 days before | First day of the month after coverage ends or after you choose a plan, whichever is later |
| Provider leaves the network | 60 days from the date your provider left the network or the date you found out | First day of the month after the provider left the network or after you choose a plan, whichever is later |
| Marriage or domestic partnership registration | 60 days from the date of marriage or registration | First day of the month after you choose a plan |
| Birth, adoption or foster placement | Usually 60 days from the date of birth, adoption or foster placement | Usually the first day of the month after the event; a different date can be arranged by calling Covered California |
| Determining you may be eligible for Medi-Cal or MCAP | Any time of year | Depends on the program; Covered California shows your date after you apply |
| Household change (adding or removing a member) | Usually 60 days from the date of the change | First day of the month after you choose a plan |
| Divorce, separation or dissolution | Usually 60 days from the major life change | Depends on your situation; call Covered California |
| Court order requiring coverage for a child | May have 60 days from the date of the court order | First day of the month after you choose a plan, unless the court order requires a different date |
| Death of a household member | Usually 60 days from the date of death | For remaining members, usually the first day of the month after you choose a plan |
| Moving within California | 60 days from the move date; may report up to 60 days before the expected move date | First day of the month after your move date or after you choose a plan, whichever is later |
| New immigration status (citizenship, national status, permanent residency, lawful presence) | 60 days from the date the new status was granted | First day of the month after you choose a plan |
| American Indian / Alaska Native | No deadline; may apply or change plans once per month, any time of year | First day of the month after you choose or change your plan |
| California Shared Responsibility Penalty appeal | 60 days from the date you filed the California state tax return | First day of the month after you choose a plan |
| Change in income | Update within 30 days; then 60 days to choose a plan if a SEP opens | First day of the month after you choose a plan |
| Employer HRA offer | 60 days before and 60 days after the HRA date insurance begins | First day of the month after you choose a plan |
| Met your Medi-Cal share of cost | 60 days from the date you met it; usable only once per calendar year | First day of the month after you choose a plan |
| Non-calendar-year plan ends | 60 days from the date the plan ends | First day of the month after coverage ends or after you choose a plan, whichever is later |
| Change of job or income | Update within 30 days; then 60 days to choose a plan if a SEP opens | First day of the month after you choose a plan |
| Open enrollment | Open enrollment is the main time to reenroll | First day of the month after you choose a plan and the insurer processes your first payment |
| Declared state of emergency | Window depends on the emergency declaration; check your account or call (800) 300-1506 | First day of the month after you choose a plan |
| Release from jail or prison | 60 days from the release date; may apply up to 60 days before | First day of the month after release or after you choose a plan, whichever is later |
| Leaving active-duty service | New applicants have 60 days from the date military coverage ends; existing enrollees update within 30 days | First day of the month after you choose a plan |
| Starting or ending AmeriCorps, VISTA or NCCC service | 60 days from the date service started or ended | First day of the month after you choose a plan |
| Rideshare or delivery driver health stipend | 60 days from the date you became stipend-eligible | First day of the month after you choose a plan |
| Enrollment or eligibility mistake | Usually 60 days from when you learned of the mistake or got notice of the determination | Depends on the correction; call Covered California |

### Filing an Appeal or Complaint

If you disagree with a Covered California eligibility or enrollment decision, or want to report a problem with your coverage, the page confirms your right to file an appeal or complaint, and points to the Ombuds Office for independent help resolving a dispute after all other options are exhausted.

### Free Help Channels Highlighted

- Find Free Local Help — free in-person help from a certified enroller or agent, in multiple languages.
- Call Us — (800) 300-1506, multiple languages.
- Live Chat — chat online with a Covered California representative.
