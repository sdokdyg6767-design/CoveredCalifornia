## Summary

This page describes Covered California's "Beyond Covered" initiative, which offers two optional benefits to eligible enrollees: a reloadable grocery debit card and a child scholarship account. Enrollees cannot apply on their own — if they qualify, the partner organisation FORWARD contacts them directly by email, text or phone. Participation is voluntary and does not affect health insurance coverage or eligibility for other assistance programs.

## Key Facts

- Beyond Covered offers **two programs**: grocery support and child scholarship accounts.
- The grocery benefit is a **reloadable debit card for groceries**.
- The child benefit is **up to $1,000** in your child's CalKIDS scholarship account.
- Grocery eligibility: enrollees with a **chronic health condition and household income at or below 250% of the federal poverty level**.
- CalKIDS eligibility: enrollees with **California-born children under 18 months old**.
- You **cannot apply or sign up on your own** — FORWARD contacts you if you are eligible.
- Invitations arrive by **email, text or phone**.
- Spots are **limited — first come, first served**.
- Participation is **voluntary** and will **not affect your health insurance**.
- Leaving the program will **not change** your Covered California health insurance.
- Eligibility for **other assistance programs is not affected**.
- Beyond Covered **launched in 2025** and will be offered **as long as funding is available**.
- Questions about the program go to **FORWARD directly**.

## Details

### Beyond Covered

Covered California's Beyond Covered initiative offers two programs for eligible members: grocery support and child scholarship accounts. If you qualify, Covered California's partner **FORWARD** will contact you by email, text or phone. Participation is voluntary and will not affect your health insurance.

### The Two Programs

| Program | Benefit | Eligibility | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grocery support | A reloadable debit card for groceries | Enrollees with a chronic health condition and household income at or below **250% of the federal poverty level** | Reply to FORWARD's invitation and complete enrollment |
| Child scholarship | Up to **$1,000** in your child's CalKIDS scholarship account | Enrollees with California-born children **under 18 months old** | Complete well-child visits and vaccines, then claim your child's CalKIDS account |

Spots are **limited — first come, first served**.

### How It Works

1. **We will reach out to you.** FORWARD will contact you by email, text or phone if you are eligible. You cannot apply or sign up on your own.
2. **Respond quickly.** Spots are limited, so respond as soon as you can when you receive your invitation.
3. **You can leave at any time.** Joining is your choice. Contact FORWARD directly if you decide to leave the program — it will not change your Covered California health insurance.

### Questions and Funding

- For questions about Beyond Covered, reach out to **FORWARD directly** if you got an invitation from them.
- Beyond Covered **launched in 2025**, and Covered California plans to offer it **as long as funding is available**.
- Eligibility for other assistance programs **will not be affected**. Eligibility is based on specific criteria and available funding, and you may be considered later in the year or in future programs. Contact FORWARD directly with questions.
