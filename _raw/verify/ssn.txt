Proof of Social Security Number
Proof of Social Security Number
Confirm your Social Security number (SSN) by sending us a copy of one of these documents. You also have options if you don’t have an SSN or your SSN application is pending.
Before You Submit
Your eligibility notice or account message will tell you who in your household needs to submit proof of your Social Security number and how to send it.
Documents You Can Submit
You only need to send one accepted document.
If this applies to you:If this applies to you:
Submit one of these documents:
You have a document with your SSN.
Social Security card
Tax form
Benefit or income statement from Social Security that shows the Social Security number
Your SSN is pending or unavailable.
Tax form
Benefit or income statement from Social Security that shows the Social Security number
You have an established religious objection.
Letter stating that the applicant refuses to obtain an SSN for established religious objections (also called an SSN Waiver)
Ready to submit your document?
Follow the instructions in your Covered California account or eligibility notice to submit your document online, by fax or by mail.
