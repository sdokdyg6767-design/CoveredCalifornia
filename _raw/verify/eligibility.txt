Eligibility Guidelines
Find out if you’re eligible for Covered California or Medi-Cal. You may also qualify for financial help to lower the cost of a health plan through Covered California.

How Eligibility Works
The Covered California application is designed to determine eligibility for health insurance programs, including Medi-Cal, and to show you the available plans. What you qualify for depends on a few things about you and your household.
Your Household
Your household includes everyone, even if they are not applying for health insurance:
You
Your spouse or domestic partner
Anyone you claim as a tax dependent
Good to know
Household size and income affect which program you qualify for and how much financial help you may get.
Your Income
We look at your household’s estimated yearly income and household size to determine what programs and financial help you may qualify for.
Good to know
Income is measured against the federal poverty level for your household size.
Where You Live
You must be a California resident.
Good to know
Moving to California from out of state is a major life change that may let you enroll outside open enrollment.
Citizenship or Immigration Status
For people who are applying for health insurance.
Good to know
Only the people applying need to share Social Security numbers or immigration information. The rest of your household can still be listed.
Current Health Insurance
Including insurance through your job, Medicare or other insurance.
Good to know
Having other health insurance doesn't disqualify you from applying, but it may affect whether you qualify for financial help.
Other Factors
Some eligibility rules depend on your age, disability status and other specific situations.
Good to know
These factors may affect which health insurance options are available to you.
Your Eligibility Results
After you apply, you will see eligibility results for each person in your household. Depending on the results, you may qualify for a Covered California plan at full price, one with financial help, or for free or low-cost Medi-Cal. Different people in the same household can qualify for different programs. This is normal and called being a mixed-program family.
If we can’t confirm information from your application, you may be asked to submit documents to confirm your eligibility. We will tell you what type of proof is needed and how to submit it so your application can be reviewed.

Who It’s For
Covered California
California residents who are lawfully present. Depending on income, you may also qualify for financial help to lower your cost.
Medi-Cal
People with lower incomes, children, pregnant individuals and others who meet Medi-Cal eligibility criteria.
Mixed-Program Family
Families where some members qualify for Medi-Cal and others qualify for Covered California.

Covered California and Medi-Cal Eligibility
Your income and household size determine which program you qualify for. If you qualify for Medi-Cal, you get free or low-cost health insurance. If you qualify for a Covered California plan instead, your income also determines how much financial help you get, if any.
Learn more about Covered California and Medi-Cal insurance once you know your results.
Have a specific situation?
A few groups have different eligibility or financial help rules.
Immigrants and Mixed-Status Households
Eligibility and financial help depend on your immigration status. Households with different statuses can still apply together.
Young Adults
If you're under 30, you may qualify for a lower-cost minimum coverage plan in addition to standard options.
American Indian and Alaska Native
Members of federally recognized tribes may qualify for extra benefits and enrollment flexibility.
People With Chronic Conditions
Compare plans for ongoing care, learn what to check before switching and get help if care is disrupted.
