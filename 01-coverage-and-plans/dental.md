# Dental Insurance

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/coverage-basics/dental
- **Covered California section:** Coverage Basics / Dental Insurance
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

Children under 19 automatically get dental insurance through their Covered California health plan at no extra cost; adults must buy a separate family dental plan at enrollment and cannot enroll in a dental plan alone. Cleanings, exams and X-rays are free for children, and the member shares the cost of fillings, root canals and crowns up to an annual cap. The page publishes a full DHMO/DPPO cost-sharing comparison, the complete list of children's dental companies by health insurer with phone numbers, and the two rules governing who can be added to a family dental plan.

## Key Facts

- "Children under 19 automatically have dental insurance through their Covered California health plan at no extra cost."
- "You can't enroll in a dental plan only — you must have a Covered California health insurance plan."
- Children's cost share: "You and the dental company share the cost of fillings, root canals and crowns, up to **$350 for one child** or **$700 for two or more children**."
- "Cleanings, exams and X-rays are free."
- "Financial help does not apply to dental plans, so you pay the full monthly cost."
- "Adult dental insurance is not considered an essential health benefit, the set of services that all health insurance plans are required to cover."
- Family dental plan eligibility rules: "At least one adult age 19 or older has to enroll." and "If one child under 19 enrolls, all children under 19 in the household have to enroll."
- DPPO deductible: "$50 per adult, $75 per child." DHMO deductible: $0.
- DPPO annual benefit limit: "$1,500 for adults, no limit for children." DHMO: No limit.
- Out-of-pocket maximum (both DHMO and DPPO): "$350 one child, $700 two or more. No maximum for adults."
- DPPO waiting period: "Adults wait six months. May be shorter or waived with proof of past coverage." DHMO: None.
- Sample service costs (DHMO / DPPO): amalgam filling (one surface) $25 / you pay 20%; root canal (molar) $300 / you pay 50%; gingivectomy (per tooth) $50 / you pay 50%; extraction (single tooth, exposed root or erupted) $65 / you pay 50%; crown (porcelain with metal) $300 / you pay 50%; medically necessary braces children only $350 / children only you pay 50%.

## Details

### How Dental Insurance Works With Covered California

"Dental insurance comes with every health insurance plan, but only for children under 19. If you want dental insurance for yourself, you pick and pay for a separate plan."

**Two kinds of plans:**

- A **dental HMO (DHMO)** "covers care from dentists in its network only, except for emergencies. You need a referral to see a specialist. DHMO plans have no deductible and set prices for each service."
- A **dental PPO (DPPO)** "covers care from dentists in and out of its network, and you do not need a referral. DPPO plans usually have a deductible, and you pay a share of the bill rather than a set price."

### Dental for Children Under 19

"Dental insurance for children under 19 is included with every health insurance plan at no extra monthly cost. Each health insurance company works with one dental insurance company, so the health plan you pick determines your child's dental plan and which dentists they can see. You can't choose a different dental company for your child without changing your health plan, unless you buy a separate family dental plan."

### Adding Children to a Family Dental Plan

You can add children even if they already have dental through their health plan — "Parents often do this when their dentist is not in the network that came with the health plan. Your child keeps both, and the two plans work together to cover services."

Two rules apply:

- At least one adult age 19 or older has to enroll. Not every adult in the household has to enroll.
- If one child under 19 enrolls, all children under 19 in the household have to enroll.

"Your monthly cost goes up with each person you add."

### How to Get Dental Insurance

1. Pick a health insurance plan in Shop and Compare — "You will need one before you can add an adult or family dental plan."
2. Answer the dental question — "Once you pick a health plan, Shop and Compare asks if you want to add a family dental plan."
3. Compare dental plans and prices — "You will see the plans available in your area, what each covers and how much each costs per month."

### Frequently Asked Questions

- **Why isn't adult dental insurance included with my health insurance plan?** "Adult dental insurance is not considered an essential health benefit, the set of services that all health insurance plans are required to cover. Because of this, dental insurance for adults is offered separately as a family dental plan. No financial help is available to lower the monthly premium for these plans."
- **Can my child have both the included dental plan and a family dental plan?** "Yes. A family dental plan can supplement the dental insurance already included with your child's health insurance plan. If your preferred dentist is not in your child's embedded dental network, a family dental plan may give you access to a broader network. When a child has both types of insurance, the plans coordinate covered services."
- **Where can I find the complete list of covered services, exclusions and limitations?** "View the full list of covered services, exclusions and limitations (PDF) for each of our dental insurance companies. You can also contact your dental insurance company directly."
