# Vision Insurance

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/coverage-basics/vision
- **Covered California section:** Coverage Basics / Vision Insurance
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

Children under 19 enrolled through Covered California already have vision insurance included with their plan and do not need to buy a separate plan. Adult vision care is not included in Covered California health plans; instead, adults buy a standalone plan directly from one of two vision insurers. The page compares EyeMed and VSP on network size, plan options, enrollment channel, and phone contact, and confirms that adult vision enrollment is available year-round rather than only during open enrollment.

## Key Facts

- "If your child is under 19 and enrolled in your plan through Covered California, vision insurance is already included. You don't need to buy a separate plan."
- "Deductibles do not apply to children's vision benefits."
- Children's vision benefits include: **eye exams** and **one pair of glasses per year, or contact lenses instead**.
- "This does not include the minimum coverage plan."
- "Adult vision care is not included in health plans through Covered California."
- "The vision insurance company handles enrollment, billing and plan questions — not Covered California."
- Two adult vision companies: **EyeMed** and **VSP**. Both enroll year-round directly.

- Plan options: EyeMed 3, VSP 2.
- Contact: EyeMed (844) 225-3107; VSP (800) 410-1857.
- "Yes. You can enroll in an adult vision plan at any time — you do not need to wait for open enrollment."
- "Your child's vision coverage starts on the same date as their health plan."

## Details

### Who Can Get Vision Insurance

"Vision insurance works differently for children and adults."

#### Vision Insurance for Children

Included with the parent's Covered California plan for children under 19. Covered items: eye exams; one pair of glasses per year, or contact lenses instead. Deductibles do not apply. Children's vision benefits are not included with the minimum coverage plan.

#### Vision Insurance for Adults

Not included in Covered California health plans. "To get vision insurance, you can buy a separate plan directly from one of our selected vision insurance companies." "Benefits vary by vision insurance company and plan selected."

### Common Questions

- **Can I enroll in adult vision insurance at any time?** "Yes. You can enroll in an adult vision plan at any time — you do not need to wait for open enrollment."
- **When does my child's vision plan start?** "Your child's vision coverage starts on the same date as their health plan."
- **Whom do I contact with questions about a vision plan?** "Contact your vision insurance company directly for questions about enrollment, billing, benefits and plan details."

### Content Note

The page does not publish adult vision plan premiums or specific copay amounts; it directs consumers to enroll directly with EyeMed or VSP, noting that "Benefits vary by vision insurance company and plan selected."
