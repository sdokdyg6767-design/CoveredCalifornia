# Coverage Basics

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/coverage-basics
- **Covered California section:** Coverage Basics (section landing page)
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

Coverage Basics is the landing page for Covered California's "Coverage Basics" section. It tells consumers that they can get coverage in one of two ways — through Covered California (private plans, with or without financial help) or through Medi-Cal — and that eligibility for either is checked during a single application. The page is mainly a navigation hub: it lists the section's child pages (Health Plan Coverage Levels, Medi-Cal, Dental Insurance, Vision Insurance) and explains how Covered California and Medi-Cal work together.

## Key Facts

- "When you apply, you can get covered in one of two ways — through Covered California or Medi-Cal."
- Covered California is described as "the state's health insurance marketplace" and "the only place to get financial help that lowers what you pay for insurance."
- The page states: "Most people who apply qualify for savings."
- Medi-Cal "provides free or low-cost health insurance for people with lower incomes." What you qualify for "depends mainly on your income and household size."
- If you decline financial help, you can still buy a Covered California health plan "at full price."
- Covered California eligibility is described as "Californians without job-based or other government insurance."
- Covered California Service Center: (800) 300-1506; TTY (888) 889-4500. Service Center hours Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 6 p.m.; closed Saturday and Sunday.
- "11,000 enrollers" are referenced elsewhere on the site as available to help consumers apply at no cost.

## Details

### How Covered California and Medi-Cal Work Together

"When you apply for insurance, we'll check to see if you're eligible for Medi-Cal or for financial help to lower the cost of a health plan through Covered California." This is a single-application screening rather than two separate applications.

### Covered California vs. Medi-Cal

| Standard table | Covered California | Medi-Cal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What It Is | Private plans you buy through the state marketplace, with or without financial help | Insurance for people with lower incomes |
| Who Is Eligible | Californians without job-based or other government insurance | Californians who qualify based on income and household size |
| What You Pay | A monthly premium, often lowered by financial help | Little to no cost |

### Understanding Health Plans

Health plans have three main parts: **Coverage Levels**, **Benefits Covered by All Plans**, and **Plan Types**. The page frames coverage levels as the balance between monthly premium and cost of care:

- Lower premium = higher cost when you need care
- Higher premium = lower cost when you need care
