## Summary

Form FTB 3853, titled "Health Coverage Exemptions and Individual Shared Responsibility Penalty," is filed with the California state income tax return. It reports which household members had health insurance during the year, lets eligible filers claim exemptions from the state insurance requirement, and shows whether a penalty is owed. The page also gives step-by-step instructions for filling out the Marketplace Coverage Affordability Worksheet using Covered California's Shop and Compare tool.

## Key Facts

- Full form name: "Form FTB 3853, Health Coverage Exemptions and Individual Shared Responsibility Penalty."
- Use it to report who in your tax household had health insurance during the year, claim exemptions, and see if you owe a penalty and how much.
- You may need to file it if you or anyone in your tax household didn't have qualifying health insurance for one or more months, if you are claiming an exemption, or if Covered California approved an exemption and gave you an exemption certificate number (ECN) to enter on the form.
- "If you file online, this form is typically automatically generated for you through your tax software."
- If filing on paper, "download Form FTB 3853 (PDF) from the California Franchise Tax Board (FTB)"; line-by-line instructions are in the "FTB 3853 Instructions (PDF)."
- Affordability worksheet inputs needed: "your ZIP code, county (if needed), total household income for the tax year, household size and ages of household members."
- Worksheet Line 1 uses the lowest-cost Bronze plan's "**Monthly premium after savings**."
- Worksheet Line 10 uses the second-lowest-cost Silver plan's "**Monthly premium before savings**."
- "Enter your exemption certificate number (ECN) on the form where indicated if you have one... You don't need to submit additional documentation with your return."
- "For questions about your penalty amount or help completing your tax return, contact the California Franchise Tax Board (FTB) or a tax professional. Covered California cannot provide tax advice."

## Details

### How Do I Get Form FTB 3853?

- **Online filing:** the form is typically generated automatically through your tax software.
- **Paper filing:** download Form FTB 3853 (PDF) from the FTB; use the FTB 3853 Instructions (PDF) for line-by-line guidance.

### What to Gather Before You Start

- Your California state tax return or tax software instructions.
- Information about who in your household had health insurance and for which months.
- Exemption certificate numbers (ECNs), if Covered California approved an exemption for you or a household member.
- If the form directs you to the Marketplace Coverage Affordability Worksheet: your ZIP code, county (if needed), total household income for the tax year, household size and ages of household members.

### Complete the Affordability Worksheet

1. **Open Shop and Compare** on Covered California.
2. **Enter your household information.** "While adding household members, select Needs Coverage for each person. For this worksheet only, do not select pregnant, blind or disabled. These checkboxes change your plan results in a way that won't work for this worksheet."
3. **Find the lowest-cost Bronze plan.** "Sort results by Lowest premium and filter by Bronze only. Find the first Bronze plan listed and select Plan Details. Scroll to the 'Monthly Premium' section and find 'Monthly premium after savings.' Enter this amount on Line 1 of the worksheet."
4. **Find the second-lowest-cost Silver plan.** "Sort results by Lowest premium and filter by Silver only. Find the second Silver plan listed and select Plan Details. Scroll to the 'Monthly Premium' section and find 'Monthly premium before savings.' Enter this amount on Line 10 of the worksheet."

### After You Complete the Form

"Form FTB 3853 will show whether you owe a penalty for any months you or your household members were without qualifying health insurance. If you owe a penalty, it will be included in your total California state tax due when you file. If you claimed a valid exemption, the form confirms your exemption for the year."
