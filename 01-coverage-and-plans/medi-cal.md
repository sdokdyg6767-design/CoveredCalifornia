## Summary

Medi-Cal is California's Medicaid program, giving free or low-cost health insurance to Californians with limited incomes. Medi-Cal and Covered California share the same application, so one submission determines eligibility for both, and the program you qualify for depends on income. Unlike Covered California, Medi-Cal can be applied for at any time of year. Most adults qualify with household income up to 138% of the federal poverty level, while children qualify up to 266% of the FPL.

## Key Facts

- "Medi-Cal is California's Medicaid program. It gives free or low-cost health insurance to Californians with limited incomes."
- "Medi-Cal and Covered California share the same application."
- Eligibility for most adults: "household income up to **138% of the federal poverty level (FPL)**."
- Eligibility for children: "Children qualify with household income up to **266% of the FPL**."
- "Medi-Cal is run by the California Department of Health Care Services (DHCS), not Covered California."
- "Medi-Cal covers health, dental and vision care for you and your children, often at little or no cost."
- "When You Can Apply": Medi-Cal — "Any time of year"; Covered California — "During open enrollment — or special enrollment if you experience a major life change."
- "Medi-Cal will choose a health plan for you if you don't choose one within **30 days**."
- Streamlined application programs: CalFresh; SSI/SSP; CalWORKs (AFDC); Refugee Assistance; Foster Care or Adoption Assistance Program.
- "You must report any household changes within **10 days** to your local county social services office."
- Call Covered California at (800) 300-1506.

## Details

### What Is Medi-Cal?

"Medi-Cal covers health, dental and vision care for you and your children, often at little or no cost." Both Medi-Cal and Covered California "cover the same essential health benefits."

### Medi-Cal vs. Covered California

| Standard table | Medi-Cal | Covered California |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What It Is | A state program that gives free or low-cost coverage to people with limited income | Private health insurance marketplace where people can get income-based financial help to reduce monthly premiums |
| Who Is Eligible | Californians with incomes at or below 138% of the federal poverty level | Californians who need insurance and aren't eligible for Medi-Cal |
| When You Can Apply | Any time of year | During open enrollment — or special enrollment if you experience a major life change |

### How to Apply

"Start your application online, and we'll automatically check to see if you are eligible for Medi-Cal or Covered California." You can also:

- Find free local help to complete your application.
- Call or visit a Medi-Cal office in your county.
- Call Covered California at (800) 300-1506.

### Medi-Cal Eligibility

"Medi-Cal eligibility can depend on your income, household size, age, pregnancy, disability and other factors. Some people qualify through programs they already use."

**Income programs you already use.** "People who are enrolled in certain benefit programs can have their application for Medi-Cal streamlined." Those programs include:

- CalFresh
- SSI/SSP
- CalWORKs (AFDC)
- Refugee Assistance
- Foster Care or Adoption Assistance Program

**Other factors.** Some people may qualify for Medi-Cal based on factors like:

- 65 or older
- Blind
- Disabled
- Under 21
- Pregnant
- Residents in skilled nursing or intermediate care homes
- People with refugee status for a limited time
- Parents or caretakers of disadvantaged children under 21
- People who were diagnosed with breast or cervical cancer

**Different limits produce mixed-program families.** "Medi-Cal uses your household's income, but the income limit is different for each person depending on their age or situation." Worked example: "in a family at 250% of the FPL, the children are under the limit for kids, but the parents are over the limit for adults. The children would usually qualify for Medi-Cal, while the parents would get a Covered California plan. This is called a mixed-program family."

### Using Medi-Cal Insurance

"If you qualify for Medi-Cal, you'll receive a packet in the mail that shows the health plans you can choose from. Medi-Cal will choose a health plan for you if you don't choose one within 30 days." Plan options depend on the county where you live; see the Medi-Cal Managed Care Health Plan Directory.

"If anyone in your family declines Medi-Cal, they can enroll in a Covered California plan without financial help — this means paying the full cost of the plan."

### Medi-Cal for Your Situation

Sub-pages cover children, pregnancy, and mixed-program families. "Medi-Cal covers children, pregnancy and mixed-program families differently."

### Get Help With Medi-Cal

"Still applying or have questions? Covered California can help. Already have Medi-Cal? Your county handles your insurance and any questions about it."

### Common Questions

- **Can I decline Medi-Cal?** "Yes. If anyone in your family declines Medi-Cal, they can enroll in a Covered California plan without financial help — this means paying the full cost of the plan."
- **I applied for Medi-Cal but haven't received any information from the county. What should I do?** "Medi-Cal is processing your application as quickly as possible, and you do not need to apply again. Sometimes, processing can take longer than expected due to a large number of people applying. Proof of certain information, like income, can also lead to longer processing times. If you need immediate help or have specific questions, you should contact your county human services agency for in-person assistance."
- **What if my income or situation changes?** "You must report any household changes within 10 days to your local county social services office. This includes any new household members, like children, and any changes to your income."
