# Medi-Cal for Mixed Program Families

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/coverage-basics/medi-cal/mixed-program-families
- **Covered California section:** Coverage Basics / Medi-Cal
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

A mixed-program family is one in which different family members qualify for different health insurance programs — some get Medi-Cal while others get a Covered California plan, sometimes with financial help. Covered California checks each person's eligibility for both programs during a single application, because eligibility depends on household details such as size, income and ages. The page explains what each program member's coverage looks like, the cost consequence of declining Medi-Cal, and the four steps to follow after applying.

## Key Facts

- "A mixed-program family is a family where different family members qualify for different health insurance programs. This is common, and it does not mean there is anything wrong with your application."
- "When you apply, we check each person's eligibility for both Covered California and Medi-Cal."
- "This happens because eligibility is based on household details like size, income and ages."
- "If you live in San Francisco, San Mateo or Santa Clara County, your child may instead qualify for the California Children's Health Insurance Program (CCHIP). CCHIP follows rules that are similar to Medi-Cal's."
- "If you decline Medi-Cal, they can enroll in a Covered California plan without financial help — this means paying the full cost of the plan."
- "If your insurance has a premium, you must pay it before you can start using your plan."
- "If your eligibility results don't look right, get help before you decline insurance."

## Details

### What Coverage May Look Like for Your Family

| If a family member qualifies for… | Cost and Coverage | What to Do Next |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A Covered California plan | They can choose a health plan and may qualify for financial help that lowers monthly premiums and out-of-pocket costs. | Compare plans, total costs and doctor networks before enrolling. |
| Medi-Cal | They can get low-cost or no-cost health insurance through Medi-Cal. | Watch for next steps or notices from your county or Medi-Cal. |

### If You Decline Medi-Cal

"If anyone in your family declines Medi-Cal, they can enroll in a Covered California plan without financial help — this means paying the full cost of the plan."

### Can My Family See the Same Doctors?

"You and your family members may be able to see the same doctors, but it depends on the health plans available where you live. Before choosing a plan, check each plan's provider network. You can contact the health insurance company to confirm which doctors are in network for each plan before you enroll."

### What Happens After You Apply

1. **Review each person's eligibility result.** "Read each one carefully before choosing or declining insurance."
2. **Choose your Covered California plan.** "Family members who qualify for a plan through Covered California can choose one online. Compare premiums, out-of-pocket costs and doctor networks."
3. **Enroll in your Medi-Cal plan.** "Your local county office or Medi-Cal will send next steps, insurance details and health plan options to people who qualify for Medi-Cal."
4. **Set up your health plan.** "Look for a welcome letter, member card and plan details in the mail. If your insurance has a premium, you must pay it before you can start using your plan."
