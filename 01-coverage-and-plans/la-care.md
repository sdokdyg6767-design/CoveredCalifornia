# LA Care

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/la-care
- **Covered California section:** Carrier
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

The LA Care carrier page on Covered California is a short marketing and enrollment page built from the site's standard carrier template. It carries the shared tagline, the benefits included in every Covered California plan, switching guidance for existing members, an application document checklist, and three enrollment channels. The page does not publish plan counts, premium figures, metal-tier availability, or a county service-region list.

## Key Facts

- Carrier name as published on the page: "LA Care."
- Tagline on the page: "Trusted Plans. Exclusive Savings."
- Page statement: "Enrolling in an LA Care plan through Covered California could help you save on your health coverage costs."
- Benefits listed as "Included in every plan": Free Annual Wellness Exam; Pre-existing Condition Coverage; Mental Health Care; Prescription Drug Coverage; ER and Hospitalization Coverage; Maternity and Newborn Care; Free Cancer Screenings; Option to Add Dental and Vision.
- Switching guidance: "Cancel your current plan after enrolling through Covered California."
- "In most cases, your accumulated deductible and out-of-pocket maximum will carry over, but you should confirm with Health-Net before you switch." As printed on the page, even though the card is headed "If you already have a LA Care Plan."
- What you'll need to apply: employer and income information; Social Security Numbers for applicants who have them; federal tax information for applicants who file taxes; immigration documents for non-citizens.
- Enrollment: apply online in about 30 minutes, apply with a certified enroller, or apply by phone; "Each option is free and confidential."
- Covered California Service Center: (800) 300-1506; TTY (888) 889-4500. Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 6 p.m.; closed weekends.

## Details

### Included in Every Plan

- Free Annual Wellness Exam
- Pre-existing Condition Coverage
- Mental Health Care
- Prescription Drug Coverage
- ER and Hospitalization Coverage
- Maternity and Newborn Care
- Free Cancer Screenings
- Option to Add Dental and Vision

### Things to Consider

"If you already have a LA Care Plan: Cancel your current plan after enrolling through Covered California. In most cases, your accumulated deductible and out-of-pocket maximum will carry over, but you should confirm with Health-Net before you switch. It helps to have your current plan details ready when shopping for a similar plan here."

> **Site note:** The card is headed "If you already have a LA Care Plan" but the carryover sentence directs the reader to confirm with **Health-Net**, a different carrier. Reproduced as printed; this appears to be an upstream copy-paste error on Covered California.

### What You'll Need to Apply

- Employer and income information for applicants who earn money.
- Social Security Numbers for applicants who have them.
- Federal tax information for applicants who file taxes.
- Immigration documents for non-citizens.

### How to Apply

- **Apply Online** — "The fastest way to enroll apply in about 30 minutes and save your progress anytime."
- **Apply with a Certified Enroller** — "Find free in-person help from a certified enroller or agent near you, available in multiple languages."
- **Apply by Phone** — "Speak with a Covered California representative by phone, available in multiple languages."

### Data Availability Note

This page does not publish LA Care plan counts, premiums, metal-tier availability, or county service regions. Per the site's Dental Insurance page, children's dental for L.A. Care members is provided through Liberty Dental Plan (DHMO), contact (800) 700-5243.
