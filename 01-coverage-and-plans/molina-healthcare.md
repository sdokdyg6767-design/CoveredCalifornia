## Summary

The Molina Healthcare carrier page is the only individual marketplace carrier page that carries carrier-specific news: a service-region withdrawal notice stating that Molina will not offer plans in northeast Los Angeles County or Orange County in 2027. The rest of the page uses the standard Covered California carrier template — the shared tagline, benefits included in every plan, switching guidance, an application checklist, and three enrollment channels.

## Details

### Molina Healthcare Is Leaving Some Regions

"Molina Healthcare won't offer plans northeast Los Angeles County or Orange County in 2027. Use Shop and Compare to see what's available and estimate your costs." The page provides a "See Your Options" link in place of an Apply link within this notice block.

### Included in Every Plan

- Free Annual Wellness Exam
- Pre-existing Condition Coverage
- Mental Health Care
- Prescription Drug Coverage
- ER and Hospitalization Coverage
- Maternity and Newborn Care
- Free Cancer Screenings
- Option to Add Dental and Vision

### Things to Consider

"If you already have a Molina Healthcare Plan: Cancel your current plan after enrolling through Covered California. In most cases, your accumulated deductible and out-of-pocket maximum will carry over, but you should confirm with Molina Healthcare before you switch. It helps to have your current plan details ready when shopping for a similar plan here."

### What You'll Need to Apply

- Employer and income information for applicants who earn money.
- Social Security Numbers for applicants who have them.
- Federal tax information for applicants who file taxes.
- Immigration documents for non-citizens.

### How to Apply

- **Apply Online** — "The fastest way to enroll apply in about 30 minutes and save your progress anytime."
- **Apply with a Certified Enroller** — "Find free in-person help from a certified enroller or agent near you, available in multiple languages."
- **Apply by Phone** — "Speak with a Covered California representative by phone, available in multiple languages."

### Data Availability Note

Beyond the 2027 regional withdrawal notice, this page does not publish plan counts, premiums, metal-tier availability, or a full service-region list. Per the site's Dental Insurance page, children's dental for Molina members is provided through DentaQuest (DHMO), contact (855) 425-4164.
