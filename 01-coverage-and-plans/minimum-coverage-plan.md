# Minimum Coverage Plan

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/coverage-basics/coverage-levels/minimum-coverage-plan
- **Covered California section:** Coverage Basics / Plan Tiers
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

A minimum coverage plan, also called catastrophic coverage, is a distinct plan type with lower monthly premiums that protects against very high medical bills in a serious emergency. For most care the member pays the full cost until the out-of-pocket maximum is reached, after which the plan pays 100 percent of covered in-network services. It is only available to people under 30 or to those 30 and older with an approved hardship or affordability exemption, and it never qualifies for financial help regardless of income.

## Key Facts

- "The minimum coverage plan, also called catastrophic coverage, offers lower monthly premium payments."
- "The minimum coverage plan usually has lower monthly premiums than Bronze, Silver, Gold or Platinum plans."
- "For most forms of care, you pay the full cost until you reach your out-of-pocket maximum. After that, the plan covers 100 percent of covered in-network services."
- "The minimum coverage plan doesn't qualify for financial help, regardless of your income."
- Eligibility route 1: "You are under 30." The plan can be bought through Covered California, and "(Once you age out, you'll be automatically moved to a Bronze plan.)"
- Eligibility route 2: "You are 30 or older and have an approved exemption." You must apply for and receive an approved affordability hardship or general hardship exemption through Covered California, which issues an **Exemption Certificate Number (ECN)**. You then buy the plan "directly from a health insurance company, not through Covered California."
- Covered benefits: free preventive care; "Three doctor (primary and other practitioner) or urgent care visits per plan year"; and 100 percent payment for in-network services after the out-of-pocket limit.
- The three primary/urgent care visits "including outpatient mental health and substance use visits, are covered with no out-of-pocket costs regardless of your deductible."

## Details

### What Is a Minimum Coverage Plan?

"The minimum coverage plan is not the same as the four standard coverage levels. It has a different cost structure and different eligibility rules." It is "designed to protect you from very high medical bills in a serious emergency."

**Financial help is not available.** "If you think you may qualify for financial help, learn more about your financial help options before choosing this plan type."

### Who Can Get a Minimum Coverage Plan?

| Condition | How to enroll |
| --- | --- |
| You are under 30 | Buy through Covered California. Once you age out you'll be automatically moved to a Bronze plan. |
| You are 30 or older and have an approved exemption | Apply for an affordability hardship or general hardship exemption through Covered California; once approved you receive an Exemption Certificate Number (ECN), then buy the plan directly from the health insurance company rather than through Covered California. |

"Under 30? Compare your plan options first. A Bronze plan may have a similar monthly premium to the minimum coverage plan and offer financial help if you're eligible."

### What This Plan Covers

The minimum coverage plan includes:

- **Free preventive care.** "Routine screenings, vaccines and wellness visits at no cost to you."
- **Three doctor (primary and other practitioner) or urgent care visits per plan year.** "These visits, including outpatient mental health and substance use visits, are covered with no out-of-pocket costs regardless of your deductible."
- **Protection from very high costs.** "Once you reach your plan's out-of-pocket limit, the plan pays 100 percent for all in-network services."

"For most other services, you pay the full cost until you reach the out-of-pocket limit."

### Minimum Coverage Plan vs. Bronze Plan

| Standard table | Minimum Coverage Plan | Bronze Plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who can buy it? | Usually people under 30, or people 30 or older with an approved hardship or affordability exemption | People of any age who are eligible for Covered California |
| Monthly premium | Usually lower, but not always the lowest total cost | Often similar to minimum coverage plans |
| Financial help available | No | Yes, if you qualify |
| Deductible and costs when you use care | Usually higher out-of-pocket costs before the plan pays for most care | May have a lower deductible and different cost-sharing |
| Exemption required | Yes, if you are 30 or older | No exemption needed |

### How to Decide if This Plan Is Right for You

1. **Check if you're eligible.** If you're under 30, you may be able to buy a minimum coverage plan through Covered California. If you are 30 or older, check whether you need an approved affordability hardship or general hardship exemption.
2. **Compare with Bronze plans.** A Bronze plan may have a similar monthly premium, a lower deductible or financial help if you qualify.
3. **Choose the option that fits your situation.** If you are under 30, shop and compare plans through Covered California. If you are 30 or older and have an approved ECN, contact the health insurance company directly.
