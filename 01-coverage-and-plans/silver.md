# Silver Plans

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/coverage-basics/coverage-levels/silver
- **Covered California section:** Coverage Basics / Plan Tiers
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

Silver is the middle coverage level among Covered California's plans: the monthly premium and the cost of care both land between Bronze and Gold/Platinum. A Silver plan covers about 70 percent of medical costs and the member pays up to 30 percent, but Silver is the only level with enhanced variants — Silver 73, Silver 87 and Silver 94 — that lower cost sharing and can cover up to 94 percent depending on household income and size. The page publishes full out-of-pocket maximum, deductible, and per-service copay tables for all four Silver variants.

## Key Facts

- "A Silver plan covers about 70 percent of your medical costs, and you pay up to 30 percent."
- Enhanced Silver plans "can cover even more — up to 94 percent" depending on income and household size.
- Two kinds of help: premium help ("Most people qualify for Silver plans, including Silver 70, that lower what you pay each month") and cost-sharing help ("Included only with Enhanced Silver plans (73, 87 and 94)").
- "Anyone can choose a Silver 70 plan." Assistance rises with lower income: "some with Silver 73, more with 87, most with 94."
- Out-of-pocket maximum individual: $9,800 (Silver 70), $8,100 (Silver 73), $3,350 (Silver 87), $1,400 (Silver 94).
- Out-of-pocket maximum family: $19,600 (Silver 70), $16,200 (Silver 73), $6,700 (Silver 87), $2,800 (Silver 94).
- Medical deductible individual: $5,200 (Silver 70), $5,200 (Silver 73), $1,400 (Silver 87), None (Silver 94).
- Medical deductible family: $10,400 (Silver 70), $10,400 (Silver 73), $2,800 (Silver 87), None (Silver 94).
- Pharmacy deductible individual: $50 for Silver 70, 73 and 87; None for Silver 94. Family: $100 for 70, 73 and 87; None for 94.
- Specialty drugs: Silver 70 and 73 — 20% up to $250; Silver 87 — 15% up to $150; Silver 94 — 10% up to $150.
- The page links to an "income limits chart (PDF)" and a "household income limits (PDF)" so consumers can estimate eligibility before applying.

## Details

### Is Silver Right for You?

"Silver is the middle coverage level among Covered California's plans. Your monthly premium and what you pay for care both land in the middle — higher than Bronze, lower than Gold or Platinum."

A Silver plan may be a good fit if you:

- Visit the doctor a few times a year and sometimes see a specialist.
- Need prescriptions occasionally.
- Want a balance — a mid-range cost for your monthly premium, and when you get care.

You may want to consider other coverage levels if you:

- Rarely use health care.
- Want the lowest possible monthly premium.
- Use your plan often and want the lowest costs for care, even with a higher premium.

### Plan Types: HMO, PPO and EPO

| Plan type | Full name | Character | Key rules | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| HMO | Health maintenance organization | Lower cost, more structure | Only covers in-network care, except for emergencies; need a referral to see a specialist; usually the lowest monthly premium | Lower costs, with a primary doctor managing your care |
| PPO | Preferred provider organization | More choice, higher cost | See doctors in or out of the network; no referral needed to see a specialist; higher monthly premium than HMO or EPO | Flexibility to choose your own doctors and hospitals |
| EPO | Exclusive provider organization | Part HMO, part PPO | Only covers in-network care, except for emergencies; no referral needed to see a specialist; costs usually land between HMO and PPO | Lower costs without needing referrals |

### You Might Pay Even Less for Silver

"If your income qualifies, an Enhanced Silver plan lowers what you pay out of pocket for care — sometimes by a lot."

### Common Questions

- **How do I get an Enhanced Silver plan, and which level?** "There's nothing extra to do. Silver 70 is open to everyone — the only part that depends on your income is the Enhanced level. When you apply, we check your household income limits (PDF) and if you qualify, we'll tell you which Enhanced Silver level (73, 87, or 94) you could qualify for. It's up to you whether to take it."
- **If I qualify for Enhanced Silver, do I have to choose it?** "No — you can pick any plan. But the extra savings on care costs only come with Enhanced Silver plans. If you choose Bronze, Gold or Platinum instead, you keep any financial help for your monthly premium that you qualify for, but give up the extra cost savings offered with an Enhanced Silver plan."
- **What happens to my plan if my income changes during the year?** "Report the change in your Covered California account. Your premium help — and your Enhanced Silver level, if you qualify — depend on your income, so keeping it up to date keeps your costs accurate. It also matters at tax time: Your premium help is based on the income you estimated, so updating your account helps you avoid owing money back."
