# Medi-Cal for Children

- **Source URL:** https://www.coveredca.com/coverage-basics/medi-cal/children
- **Covered California section:** Coverage Basics / Medi-Cal
- **Retrieved:** 2026-10-13

## Summary

Children in California may qualify for either Medi-Cal for Children or the County Children's Health Initiative Program (CCHIP), and which one applies depends on age, household income, county of residence, and immigration or citizenship status. Medi-Cal for Children covers children under 19 up to 266% of the federal poverty level in all California counties, while CCHIP serves San Francisco, San Mateo and Santa Clara counties for children over 266% FPL up to 322% FPL. Applying through Covered California is free and one application determines the program for each family member.

## Key Facts

- "Many children in California qualify for free or low-cost health insurance through Medi-Cal. In certain counties, they could be eligible for CCHIP."
- Medi-Cal for Children income limit: "Up to **266% federal poverty level (FPL)**." CCHIP income limit: "Over **266% FPL to 322% FPL**."
- Medi-Cal for Children is available in "All California counties." CCHIP is available in "San Francisco, San Mateo and Santa Clara counties only."
- Medi-Cal for Children cost: "For eligible children, health insurance is free or low-cost."
- CCHIP cost: "No monthly premium for eligible children; some copays may apply depending on income."
- Immigration status: Medi-Cal for Children — "Children under 19 may qualify, no matter their immigration status." CCHIP — "Child must be a U.S. citizen, U.S. national or lawfully present."
- "Applying through Covered California is free. One application checks what each family member qualifies for, including Medi-Cal and CCHIP for children."
- "If you decline insurance for your child, they can't get financial help for a plan through Covered California. This means they would need to be enrolled in a full-cost plan."
- Newborns: "If your baby was born while you were receiving Medi-Cal, your baby may be automatically enrolled in Medi-Cal and may not need a separate application. At your baby's first birthday, the county will complete a Medi-Cal redetermination."
- Medi-Cal for Children covers "check-ups and doctor visits, screenings and vaccinations, emergency care, dental and vision care, prescriptions, mental health services and more."

## Details

### Insurance Programs for Children

Children may qualify for one of two programs: Medi-Cal for Children or the County Children's Health Initiative Program (CCHIP).

"When you apply through Covered California, your child may qualify for a different program than you do."

Which program your child gets depends on:

- your child's age
- your household income
- where you live
- your child's immigration or citizenship status

### Medi-Cal for Children vs. CCHIP — Covered Benefits for Children

### What Medi-Cal for Children Covers

"Medi-Cal for Children covers many kinds of care. This includes check-ups and doctor visits, screenings and vaccinations, emergency care, dental and vision care, prescriptions, mental health services and more."

### How to Apply

1. **Gather basic information.** "Have your household size, household income and each family member's information ready before you start."
2. **Check your eligibility.** Use the Shop and Compare Tool to see what programs your family may qualify for, or start your application.
3. **Review your results.** "The application will show the program each family member qualifies for, including any family members who qualify for Covered California, Medi-Cal or CCHIP."
4. **Follow the next steps.** "For Medi-Cal or CCHIP, their office will contact you with enrollment information and any next steps. For Covered California, you will be able to choose a plan online and pay your first bill."

"Need help or have questions? You can get free help from a certified enroller."

### Special Situations

- **Newborn baby:** "If your baby was born while you were receiving Medi-Cal, your baby may be automatically enrolled in Medi-Cal and may not need a separate application. At your baby's first birthday, the county will complete a Medi-Cal redetermination. Contact your local county office to make sure your baby's insurance is in place."
- **Families with mixed program eligibility:** "This is common and called a mixed-program family. A parent may qualify for a Covered California health insurance plan with financial help, while their child qualifies for Medi-Cal or CCHIP instead."
